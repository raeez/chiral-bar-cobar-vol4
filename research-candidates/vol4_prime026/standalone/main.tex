\begin{abstract}
The Mellin transform sends multiplication by minus the logarithm to
spectral differentiation. It sends the normalized dilation generator
to spectral multiplication. An explicit embedding of integer
occupation states into distributions realizes the first action, but
its composition with zero residues does not intertwine occupation
energy with zero ordinates. The second action induces triangular
operators on full residue jets. Positive evaluation norms give
Hilbert spaces with these prescribed spectral data, while evaluation
from the original continuous Hilbert space is not closable. The
normalizations, multiple-zero terms, and conditions for symmetry are
computed explicitly.
\end{abstract}
