\chapter*{Scope and mathematical bridge}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Scope and mathematical bridge}

A local collision law and an automorphic character live on different
carriers. The governing problem is to construct typed passages between them:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{collision}
&\longrightarrow \text{ordered fusion}
 \longrightarrow \text{derived centre}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{Hall extension}
 \longrightarrow \text{quantum double}
 \longrightarrow \text{character}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Each arrow denotes a separate construction, with its source, target, grading,
and completion fixed before composition.

Holomorphic collision on a smooth curve gives a factorization coefficient.
An oriented transverse interval gives an internal $\Eone$ multiplication and
hence ordered fusion. In the fixed presentable, stable, closed
symmetric-monoidal coefficient category, whose tensor product preserves
geometric realizations separately, compatible bulk actions on $A$ factor through
\[
Z_{\ord}(A)=\RHom_{A\tensor A^{\op}}(A,A).
\]
A primitive Lie algebra enters through a separate Chevalley or Hall
comparison. Under the stated base-change, orientation, and convergence
hypotheses, extension correspondences define the associative Hall product.
A coproduct, Cartan extension, negative half, and nondegenerate continuous
Hopf pairing define the completed Drinfeld double.

Assume that each root space of the Igusa root algebra is finite-dimensional.
Fix a proper integral height on the positive root cone, so each bounded-height
region contains finitely many roots. In the separated inverse limit of the
finite-height quotients, the coefficientwise Euler supercharacter is well
defined. Its Weyl-shifted character is the Borcherds product. This calculation
records signed root multiplicities. The bracket, parity decomposition, Hall
correspondence, and orientation line remain separate data.

The arithmetic shadow-$L$ theory requires an arithmetic coefficient object
and a comparison from its Hochschild trace class to the completed character.
The Kummer--Bernoulli theory also requires compatible integral and $p$-adic
realization and specialization maps. A Hilbert--P\'olya statement requires a
Hilbert-space realization, a specified self-adjoint operator, and a
determinant comparison with the completed $L$-function. These arithmetic
constructions lie outside the scope of this monograph. Any arithmetic
consequence of the displayed chain is conditional on those additional data.
