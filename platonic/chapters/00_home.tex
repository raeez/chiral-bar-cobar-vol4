\chapter*{This repository and the programme}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{This repository and the programme}

This monograph is the integrated treatment shared by the mathematics
repositories: one canonical spine, byte-identical wherever it is carried,
together with the audit and obstruction chapters merged in from the
ordered-chiral reconstruction.

The programme has three volumes. \textbf{Volume I} is ordered chiral
geometry, in \texttt{chiral-bar-cobar}. \textbf{Volume II} is the mixed
holomorphic--topological theory, in \texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2}.
\textbf{Volume III} is Calabi--Yau quantum groups, in
\texttt{calabi-yau-quantum-groups}. The universal chiral BV and Einstein
completion accompanies them without a repository of its own.

This repository is \emph{not} one of the three volumes. Its own subject is
arithmetic: shadow $L$-functions, Kummer and Bernoulli structure, and the
Hilbert--P\'olya reading of the chiral spectrum. \textbf{None of that
subject appears in the spine.} The integrated monograph is built here so
that a plain build produces the current programme text, but the arithmetic
capstone of this repository has not been carried into it, and no claim is
made that the spine supersedes it. Porting or retracting that material is
an open judgment.
