\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

The exact Jacobi coefficients of $\phi_{0,1}$ determine the monic weight-five Borcherds product $\Dmon$ and its Gritsenko--Nikulin root superalgebra.  The problem is to place this denominator, its algebraic realizations, and the primitive reduced $K3\times E$ series on one carrier.  That carrier is the universal Igusa completion $\mathfrak K_\Delta$, formed through proper-height limits.

Constructed morphisms connect the root presentation to its proper-height Chevalley coalgebra and local--wrapped mixed holomorphic--topological current theory.  They also connect it to the finite-window Hall and three-Calabi--Yau models, Hong quantum supergroup, and root-diagonal Fredholm family.  Common root grading and the Poincar\'e--Birkhoff--Witt (PBW) filtration make these constructed morphisms compatible.

On the absolute-convergence chamber, $K_Z$ is trace class, and its oriented skew doubling $\mathbb A_Z$ satisfies
\[
 e^{-\rho}\operatorname{sPf}_F(\mathbb A_Z)=\DenIg(Z)=\Dmon(2Z).
\]
This is the Weyl-shifted denominator identity.  Half-coordinate pullback sends $\DenIg$ to $\Dmon$, whose square is $\ChiTen$.  The Oberdieck--Pixton theorem, with the reduced-incidence minus sign, identifies the resulting inverse square as $\mathcal Z^{\red}_{K3\times E}=-\ChiTen^{-1}$.

For a presentation-preserving protected realization $\mathfrak T$, the universal property gives a unique primitive Lie morphism $\nIg\to\Prim(\mathfrak T)$.  PBW promotes an assumed equivalence of this primitive map to equivalences of the completed enveloping and Fock objects.  Full identification also requires the selected Hall, Ginzburg, current, quantum-double, and determinant comparison maps to be equivalences.


\chapter*{The theorem spine}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{The theorem spine}
The work is organized by the order in which geometry creates algebra:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{collision}&\longrightarrow\text{ordered fusion}\longrightarrow\text{centre}\longrightarrow\text{Hall extension}\\
&\longrightarrow\text{quantum double}\longrightarrow\text{character}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Each arrow is constructed in a fixed category.  Each inverse limit uses a proper positive filtration.  Each physical interpretation is the image of a displayed open--closed, Hall, current, trace, or determinant morphism.  The central examples are calculated in generators and relations before their abstract generalization.

\chapter*{Notation and normalization}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Notation and normalization}
The ground field has characteristic zero.  Stable categories are presentable and idempotent-complete.  Tensor products preserve colimits separately.  Completions are separated inverse limits of finite weight or proper-height quotients.  The Igusa normalization is
\[
 \Dtheta=64\Dmon,\qquad \ChiTen=\Dmon^2,
\]
with $\Dmon$ monic.  Physical charge is additive; Gram degree is carried through its polarization cocycle.  Bar, factorization, and Hopf coproducts retain distinct notation and distinct geometric meanings.
