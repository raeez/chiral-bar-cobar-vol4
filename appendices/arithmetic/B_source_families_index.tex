\section{Obstructions Across Source Families}
\label{v4-arith:app:B:falsifications}

The following comparisons concern specific algebraic and geometric
constructions. Positive models and obstructions retain their own
carriers, coefficient topologies, and hypotheses:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The Hochschild trace class \(\mathcal V^{\mathrm{prim}}\)
does not itself specify a vertex algebra. Local Heisenberg fields
exist, while the prescribed infinite Sugawara sum fails on its
algebraic vacuum. Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:polyakov} also records the
prime-zeta natural boundary, separately from the rank-residue
calculation and the remaining conformal comparison.
\item Abstract common field extensions of all \(\mathbb Q_p\)
exist, as in Observation~\ref{v4-arith:obs:no-single-field}.
Such a field does not supply continuous local embeddings or an
adelic topology. An arithmetic restricted tensor still needs its
coefficient and representation maps.
\item The \(p\)-adically completed tensor
\(\mathbb Z_p\widehat\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Z_p\)
is \(\mathbb Z_p\). The ordinary tensor has a nonzero multiplication
kernel, as proved in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:drinfeld-kapranov}.
These are different completions of the diagonal problem.
Neither calculation constructs the required arithmetic collision
operations.
\item \(T_{n}\) does not lift Adams operations to \(GL_{2}\) Hecke
operators. The explicit Kazhdan--Beilinson computation gives
\(T_{p}^{2}(a^{(p)}_{-1}|0\rangle)-p\cdot a^{(p)}_{-1}|0\rangle\neq T_{p^{2}}(a^{(p)}_{-1}|0\rangle)\).
\item The index-shift Adams maps fail the Frobenius congruence on
 the polynomial Newton-generator lattice, whereas they satisfy it on
 the integral Hall lattice of symmetric functions. The divisor
 substitutions \(T_p\) still fail on that Hall lattice.
 These are distinct integral carriers and operations, as proved in
 Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:gelfand-borger}. Neither lattice calculation
 supplies a \(\Lambda\)-structure on the arithmetic trace class
 without a compatible arithmetic comparison.
\item Arithmetic Yangian RTT relations have no global spectral
parameter. The Yang-Baxter spectral parameter
$u-v$ has no analogue across distinct primes; there is no meromorphic
$1$-form on $\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$.
\item A 4D class-\(\mathcal{S}\) gauge boundary does not transplant to
\(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\). The Hitchin base
\(\bigoplus_i H^0(\Sigma,K_\Sigma^{d_i})\) uses a holomorphic
canonical bundle on a complex curve. The Arakelov dualizing line on
\(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb Z}\) is not such an object.
\item The curve-theoretic category of \(\mathcal{D}\)-modules on
\(\mathrm{Bun}_{GL_{2}}\) does not arise from
\(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\) itself.
The moduli stack $\mathrm{Bun}_{GL_{2}}(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z})$
is \(BGL_{2}(\mathbb{Z})\): one coarse point with arithmetic
stabilizer. This stack has representation theory of the stabilizer,
not the geometric Hecke correspondence of the curve case.
\item The integral \(E_{2}\)-chiral-centre enhancement is not obtained
by replacing the chain operad \(C_{\bullet}(E_{2};\mathbb Z)\) with
\(\mathrm{Ger}_{\mathbb Z}\). Dyer--Lashof and Kaledin-type
\(p\)-curvature classes obstruct that strictification, not the
existence of an integral \(E_{2}\)-algebra.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Common Structural Constraints}
\label{v4-arith:app:B:converged-structures}

The proposed arithmetic realization has the following structural
requirements:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The arithmetic curve is Arakelov-compactified
\(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\), not \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\)
(across source families).
\item The finite-place incidence poset does not supply formal
collision coordinates. Formal Heisenberg fields can nevertheless
be constructed over \(\mathbb Q_p\), as in
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:drinfeld-kapranov}. Comparing them with
arithmetic coefficient objects requires actual operation maps.
\item The Steinberg relation \(\{p,1-p\}=0\) holds in Milnor
\(K\)-theory. Comparing its residue complex with chiral collision
operations requires a common carrier and maps preserving the
operations, as in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:arakelov-bar}.
\item The Hecke structure is carried by the Iwahori-equivariant
affine Hecke category, not the spherical category
(Bezrukavnikov+Bernstein, Kazhdan+Beilinson).
\item The Iwahori Hecke Hochschild trace is the local
Bezrukavnikov--Bernstein model. Its global identification with
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) is the arithmetic BZN input F3. Kim
arithmetic Chern--Simons (Kim arXiv:1510.05818) provides the classical
boundary torsor construction, and the adelic factorization algebra
lives on \(\mathbb{C}_{\infty}\) (Costello+Gwilliam).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Compatibility and the Arithmetic Construction Problem}
\label{v4-arith:app:B:convergence-theorem}

\begin{remark}[compatibility of specified identifications]
\label{v4-arith:app:B:thm:obstruction-theoretic-convergence}
Suppose the proposed arithmetic presentations are constructed in a
specified common category. Suppose each has a specified isomorphism
\(f_i:X_i\to V\) preserving the stated operations and arithmetic data.
Then \(f_j^{-1}f_i:X_i\to X_j\) identify these presentations and satisfy
\((f_k^{-1}f_j)(f_j^{-1}f_i)=f_k^{-1}f_i\).
Transporting a six-window conclusion to \(V\) requires a proved
source statement and comparison maps preserving its hypotheses and
structures. Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:thm:six-window-placement}
does not supply that source statement.
\par
The composite of two structure-preserving isomorphisms preserves
those structures. The displayed identity follows by cancelling
\(f_jf_j^{-1}\). Any property transported to \(V\) must be among
the properties preserved by the specified maps.
\end{remark}

\begin{question}[arithmetic trace comparisons]
\label{v4-arith:app:B:comparison-construction}
Construct the global arithmetic Iwahori Hecke category, its Hochschild
trace complex, and the proposed arithmetic boundary and factorization
objects. Specify their coefficient rings, gradings, tensor products and
completions. Construct the functors or chain maps relating their
specified common carriers, with compatibility for central and Hecke
actions, local gluing and the claimed trace pairings.
The local-to-global categorical and spectral comparison is F1, the
finite-place enhancement is F2, and the arithmetic trace comparison is F3
in Conjectures~\ref{v4-arith:of:F1}, \ref{v4-arith:of:F2}, and
\ref{v4-arith:of:F3}. Their existence and mutual compatibility are the
missing inputs to a common arithmetic realization.
\end{question}

Several starting constructions have different, explicit carriers.
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:gelfand-borger} constructs the polynomial divisor
and Adams operations and the integral Hall lattice.
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:connes-soibelman} constructs gauge Hochschild
classes on its specified Bost--Connes algebras and analyzes their domains.
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion} computes finite-algebra extension
products and regular Hochschild homology. None of these calculations
by itself supplies the arithmetic functors in the preceding question.

\subsection{Obstructions}
\label{v4-arith:app:B:genus-g:falsifications}

The six source families jointly obstruct six readings of the initial
ansatz (\Cref{v4-arith:ag:falsified} indexes the full list):
the category-error reading of ``almost complex structure on an
arithmetic scheme'' (no real tangent bundle on \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\);
Newlander--Nirenberg automatic on real \(2\)-manifolds); ``arithmetic
Euler characteristic $=1-g$'' (Gillet--Soul\'e analytic torsion
term); K\"unneth factorisation in arithmetic Chow (Bost--K\"unnemann
Bott--Chern term); finite genus of $\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}$
in the Deninger frame (Deninger-genus $=+\infty$); conflation of
curve-genus with Siegel-genus (distinct integers); and the
$\kappa+\kappa^{!}=2-2g$ Euler-shift (Koszul sum preserved at $0$;
genus enters via rank, not shift).

\subsection{Proposed Arithmetic-Modular Datum}
\label{v4-arith:app:B:genus-g:converged}

The arithmetic genus-\(g\) datum \(\mathcal{D}_g=(\mathcal{B}_g,\mathcal{E}_g,\varphi_g)\)
(\Cref{v4-arith:ag:defn:datum}), with the essential conditional
\(\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar},g}_{\mathsf{G}}(\mathcal{D}_g)+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar},g}_{\mathsf{G}}(\mathcal{D}_g)=0\Leftrightarrow\Lambda_{\mathcal{D}_g}(s)=\epsilon_{\mathcal{D}_g}\Lambda_{\mathcal{D}_g}(w_{\mathcal{D}_g}-s)\)
(\Cref{v4-arith:ag:thm:all-genus}), with
\(w_{\mathcal{D}_g}\in\{1,2,2g+1\}\) window-indexed. The
functional-equation clause is not a zero-placement theorem. The
all-genus F-RH corollary
(\Cref{v4-arith:ag:cor:all-genus-FRH}) contains the corresponding
zero-placement clauses only after the relevant spectral-positivity
input is imposed: RH for \(\zeta\), Hasse--Weil RH for Jacobians,
Dedekind-zeta GRH for totally imaginary fields, and Siegel-spin RH for
\(\mathrm{GSp}_{2g}\) cusp forms.

The arithmetic-modular extension sources the finite-level
arithmetic Chern--Simons state sums and the conjectural renormalized
$GL_{2}(\widehat{\mathbb{Z}})$ limit on the \'etale
$\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}$ of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:3d-acs-E1bar}. The finite-level boundary
category is defined there after the finite cobordism input, and the
ordered $E_{1}$-bar complex is constructed there; the identification
of the Hochschild boundary trace with $\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}$
and the partition-function identity with $\xi$ remain conditional on
profinite renormalization and local trace comparison. The functional
equation then appears as the involutive symmetry of the partition
function; zero-placement remains the separate positivity condition.
