\section{Falsifications Across Voices}
\label{v4-arith:app:B:falsifications}

The following nine naive structural claims about $\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}$ fail at specific algebraic or geometric joints, identified independently across the fifteen trajectories:

\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}$ is a vertex operator algebra (VOA):
\textbf{false} (Polyakov). The central charge
$c=\sum_{p}2=\infty$, and $\zeta_{\mathbb{P}}(0)$
has a natural boundary.
\item \(\bigotimes^{!}_{p}\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) is defined over a field:
\textbf{false} (Drinfeld+Kapranov). Different \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\)
cannot be glued into a single field.
\item Beilinson--Drinfeld factorization algebra on \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\):
\textbf{false} (Costello+Gwilliam). The completed tensor product
\(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\,\widehat{\otimes}_{\mathbb{Z}}\,\mathbb{Z}_{p}=\mathbb{Z}_{p}\)
admits no $p$-adic diagonal thickening.
\item \(T_{n}\) lifts Adams operations to \(GL_{2}\) Hecke operators:
\textbf{false} (Kazhdan+Beilinson). The explicit computation gives
\(T_{p}^{2}(a^{(p)}_{-1}|0\rangle)-p\cdot a^{(p)}_{-1}|0\rangle\neq T_{p^{2}}(a^{(p)}_{-1}|0\rangle)\).
\item $\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}$ is a Borger $\Lambda$-ring:
\textbf{false or vacuous} (Gelfand+Borger). The Wilkerson
congruence fails on the Fock $\mathbb{Z}$-lattice; the claim is
vacuous over $\mathbb{C}$.
\item Arithmetic Yangian via RTT relations:
\textbf{false} (Drinfeld--solo). The Yang-Baxter spectral parameter
$u-v$ has no analogue across distinct primes; there is no meromorphic
$1$-form on $\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$.
\item 4D class-$\mathcal{S}$ gauge boundary: \textbf{false} (Gaiotto).
The Arakelov vanishing \(H^{0}(\overline{C},K^{k})=0\) forces all
Coulomb branches to vanish.
\item $\mathcal{D}$-modules on \(\mathrm{Bun}_{GL_{2}}(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z})\):
\textbf{false} (Bezrukavnikov+Bernstein).
The moduli stack $\mathrm{Bun}_{GL_{2}}(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z})$
is a point (class number $1$), so carries no nontrivial
$\mathcal{D}$-module theory.
\item $E_{2}$-chiral-centre enhancement integral: \textbf{false}
(Kontsevich+Soibelman). The Dyer--Lashof operation mod $p$ and the
Kaledin $p$-curvature cocycle produce an obstruction class.
\end{enumerate}
