\begin{abstract}
Arithmetic realization compares the operations that produce an invariant
before evaluating it. We study coefficient algebras with specified
modules, local conditions, units and functionals, together with the
comparison maps that preserve these data. Explicit Gaussian, cubic and
matrix examples distinguish a scalar equality from a comparison of
products, higher operations or intermediate states.

The algebraic constructions include relative coefficient envelopes,
composition of interfaces and their extension--restriction corings.
Descent is proved under explicit completeness and detection hypotheses.
For a strict cospan of differential graded algebras over a commutative
ring, the two endpoint products admit an integral all-arity isomorphism
of bar coalgebras. Its module and bimodule forms retain every ordered
product of local primitives. The comparison extends to restricted
inverse limits of finite coefficient bars; an unrestricted product over
tensor lengths can fail continuity.

The arithmetic examples concern Galois cochains and local conditions,
Hall and Hecke operations, coefficient duality, categorical traces and
Chern--Simons comparisons. Their geometric and automorphic
identifications require separately specified maps. The analytic endpoint
is the complete signed Weil functional, with its polar, archimedean and
prime contributions. Positive forms on auxiliary algebras do not identify
this functional without a compatible evaluation theorem. The native
arithmetic comparisons and positivity on the full Weil test domain
remain distinct construction problems.
\end{abstract}
