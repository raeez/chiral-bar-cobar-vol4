\begin{abstract}
The comparison functor
\[
 \mathrm{Real}(X,T)=(d,e)
\]
sends a theorem \(T\) of the first three volumes to a pair of
constructions \(d,e\): \(d\) produces the invariant and \(e\)
computes it by another route.
The two components are required to meet only in the invariant being
identified. Thus an equality such as a central charge formula, a
Koszul duality equivalence, or a determinant identity is tested
against a construction which does not contain the formula as an
input.

Vol~I inputs to \(\mathrm{Real}\): the Virasoro shadow tower with
Stokes pole \(c_S=-218/45\), Borel radius
\(|\omega|^2(c)=c^2(5c+22)/[4(45c+218)]\), Riccati equation
\(U'+2U^2=Q_c''/(2Q_c)\) on \(U=(\log H)'-2/t\), the four-conductor
split \((K,K^\kappa,K_c,K_g)\), the \(p=691\) Chenevier
determinant, and the eight-form Gritsenko--Cl\'ery spread.
Vol~II inputs: the \(\mathsf{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}\) heptagon and
the finite-jet Khan--Zeng (KZ) Poisson vertex algebra (PVA) sector,
in which the shifted-cotangent Batalin--Vilkovisky (BV) action and
the classical master equation (CME) / PVA Hamiltonian equivalence
are constructed; the all-loop quantum master equation (QME),
reflected weight identity, boundary vertex algebra, and \(E_3\)-lift
remain conditional on the four endpoint hypotheses: the KZ analytic
spectral-decomposition representation (SDR), the Fulton--MacPherson
Stokes datum, the reflected weights, and the \(T\)-lift. Vol~III inputs: the
two-stage Calabi--Yau-to-chiral factorisation
\(\Phi_d=\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d\), the K3 Hall--Drinfeld chiral bialgebra
\(\mathbf{H}_{\Delta_5}\), the Joyce vertex algebra paired with
Kontsevich--Pandharipande--Soibelman (KPS) orientation data on
generic compact Calabi--Yau threefolds with
Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande (MNOP) invariants,
\(S^3\)-framing on generic compact Calabi--Yau threefold Lagrangians,
and the \(\mathfrak{so}(4,20)\) K3 Yangian representation-target
trichotomy.

The arithmetic branch extends \(\mathrm{Real}\) to
\(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb Z}\). Its boundary is not a closure
theorem: it consists of the RH-equivalent archimedean Tate positivity
obstruction, the global arithmetic comparison inputs, the local
categorical and Bernstein-centre gluing inputs, the condensed-Hilbert
descent input, and the remaining supersingular or icosahedral
generator-comparison tails. On the critical-line support
\(\Gamma_\xi^{\mathrm{crit}}=\{\gamma\in\mathbb R:\xi(\tfrac12+i\gamma)=0\}\),
the ordinary \(M_t\)-graph dual carries neither point nor finite-jet
Hadamard residue data; any surviving spectral-flow argument passes
through a stronger rigged, Paley--Wiener, de Branges,
reproducing-kernel, nonlocal \(L^2\), or BV--BFV boundary topology
compatible with the post-Tate quotient.
\end{abstract}
