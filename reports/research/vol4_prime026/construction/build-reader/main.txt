Occupation, Mellin Dilation, and Arithmetic Comparisons

                                           Raeez Lorgat




                                              Abstract

     The Mellin transform sends multiplication by minus the logarithm to spectral differentiation. It
 sends the normalized dilation generator to spectral multiplication. An explicit embedding of integer
 occupation states into distributions realizes the first action, but its composition with zero residues
 does not intertwine occupation energy with zero ordinates. The second action induces triangular
 operators on full residue jets. Positive evaluation norms give Hilbert spaces with these prescribed
 spectral data, while evaluation from the original continuous Hilbert space is not closable. The
 normalizations, multiple-zero terms, and conditions for symmetry are computed explicitly. Core
 isometries transport closed operators, traces, and determinants under explicit domain hypotheses.
 Positive convolution forms produce unitary representations, while their cyclic spectral weights do
 not determine ordinary trace multiplicities. The prime measure has an exact exponential threshold
 for temperedness. The Hadamard constant, polar terms, and Fourier weights in the explicit formula
 are derived on specified test spaces. A full occupation perturbation has a continuous trace map on
 Schwartz spectral tests. Its one-sided spectral support excludes the full Weil trace identity.

    The physical distinction is between logarithmic position and its conjugate momentum. Integer oc-
cupation selects discrete position values. Mellin dilation measures continuous momentum. Residue
