The local-to-global problem requires coherent objects, compatible
actions and effective descent. A separate local calculation compares
weighted occupation vectors, diagonal Hilbert--Schmidt operators and
radial functions. The spherical Whittaker norm is computed with its
measure factor, and its unit normalization gives an explicit line
isometry. These constructions isolate the actual maps still needed
for arithmetic Koszul--Petersson compatibility.

\section{Bernstein Gluing and Local Pairing Conditions}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:context}
The BC-diamond-glue condition concerns local coherent Springer
objects on the intended spectral carriers, their equivariant descent
to a Selmer target, and identification of the descended central
action with the global automorphic Bernstein centre. Coherent
Springer theory on a Langlands-parameter stack does not itself
construct the comparison to an Emerton--Gee stack.

For pairing compatibility, fix both carriers and the type of each
form. Hermitian forms are linear in the first variable and
conjugate-linear in the second; inserting an antilinear map into the
second slot of a bilinear form produces this type. On the prime
cyclic Gibbs carrier, \(J\mu_{p^k}\Omega=p^{k/2}\mu_{p^k}^*\Omega\).
Its arithmetic transport requires the action-compatible placement
maps of Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}.

\section{BC-Diamond-Glue: Decomposition}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:decomposition}

\begin{definition}[the three sub-operations of (BC-diamond-glue)]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:def:three-sub-operations}
(BC-diamond-glue) decomposes into three sub-operations:
\begin{description}
\item[(BCglue.local)] \emph{Local Ben-Zvi--Chen--Helm--Nadler construction.} At each
finite prime \(p\), construct a coherent Springer sheaf
\(\mathcal{F}_{p}\in\mathrm{Coh}(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},p}_{GL_{2}})\)
whose action on the local \(\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\)-category
realises the local Bernstein centre \(\mathcal{Z}_{p}\).
\item[(BCglue.glue)] \emph{Cross-prime coefficient descent.}
Construct a common coefficient carrier and actual transition
correspondences for the local sheaves, with the support, filtration,
and higher-coherence data of
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:lem:prismatic-flatness}.
Supply the effective coherent-descent model on the Selmer target
required by Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:prismatic-gluing}.
The global sheaf is obtained from that descent equivalence; a
formal product across distinct primes or a filtered colimit of
coherent sheaves does not supply it.
\item[(BCglue.core)] \emph{Global Bernstein-centre compatibility.}
The action of \(\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{glob}}^{\mathrm{prism}}\) on
\(\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\bar\rho,\det,\mathcal{L}})\)
agrees, under the conjectural arithmetic AG equivalence of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:thm:arithmetic-AG}, with the global Bernstein-centre
action on \(\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}([GL_{2}])\).
\end{description}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[decomposition of (BC-diamond-glue)]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:decomposition}
(BC-diamond-glue) is equivalent to the
conjunction
\[
(\mathrm{BCglue.local})\;\wedge\;(\mathrm{BCglue.glue})\;\wedge\;(\mathrm{BCglue.core}).
\]
The core condition concerns the action on the object obtained from
the first two constructions; no logical independence is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Forward.} Suppose (BC-diamond-glue), i.e.\
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:acd:cj:bc-diamond-glue-concrete}, holds. Then the
local coherent Springer sheaves \(\{\mathcal{F}_{v}\}_{v}\) exist
(supplying (BCglue.local)), glue to
\(\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{glob}}^{\mathrm{prism}}\) along the diagonal
(supplying (BCglue.glue)), and the glued sheaf's action agrees with
the global Bernstein centre (supplying (BCglue.core)).

\emph{Reverse.} Suppose (BCglue.local), (BCglue.glue),
(BCglue.core) all hold. Then (BCglue.local) gives the local sheaves;
(BCglue.glue) assembles them into a global coherent sheaf;
(BCglue.core) gives the Bernstein-centre compatibility. Together,
these are exactly the content of
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:acd:cj:bc-diamond-glue-concrete}.

The equivalence separates the required data without constructing them.
\end{proof}

\section{BC-Diamond-Glue: Local Construction (Ben-Zvi--Chen--Helm--Nadler)}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:local}

\begin{theorem}[Transport of a local coherent Springer object]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:local-BZCHN-plus-EG}
Fix a common coefficient category and a coherent Springer object
\(S_p\) on a specified parameter carrier \(\mathcal Y_p\).
Supply an exact functor
\[
 T_p:\mathrm{Coh}(\mathcal Y_p)\longrightarrow
       \mathrm{Coh}(\mathcal X_p^{\mathrm{EG}})
\]
preserving the required singular support, together with compatible
natural equivalences identifying its transported central action with
the action on the stated local representation category. Then
\(\mathcal F_p=T_p(S_p)\) is a coherent object with that central action.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Preservation of coherence and support gives the object on the stated
carrier. Apply \(T_p\) to the action morphisms. Functoriality preserves
their unit and composition identities, and the supplied natural
equivalences identify the resulting action with the required one.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}[Local coefficient and spectral comparisons]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:rem:domain-local}
Ben-Zvi--Chen--Helm--Nadler construct the coherent Springer input on
its Langlands-parameter carrier. Construct the functor \(T_p\) and
the compatible representation-theoretic action comparison above.
If several coefficient realizations are used, construct a common
comparison carrier, the functors to it, and compatible isomorphisms
between the resulting objects. In particular, the \(\ell=p\) and
cross-coefficient comparisons must be supplied; agreement of
parameters or traces alone does not produce these isomorphisms.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Prismatic Transition Maps}

\begin{definition}[Nygaard-convention-aware transition map]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:def:nygaard-transition}
For distinct primes \(p,q\) and pairs
\((R_{p},\varphi_{p},\Gamma_{p})\), \((R_{q},\varphi_{q},\Gamma_{q})\)
of Emerton--Gee data (here \(R_{p}\) is the period ring,
\(\varphi_{p}\) the Frobenius, \(\Gamma_{p}=\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})/\mathbb{Q}_{p})\)
the cyclotomic Galois group, as in Emerton--Gee arXiv:1908.07185 \S2),
the \emph{Nygaard-convention-aware transition
map} is a proposed comparison morphism
\[
\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},p}_{GL_{2}}\times\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},q}_{GL_{2}}\;\to\;\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},\{p,q\}}_{GL_{2}}
\]
where the target denotes a sought common moduli carrier for the
rank-\(2\) local data at \(p\) and \(q\). Its construction and
coefficient comparison are part of
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:lem:prismatic-flatness}; a product
of formal bases at distinct primes does not define it.
The Nygaard filtration convention of Bhatt--Lurie
arXiv:2201.06120 \S8 is used to specify the expected comparison data.
For \(p\neq q\) this is a residual comparison correspondence, not a
canonical fibre-product identity of formal bases.
\end{definition}

\begin{hypothesis}[Cross-prime transition and descent data]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:lem:prismatic-flatness}
For the proposed comparison of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:def:nygaard-transition}, supply a
common coefficient category and actual transition correspondences.
Assume their pullback functors preserve coherent objects and the
specified nilpotent singular support, commute with the required
colimits, and carry compatible Nygaard filtrations. Assume the
comparison isomorphisms on double overlaps satisfy the triple
cocycle and all higher coherences. These are cross-prime data,
not consequences of separate theories over \(\mathbb Z_p\) and
\(\mathbb Z_q\).
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{remark}[Flat base change and the missing arithmetic maps]
For a flat homomorphism of Noetherian rings \(A\to B\), tensoring
with \(B\) is exact and preserves finite presentation: a finite
presentation \(A^r\to A^s\to M\to0\) becomes
\(B^r\to B^s\to B\otimes_A M\to0\). Thus it sends coherent
modules to coherent modules. This elementary calculation explains
one required property once a common base and a flat map are given.
It constructs neither a cross-prime map nor a singular-support
comparison. A filtered colimit of coherent objects also need not
be coherent: the increasing finite-dimensional subspaces of
\(\bigoplus_{n\ge1}k\) are a counterexample over a field.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Effective Descent of the Local Sheaves}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional coefficient descent]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:prismatic-gluing}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:lem:prismatic-flatness}.
Let \(X=\mathcal X^{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\bar\rho,\det,\mathcal L}\).
Supply an effective descent diagram \(U_\bullet\to X\) and an
explicit equivalence
\[
 \mathrm{Coh}(X)\xrightarrow{\sim}
 \operatorname{Tot}\bigl(\mathrm{Coh}(U_\bullet)\bigr)
\]
on the prescribed singular-support and coefficient subcategories.
If the local coherent Springer sheaves and comparison isomorphisms
form an object of the displayed totalization, they determine a
coherent sheaf \(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{glob}}^{\mathrm{prism}}\) on \(X\),
unique up to the equivalence of descent objects.
Any archimedean factor requires its own compatible complex/Hodge
comparison data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The sheaves, overlap isomorphisms, and higher coherences define the
stated descent object. Apply the inverse of the supplied descent
equivalence. The resulting sheaf pulls back to each prescribed local
object, and full faithfulness gives uniqueness and compatibility of
its morphisms. Coherence follows from the target of that equivalence,
not from an arbitrary filtered colimit in an ind-category.
\end{proof}

\end{question}

\subsection{Reduction to core compatibility}

\begin{theorem}[Compatible finite-stage central actions]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:core-compatibility}
Let \(F:\mathcal C\simeq\mathcal D\) be an equivalence of stable
categories. Let \((A_S)\) be a compatible diagram of commutative
algebras indexed by all finite sets of places, with
\(A=\varinjlim_S A_S\). Suppose compatible actions
\[
 a_S:A_S\to Z^0(\mathcal C),\qquad b_S:A_S\to Z^0(\mathcal D)
\]
are given, where \(Z^0(\mathcal C)=\operatorname{End}(\operatorname{Id}_{h\mathcal C})\)
is the ordinary centre of the homotopy category, consisting of ordinary
natural transformations; similarly for \(\mathcal D\). If
\(F(a_S(z)_M)=b_S(z)_{F(M)}\) for every \(S,z,M\), then \(F\)
intertwines the induced \(A\)-actions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The compatible algebra maps factor uniquely through \(A\). Every
element of \(A\) comes from a finite stage, where the intertwining
identity holds. Thus it holds for the induced actions.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}[Coherent actions and the arithmetic centre]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:rem:core-vs-unconditional}
For higher actions, the corresponding input is a compatible system
of maps in the chosen category of derived algebras, with a coherent
homotopy between the two systems. The colimit identity
\[
 \operatorname{Map}(\operatorname*{colim}_S A_S,Z)
 \simeq\operatorname*{lim}_S\operatorname{Map}(A_S,Z)
\]
assembles that supplied homotopy. Equalities in degree zero do not
supply it. The index consists of all finite sets; the poset of pairs
alone is not filtered.

For BCglue.core, one must further identify \(A\) with the actual
global automorphic Bernstein centre, and \(b_S\) with the actions
of the equivariantly descended coherent object. An equivalence of
underlying categories does not do this. For example, on
\(\mathcal C=\mathcal D=D(k)\), the identity equivalence fails to
intertwine the two \(k[t]\)-actions \(t\mapsto0\) and
\(t\mapsto1\): at the object \(k\) the required equation would be
\(0=\mathrm{id}_k\).

The resulting implication uses the complete data:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{local comparison and effective equivariant descent,}\\
 \text{arithmetic AG and compatible global centre comparisons}
 \end{gathered}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad\text{BC-diamond-glue}.
\]
The original cross-prime and global-centre constructions remain
required, with their coherent equivariant descent data.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The Local Adjoint \(L\)-Factor}

\begin{definition}[local adjoint \(L\)-factor \(L_{p}(s,\mathrm{Ad})\)]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:def:adjoint-L}
For an unramified principal-series representation
\(\pi_{p}=\mathrm{Ind}_{B}^{GL_{2}}(\chi_{1}\otimes\chi_{2})\) at
\(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})\) with Satake parameters
\((\alpha_{p},\beta_{p})=(\chi_{1}(p),\chi_{2}(p))\) on the unit
circle, the local adjoint \(L\)-factor is
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:eq:adjoint-L}
L_{p}(s,\mathrm{Ad}(\pi_{p}))
\;:=\;\bigl(1-\alpha_{p}\beta_{p}^{-1}\,p^{-s}\bigr)^{-1}
\cdot(1-p^{-s})^{-1}
\cdot\bigl(1-\alpha_{p}^{-1}\beta_{p}\,p^{-s}\bigr)^{-1}.
\end{equation}
Reference: Jacquet 1979 Corvallis ``Principal \(L\)-functions'' \S2,
or Bump 1997 \emph{Automorphic Forms and Representations} \S3.2.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Finite adjoint factor for an unramified tempered representation]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:lem:Lp-1-well-defined}
Fix an unramified tempered representation \(\pi_p\) of
\(GL_2(\mathbb Q_p)\), with unitary-normalized Satake parameters
\(\alpha_p,\beta_p\) satisfying \(|\alpha_p|=|\beta_p|=1\).
Then the factor \(L_p(1,\mathrm{Ad}\,\pi_p)\) of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:def:adjoint-L} is finite and nonzero.
This is a statement about the specified representation; its placement
in an arithmetic Hochschild realization is separate comparison data.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The three denominator terms are
\[
 1-\alpha_p\beta_p^{-1}p^{-1},\qquad
 1-p^{-1},\qquad
 1-\alpha_p^{-1}\beta_p p^{-1}.
\]
Each is nonzero because the subtracted term has absolute value
\(p^{-1}<1\). Their inverse product is therefore finite and nonzero.
No inequality between \(\alpha_p\) and \(\beta_p\) is needed.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Absorption Convention}

\begin{definition}[Koszul-dual normalisation convention]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:def:koszul-dual-norm}
The \emph{Koszul-dual normalisation convention} is the rescaling
of the local Koszul pairing at a finite prime \(p\), assuming
\(L_p(1,\mathrm{Ad})\) is finite and nonzero, by the local
adjoint \(L\)-factor:
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:eq:koszul-dual-norm}
\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul,Ad}}_{p}
\;:=\;
L_{p}(1,\mathrm{Ad})^{-1}\cdot\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}_{p}.
\end{equation}
The global normalized pairing is defined using the specified
continued nonzero value \(c_\pi=L(1,\mathrm{Ad}\,\pi)\), as
\(c_\pi^{-1}\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}\),
with the actual adelic extension and comparison maps of
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}.
It is not defined by an unproved ordinary product at \(s=1\).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Spherical Whittaker norm with specified measure]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:lem:spherical-norm}
Let \(\pi_p\) be normalized unramified tempered principal series,
with \(|\alpha|=|\beta|=1\), and take an additive character of
conductor \(\mathbb Z_p\). Let \(W(1)=1\) be its spherical
Whittaker function. Set \(m_p=\operatorname{vol}_{d^\times a}
(\mathbb Z_p^\times)>0\). Its standard spherical formula is
\[
 W(\operatorname{diag}(p^j,1))=p^{-j/2}h_j(\alpha,\beta)
 \quad(j\ge0),\qquad
 h_j=\sum_{r=0}^j\alpha^{j-r}\beta^r,
\]
and it vanishes at negative valuations. For \(\operatorname{Re}s>0\),
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:eq:GJ-funeq}
 Z_p(s):=\int_{\mathbb Q_p^\times}|W(a)|^2|a|^{s-1}\,d^\times a
 =m_p\frac{L_p(s,\pi_p\times\widetilde\pi_p)}{\zeta_p(2s)}.
\end{equation}
In particular
\[
 C_p:=Z_p(1)=m_p(1+p^{-1})L_p(1,\operatorname{Ad}\pi_p)>0.
\]
For \(d^\times a=da/|a|\) with additive unit volume,
\(m_p=1-p^{-1}\), so \(C_p=(1-p^{-2})L_p(1,\operatorname{Ad}\pi_p)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Shell integration gives
\(Z_p(s)=m_p\sum_{j\ge0}|h_j|^2p^{-js}\), which converges
absolutely since \(|h_j|\le j+1\). With \(r=\alpha/\beta\),
summing geometric series gives
\[
 \sum_{j\ge0}|h_j|^2x^j
 =\frac{1+x}{(1-x)(1-rx)(1-r^{-1}x)}.
\]
For \(r\ne1\), this follows by writing
\(|h_j|^2=(2-r^{j+1}-r^{-j-1})/|1-r|^2\) and summing each term.
The case \(r=1\) follows by continuity, or from
\(\sum(j+1)^2x^j=(1+x)/(1-x)^3\).
The rank-four Rankin--Selberg factor has denominator
\((1-x)^2(1-rx)(1-r^{-1}x)\). Multiplication by \(1-x^2\)
proves the displayed integral. Since
\(L_p(s,\pi_p\times\widetilde\pi_p)=\zeta_p(s)L_p(s,\operatorname{Ad}\pi_p)\),
evaluation at one gives \(C_p\). This is a positive norm; finiteness
also follows directly from the convergent shell series. The measure
normalization is the Kirillov normalization used by
Blomer--Jana--Nelson, \emph{Local Integral Transforms and Global
Spectral Decomposition}, \S2, (2.7), and \S3.1.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Occupation, diagonal Hilbert--Schmidt and radial function models]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:K5-absorption-canonical}
Let \(H_{\mathrm{occ}}=\ell^2(\mathbb N_0,p^{-j})\), with coordinate
vectors \(\delta_j\), and let \(E_j=p^j\mathbb Z_p^\times\).
There are explicit isometries
\[
 T\delta_j=p^{-j/2}m_p^{-1/2}\mathbf1_{E_j},\qquad
 D\delta_j=p^{-j/2}E_{jj}
\]
onto, respectively, the closed radial subspace supported on
nonnegative valuations in \(L^2(\mathbb Q_p^\times,d^\times a)\)
and the diagonal subspace of \(\operatorname{HS}(\ell^2\mathbb N_0)\).
They intertwine coordinate conjugation with function conjugation and
\(JX=X^*\). Thus, for all \(v,w\in H_{\mathrm{occ}}\),
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:eq:K5-match}
 H_{\mathrm{occ}}(v,w)=H_{\mathrm{Whit}}(Tv,Tw)
 =\operatorname{Tr}(Dv(Dw)^*).
\end{equation}
The spherical function is
\(W=T(\sqrt{m_p}\sum_{j\ge0}h_j\delta_j)\).
The map \(a\Omega\mapsto aW/\sqrt{C_p}\) is an isometry from the
unit prime Gibbs-vacuum line to the spherical Whittaker line.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The shells are disjoint, each of measure \(m_p\), so the Gram
matrix of \(T\delta_j\) is \(\delta_{jk}p^{-j}\).
The matrix units are Hilbert--Schmidt orthonormal, giving the same
Gram matrix for \(D\delta_j\). Their finite spans are dense in the
stated ranges, proving the extensions and surjectivity onto those
ranges. Both displayed bases are fixed by their conjugations.
The spherical expansion follows shell by shell and converges since
\(\sum p^{-j}|h_j|^2<\infty\). Its norm is \(C_p\), proving the
normalized-line isometry. Notice that \(W\ne T\delta_0\) in
general; its coefficients depend on the representation.
\end{proof}

\begin{conjecture}[Arithmetic local pairing comparison]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:conj:local-comparison-datum}
Construct the arithmetic placement and action-intertwining maps from
the actual bar--cobar carrier to the specified local representation
and Whittaker models, with domains, Haar measure and spherical
normalization fixed. Require them to transport the arithmetic
bilinear pairing and its conjugation to the corresponding Hermitian
form, compatibly with Hecke actions and the adelic extension in
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}.
The preceding radial and diagonal isometries are explicit bounded
models; they do not construct this full arithmetic comparison.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Pairing type and normalization]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:rem:K5-domain}
The Whittaker integral and the occupation Hilbert form are linear
in the first variable and conjugate-linear in the second. An
antilinear map cannot be inserted in their second slot without
changing that type. If \(B_{\mathrm{Whit}}(x,y)=H_{\mathrm{Whit}}(x,Jy)\)
is the corresponding bilinear form, then
\(H_{\mathrm{Whit}}(x,y)=B_{\mathrm{Whit}}(x,Jy)\).
This is the type required in an arithmetic Koszul comparison.

Adjoint rescaling alone changes the spherical norm to
\(m_p(1+p^{-1})\), not to one. Unit normalization uses the full
positive factor \(C_p\). This scalar is distinct from
\(\operatorname{vol}(GL_2(\mathbb Z_p))=(1-p^{-1})(1-p^{-2})\)
for the standard additive-matrix Haar convention. Ramified and
non-tempered representations require their own integral and domain
calculation. The finite nonzero continued global value in
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:K5-adelic-consistent} is a
separate analytic input, not a proof of local denominator finiteness.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Adelic Consistency}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional adelic normalization on the cuspidal sph-fin locus]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:K5-adelic-consistent}
Fix a cuspidal representation \(\pi\) and the local comparison datum
of Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:conj:local-comparison-datum}.
Assume the placement and adelic pairing maps of
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing} on the
specified sph-fin core. Suppose the adjoint Euler product, initially
defined in a half-plane of absolute convergence, has a specified
holomorphic continuation to \(s=1\) with
\(c_\pi=L(1,\mathrm{Ad}\,\pi)\ne0\).
Then
\[
 \langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul,Ad}}
 :=c_\pi^{-1}\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}
\]
is a well-defined rescaled pairing on that core. If the Petersson
pairing is rescaled by the same scalar, the assumed pairing
comparison is preserved. The value \(c_\pi\) is a continued value;
no absolute convergence of \(\prod_pL_p(1,\mathrm{Ad}\,\pi)\)
is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Multiplication by the nonzero scalar \(c_\pi^{-1}\) defines the
rescaled form. Multiplying the assumed identity
\(\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}=
\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}w\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}\)
by that scalar gives the identity of reduced pairings on the same
domain. It neither enlarges the domain nor constructs its placement.

The distinction between an Euler product and its continued value is
already visible for tempered unramified local parameters. In that
case the three adjoint parameters have absolute value one. For
\(\sigma=\operatorname{Re}(s)>1\), the logarithmic series is bounded by
\[
 \sum_p\sum_{j\geq1}\frac{3p^{-j\sigma}}{j}
 \leq\frac{3}{1-2^{-\sigma}}\sum_p p^{-\sigma}<\infty.
\]
Finitely many ramified factors do not affect this estimate away from
their poles. The bound establishes absolute convergence in this
half-plane; it gives no convergence assertion at \(s=1\).
For other local regimes one starts in a half-plane where the Euler
product is known to converge. Analytic continuation and nonvanishing
at one are separate analytic inputs, of the kind considered in
Hoffstein--Lockhart 1994 and the Langlands--Shahidi theory; their
applicability to the chosen representation is part of the hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[K-P compat on the cuspidal sph-fin locus under the local comparison datum]

\emph{(conditional on

interpreted under the Koszul-dual normalisation convention of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:def:koszul-dual-norm} and the

with the additional arithmetic maps of

\begin{proof}
The local comparison datum supplies the finite-place pairing match

Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:kp:thm:main-full} therefore applies to these
maps. Multiplying both forms by the same nonzero \(c_\pi^{-1}\)

neither the global placement nor this extension. The additional ramified and continuous
comparisons remain those of Remark~\ref{v4-arith:kp:rem:domain-restriction}.
\end{proof}

\section{Remaining Arithmetic Constructions}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:residual}
The local objects and their comparison functors must be constructed
on the chosen coefficient and spectral carriers. Their cross-prime
assembly requires actual equivariant descent data, while the
global centre identification remains separate from the underlying
arithmetic AG equivalence.

\begin{remark}[The complete descent and pairing data]
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:rem:residual-summary}
After local transport and effective coherent descent are supplied,
BCglue.core asks for the actual global-centre identification and its
coherent action comparison. Pairwise statements alone do not supply
this datum. The conditional composition theorem explains how a
compatible system over all finite sets assembles once constructed.

The K5 calculation gives concrete local Hilbert-space maps and the
spherical norm. Its arithmetic extension requires maps preserving
Hecke actions, the bilinear pairing and conjugation. Adelic transport
also requires its completed domains and normalization. These are
not consequences of a finite nonzero adjoint factor.
\end{remark}

\section{Constructed Models and the Global Target}
\label{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:summary}
The local coherent transport theorem, finite-stage central-action
composition, spherical integral and explicit Hilbert isometries are
proved on their stated carriers. Effective coherent descent is

hypotheses of Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:cor:K-P-unconditional}.

GNS space with the automorphic carrier. Ramified, full archimedean

Arithmetic Positselski, the Hilbert--P\'olya realization and Li positivity
also remain separate inputs to the sufficient RH implication.
