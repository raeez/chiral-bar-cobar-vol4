\chapter{Conditional Hypotheses in Spine-A Arithmetic Chapters}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A}

Several structural reductions in the Spine-A chapters are logically
correct as equivalences yet describe component sub-obligations as
theorems when those sub-obligations are conditional on open inputs:
the Fargues--Scholze-to-Emerton--Gee categorical equivalence
(F1.a, \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification), the arithmetic
Arinkin--Gaitsgory conjecture
(\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.1\)--\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.3\)),
and the formal-Noetherian extension of Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum
(R1.formal). This chapter records the corrected conditional
hypotheses, taking
\Cref{v4-arith:app:C:residual-table-w2},
\Cref{v4-arith:platonic-rh-completion}, and
\Cref{v4-arith:rh-reformulation} as the authoritative reference for
current claim status.

\section{Corrected Conditional Statements}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:catalog}

Each item below names the theorem or corollary carrying a status
discrepancy, states the corrected hypothesis, and cites the
authoritative reference for the correction.

\subsection{Corrections in \Cref{v4-arith:closure}: Closure of F1--F3}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:ch22}

\paragraph{F1.a scope: Fargues--Scholze local geometrisation versus categorical equivalence.}
\Cref{v4-arith:thm:F1-reduction} carries status
\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification for F1.a, the local categorical
equivalence
\[
\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})])
\simeq
\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},p}_{GL_{2}})
\]
(where \(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},p}_{GL_2}\) is the
Emerton--Gee moduli stack at the prime \(p\),
\(\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\) denotes ind-coherent sheaves
with nilpotent singular support in the sense of Arinkin--Gaitsgory,
and \(\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\) the spherical-finite
derived category of smooth \(GL_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)\)-representations).
Fargues--Scholze arXiv:2102.13459 supplies the local geometrisation,
spectral action, and Langlands-parameter package for \(GL_2\); the
displayed categorical equivalence is not contained there.
Dotto--Emerton--Gee arXiv:2603.26887 gives a fully-faithful-only,
fixed-central-character functor for \(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})\) at
\(p\geq 5\). The authoritative reference for the current F1.a status
is \Cref{v4-arith:app:C:residual-table-w2} and
\Cref{v4-arith:thm:F1-reduction}.

\paragraph{Emerton--Gee preprint identifier.}
The correct preprint identifier for Emerton--Gee is arXiv:1908.07185
(\emph{Moduli stacks of \'etale \((\varphi,\Gamma)\)-modules and the
existence of crystalline lifts}, Annals of Mathematics Studies).
See App.~A \S\ref{v4-arith:app:A:langlands}.

\paragraph{Ben-Zvi--Chen--Helm--Nadler preprint identifier.}
The correct identifier for Ben-Zvi--Chen--Helm--Nadler is
arXiv:2010.02321 (\emph{Coherent Springer theory and the categorical
Deligne--Langlands correspondence}, Invent.~Math.~235 (2024)).
See App.~A \S\ref{v4-arith:app:A:BZN-coherent-Springer}.

\subsection{Corrections in \Cref{v4-arith:adelic-descent}: Adelic Categorical Descent}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:ch27}

\paragraph{Function-field transport: conditional closure of \((L_2)\).}
\Cref{v4-arith:acd:thm:function-field-transport} carries status
\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification. The closure of \((L_{1})\) follows
from Raskin's \(\infty\)-categorical construction independently of
function-field Langlands; the closure of \((L_{2})\) requires the
local categorical comparison F1.a (\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification
via Dotto--Emerton--Gee) and Bernstein-centre diamond gluing.

\paragraph{\((L_2)\) reduction: Fargues--Scholze scope.}
\Cref{v4-arith:acd:thm:L2-reduction} carries status
\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification. The comparison functors
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{loc}}_{v}\) identifying \(\mathcal{C}_{v}\) with
\(\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},v}_{GL_{2}})\)
are not supplied by Fargues--Scholze arXiv:2102.13459; that source
gives the local geometrisation and spectral action. The local
categorical comparison is the residual open input.

\paragraph{F1 reduction: two open inputs.}
\Cref{v4-arith:acd:thm:F1-closure} states at
\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification that F1 reduces to F1.a
plus (BC-diamond-glue). Since F1.a is itself
\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification, the reduction leaves F1 conditional
on two independent open inputs: F1.a and (BC-diamond-glue).

\subsection{Corrections in \Cref{v4-arith:arith-bzn}: Arithmetic Ben-Zvi--Nadler}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:ch28}

\paragraph{F3.BZN\({}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\): procyclic shadow versus full Weil parameter stack.}
\Cref{v4-arith:bzn:thm:main} carries status \ClaimStatusConjectured.
The comparison target for F3.BZN\({}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) is the full
Weil/\((\varphi,\Gamma)\) parameter stack
\(\mathrm{LocSys}^{W,\varphi\Gamma}_{GL_{2}}\), not the procyclic
shadow
\(\mathrm{Map}(B\widehat{\mathbb{Z}},\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\bar\rho,\det,\mathcal{L}})\),
which forgets Frobenius and unramified data. The theorem is
conditional on F1 and F2. \Cref{v4-arith:bzn:prop:EG-to-LocSys}
carries \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification; the closed-immersion
property \Cref{v4-arith:bzn:prop:EG-image-flat} carries
\ClaimStatusConjectured, since the closed-immersion property is
unavailable on the inertia-only shadow.

\subsection{Corrections in \Cref{v4-arith:audit}: Structural Reduction Theorems}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:ch30}

\paragraph{\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) equivalence and sub-obligation statuses.}
\Cref{v4-arith:sw:thm:LACD-split} \ClaimStatusProvedHere: the
adelic categorical descent condition \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) is
equivalent to the conjunction of sub-obligations
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.1\) through \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.4\).
The equivalence itself is a logical factorisation at
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. Sub-obligations
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.1\)--\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.3\) carry
\ClaimStatusConjectured; \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.4\) is a theorem
(Kneser 1966). The residual content is recorded in the
sub-obligation statuses.

\paragraph{F3.BZN\({}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) equivalence via Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum.}
\Cref{v4-arith:sw:thm:BZN-Ar-split} \ClaimStatusProvedHere:
F3.BZN\({}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\), assembled through Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum
DAG Vol.~II, is equivalent to the conjunction of R1, R2, and R3.
The equivalence is a valid logical factorisation. R1 holds as a
theorem for smooth finite-type stacks (Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum) and
is \ClaimStatusConjectured for the formal-Noetherian Emerton--Gee
setting (R1.formal); R2 is \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification; R3 is
definitional. The R1 fine structure and the honest residual are
recorded in \Cref{v4-arith:bzn-sub} and
\Cref{v4-arith:bzn-sub:cor:F3-BZN-closure-path}.

\paragraph{Arithmetic Positselski C1--C5: conditional hypotheses.}
\Cref{v4-arith:rh-reformulation} treats the arithmetic Positselski
equivalence C1--C5 as five sub-obligations, of which C1--C4 are
conditional on explicit hypotheses and C5 (functoriality) is the
named residual. The claimed unconditional arithmetic Heisenberg
equivalence is scoped to the abelian-Heisenberg sector; the C5
functoriality residual limits the full claim.

\subsection{Corrections in \Cref{v4-arith:lacd-sub}: \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) Sub-Obligations}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:ch31}

\paragraph{\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.0\): archimedean stratum construction.}
\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:thm:L0-closed} \ClaimStatusProvedHere: the
archimedean local category \(\mathcal{C}_{\infty}\), distinguished
unit, and Langlands--Vogan classification are constructed
unconditionally. The four construction items are theorems of
Harish-Chandra (1966), Vogan (1981), and Wallach (1988). This closes
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.0\) as a construction theorem independent
of the global \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\).

\paragraph{\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.1\): conditional on F2.c tightened.}
\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:thm:L1-closed} \ClaimStatusProvedHere\ (conditional
on F2.c tightened): the closure of \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.1\)
uses F2.c tightened (\Cref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}), which
carries status \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification
(\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:tbl:status}). The hypothesis clause of
\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:thm:L1-closed} states ``assume F2.c tightened'';
the claim-status tag is updated to
\ClaimStatusProvedHereConditional\ to match the wording of
\Cref{v4-arith:thm:P3-resolution} for P3.

\paragraph{\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.2\): local Hecke commutativity, not global functoriality.}
\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:thm:L2-closed} \ClaimStatusProvedHere: formal
tensor-factor commutativity of local Hecke endofunctors, once the
restricted tensor product is constructed. The scope remark
\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:rem:L2-formal-scope} records that global Hecke
functoriality is not proved here; the
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.2\) label refers to the local
commutativity sub-obligation only.

\paragraph{\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) residual: three-component decomposition.}
\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:cor:LACD-residual} records that
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) reduces, under F2.c tightened, to
(BC-diamond-glue). The residual (BC-diamond-glue) further decomposes
as (BCglue.local) \(\wedge\) (BCglue.glue) \(\wedge\)
(BC-diamond-glue.core) per \Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:decomposition},
each component carrying distinct status. Closure of
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) requires F1.a in addition
(\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification via Dotto--Emerton--Gee).
An explicit note to this effect is added to
\Cref{v4-arith:lacd:cor:LACD-residual}.

\subsection{Corrections in \Cref{v4-arith:bzn-sub}: BZN Formal Noetherian and EG Derived}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:ch32}

\paragraph{R2: derived enhancement of the Emerton--Gee stack.}
\Cref{v4-arith:bzn-sub:thm:R2-main} \ClaimStatusProvedHere: the
Emerton--Gee stack carries the structure of a derived formal algebraic
stack in the Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum DAG sense, assembled from local
derived enhancement (Mazur--Kisin--Lurie DAG~X), finite-product
cotangent complex, ind-colimit cotangent complex, and quasi-smoothness.
The derived enhancement packages existing Emerton--Gee data using
Lurie DAG machinery; it does not reconstruct that data. The
finite-type Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum theorem (R1.fin-type) applies to
smooth finite-type stacks; its arithmetic application to the formal
Noetherian stack
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\bar\rho,\det,\mathcal{L}}\) is
mediated by R1.formal (\ClaimStatusConjectured), as recorded in
\Cref{v4-arith:bzn-sub:thm:R1-formal-reduction}.

\paragraph{R1 scope: finite-type theorem inapplicable to formal Noetherian setting.}
The Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum integral-transforms theorem for smooth
finite-type stacks (R1.fin-type) does not directly apply to the
global Emerton--Gee stack, which is formal Noetherian. The
extension to the formal Noetherian setting is R1.formal
(\ClaimStatusConjectured). The chapter summary of
\Cref{v4-arith:bzn-sub} records that downstream closure of
F3.BZN\({}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) is conditional on R1.formal.

\subsection{Corrections in \Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5}: BC-Diamond-Glue Decomposition}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:ch35}

\paragraph{(BC-diamond-glue) three-component factorisation: status of components.}
\Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:decomposition} \ClaimStatusProvedHere:
\[
\text{(BC-diamond-glue)}
\;\Leftrightarrow\;
\text{(BCglue.local)} \wedge \text{(BCglue.glue)} \wedge
\text{(BC-diamond-glue.core)}.
\]
The equivalence is a valid logical factorisation. The component
(BCglue.local) requires a primary-source transition theorem unifying
BZCHN at \(\ell\neq p\), Zhu/Emerton--Gee at \(\ell = p\), and
Fargues--Scholze \(\ell\)-independence;
\Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:local-BZCHN-plus-EG} therefore
carries \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification. The component (BCglue.glue)
requires prismatic faithfully-flat descent
(\Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:lem:prismatic-flatness},
\ClaimStatusConjectured); \Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:thm:prismatic-gluing}
carries \ClaimStatusConjectured. The summary
\Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:rem:residual-summary} is updated to
describe (BCglue.local) as theorem-waiting and (BCglue.glue) as
conjectural, matching their proof-level tags.

\paragraph{\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) tight reduction: conditional on three inputs.}
\Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:cor:LACD-tight-catalog} \ClaimStatusConjectured:
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) reduces to (BC-diamond-glue.core),
conditional on arithmetic algebraic geometry hypotheses (scope
10--15 pp). The reduction requires: (i) (BCglue.local) elevated
from \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification; (ii) (BCglue.glue) elevated
from \ClaimStatusConjectured; (iii) F2.c tightened, F1.a, and the
upstream \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.k\) sub-obligations. The status
is updated from \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification to
\ClaimStatusConjectured to reflect this dependence.

\section{Status Summary for Spine-A Chapters}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:summary}

The status corrections recorded in \S\ref{v4-arith:w3-audit-A:catalog}
do not alter the structural reductions proved in Spine-A; they
align the claim-status tags with the conditional hypotheses already
named in those proofs.

In \Cref{v4-arith:closure}: F1.a carries \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification
(Dotto--Emerton--Gee arXiv:2603.26887, fully-faithful-only at \(p\geq 5\),
fixed central character); F1.c is theorem (Emerton--Gee arXiv:1908.07185).

In \Cref{v4-arith:adelic-descent}: \Cref{v4-arith:acd:thm:function-field-transport},
\Cref{v4-arith:acd:thm:L2-reduction}, and \Cref{v4-arith:acd:thm:F1-closure}
carry \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification, conditional on F1.a and (BC-diamond-glue).

In \Cref{v4-arith:arith-bzn}: \Cref{v4-arith:bzn:thm:main} carries
\ClaimStatusConjectured (four residuals: F1, F2, formal-loop continuity,
full Weil/\((\varphi,\Gamma)\) target).
\Cref{v4-arith:bzn:prop:EG-to-LocSys} carries
\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification;
\Cref{v4-arith:bzn:prop:EG-image-flat} carries
\ClaimStatusConjectured.

In \Cref{v4-arith:audit}: \Cref{v4-arith:sw:thm:LACD-split} and
\Cref{v4-arith:sw:thm:BZN-Ar-split} are valid logical equivalences
at \ClaimStatusProvedHere; their sub-obligation statuses carry
\ClaimStatusConjectured (with \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}.4\) a theorem
via Kneser 1966).

In \Cref{v4-arith:lacd-sub}: \Cref{v4-arith:lacd:thm:L1-closed}
tag is updated to \ClaimStatusProvedHereConditional\ (conditional on
F2.c tightened); \Cref{v4-arith:lacd:cor:LACD-residual} is annotated
to record the additional conditionality on F1.a and on
(BCglue.local)+(BCglue.glue).

In \Cref{v4-arith:bzn-sub}: R2 (\ClaimStatusProvedHere, derived
enhancement of Emerton--Gee via Lurie DAG) and R1 split
(R1.fin-type theorem for smooth finite-type, R1.formal
\ClaimStatusConjectured) are in place. Downstream F3
\ClaimStatusProvedHere for the reduction to three sub-obligations;
F3 itself remains conditional on R1.formal.b, R1.formal.c, and R4.

In \Cref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion}: R3 (\ClaimStatusProvedHere,
under explicit formal-Noetherian hypotheses); R4
(\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification, using Bezrukavnikov arXiv:1209.0403
and Etingof--Ostrik, Jantzen, Carter, Bushnell--Henniart).

In \Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5}:
\Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:rem:residual-summary} is updated to record
(BCglue.local) as \ClaimStatusNeedsVerification and (BCglue.glue) as
\ClaimStatusConjectured.
\Cref{v4-arith:bcglue-k5:cor:LACD-tight-catalog} is updated from
\ClaimStatusNeedsVerification to \ClaimStatusConjectured.

and the RH completion package \Cref{v4-arith:platonic:def:RH-package}.
