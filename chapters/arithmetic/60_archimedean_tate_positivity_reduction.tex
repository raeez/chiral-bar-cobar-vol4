\chapter{The Archimedean Tate Positivity Reduction}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:chapter}

The sufficient path of Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:rh-reformulation}
requires both categorical comparisons and spectral realization.
The conditions in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:fle-completion-table}
separate these requirements. The arithmetic
Positselski biconditional
\(\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}=0
\Leftrightarrow \xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\)
is conditional on the abelian Heisenberg inputs; the
Koszul--Petersson compatibility is restricted to its stated core; the
Hilbert--P\'{o}lya operator skeleton is constructed on its stated
domain; HP--Li positivity requires a positive realization of the full
Weil form on the compact convolution test algebra.  The
archimedean Tate positivity conjecture A-TP of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:chapter} is RH-equivalent, but it is not
the whole obstruction.
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-synth:thm:RH-equiv-ATP} records only this
conditional placement.

\subsection{Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:hp-operator}: The Hilbert--P\'{o}lya Operator Skeleton}

operator-algebraic form.

\subsection{Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter}: Zero counts, Gaussian tests and formal completion}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:ch59}

Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:thm:rvm} establishes
the sharp Riemann--von Mangoldt density with the Titchmarsh bound
\(|S(T)|=O(\log T)\) unconditionally.
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:thm:weil-gauss-closed}
gives the closed-form Weil evaluation on Gaussians.
The difference between the two sides of the explicit formula is zero.
The Weil value itself is the zero sum, which need not vanish.
In the normalization \(f_\lambda(t)=e^{-\lambda t^2/2}\), put
\[
 k(u)=\log\pi-\Re\psi_\Gamma(1/4+iu/2),\qquad
 H(\lambda)=\frac1{\sqrt{\pi\lambda}}
             \int_{\mathbb R}e^{-u^2/\lambda}k(u)\,du,
\]
where \(\psi_\Gamma=\Gamma'/\Gamma\).
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w7-d:thm:gauss-ATP} gives the exact sign range
\[
 W_\infty(f_\lambda*f_\lambda)
 =\sqrt{\frac\pi\lambda}H(\lambda)\ge0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 0<\lambda\le\lambda_c,
\]
where \(\lambda_c\) is the unique zero of \(H\).
The full arithmetic value is
\[
 W(f_\lambda*f_\lambda)
 =\frac{4\pi}{\lambda}e^{1/(4\lambda)}
  -\sqrt{\frac\pi\lambda}H(\lambda)
  -2\sqrt{\frac\pi\lambda}\sum_{n\ge2}
     \frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}e^{-\lambda(\log n)^2/4}.
\]
The gamma sign range alone does not settle the nonnegativity of this
expression or of Gaussian cross terms.

Artin--Rees identifies the induced and intrinsic adic completions
of a finite Iwasawa submodule
(Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:thm:formal-summary}).
The arithmetic-loop comparison remains conditional on preservation
of the specified colimit
(Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:thm:R1-formal-b-closes}).
The flat-subcategory comparison is
Question~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:prop:c2-closure}.

Primary sources: Riemann 1859 Monatsber.~Berlin Akad.;
von Mangoldt 1895 J.~Reine Angew.~Math.~114; Titchmarsh 1986 (2nd edn);
Selberg 1944; Weil 1952; Tate 1950; Burnol 2000; Cartier--Voros 1990;
Atiyah--Macdonald 1969; Bourbaki 1961 (Alg.~Comm.~III); Lang 1994.

\section{The Refined Sufficient Path to RH}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:refined-sufficient-path}

The sufficient path of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:rh:thm:sufficient-path} took four
inputs:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(P1)] arithmetic Positselski: the G-row biconditional
\(\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}=0
\Leftrightarrow \xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\);
\item[(KP)] Koszul--Petersson compatibility
\(\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}=\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}\)
on \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\);
\item[(HP)] Hilbert--P\'olya\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\): a self-adjoint
\(H_{\mathrm{HP}}\) with
\(\det_{\infty}(s\mathrm{id}-\Theta_{\xi})=\xi_{\mathbb{R}}(s)\)
and Weil--Connes trace distribution;
\item[(HPLi)] HP--Li positivity:
\(W(f*\widetilde{f})\geq 0\) on admissible Weil test functions.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Domains of the Four Inputs}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:per-input}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{P1 (arithmetic Positselski).} On the abelian Heisenberg
sector, the intended equivalence requires the categorical hypotheses
and the actual coefficient, completion, algebra and pairing comparisons
of Question~\ref{v4-arith:w5-d:combined:thm:closure}. Its G-row formulation
also requires the compatible identification of arithmetic obstruction

domain.
\item \textbf{KP (Koszul--Petersson compatibility).} On the selected
discrete carrier, Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity}
is conditional on the arithmetic placement, local pairing maps and
adelic extension. Maass temperedness and the spectral trace-placement
comparison are additional inputs where used. The continuous pairing
requires its own measure and normalization maps.
\item \textbf{HP (Hilbert--P\'olya).} (W) weight existence closed
unconditionally by
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:thm:N-BC-self-adjoint}: the Bost--Connes
log-modular operator supplies the zero-mode eigenvalues \(\log m\)
on the primary Fock image. (H) trace bridge remains open,
equivalent to the Connes absorption-trace condition and to the
twist-existence statement
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:hyp:twist-existence}.
\item \textbf{HPLi (HP--Li positivity).}
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:hpli-form} proves that a positive Hilbert

strictly equivalent to archimedean Tate positivity A-TP
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:ATP-equals-weil-positivity}), which is
strictly equivalent to RH.
\end{enumerate}

\section{A-TP and the Other Residuals}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:single-residual}

\begin{theorem}[A-TP is RH-equivalent on the stated path]
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:thm:RH-equiv-ATP}
\emph{Conditional statement.} Assume the reductions of Chapters~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:chapter}--\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter}:
\begin{itemize}
\item P1 on the abelian Heisenberg sector, conditional on its
Positselski inputs
(Question~\ref{v4-arith:w5-d:combined:cor:G-row-upgrade});
\item conditional KP transfer on the selected discrete-spectrum core
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity});
\item HP (W) weight existence
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:thm:N-BC-self-adjoint});
\item the positive-realization criterion for the full Weil form
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:hpli-form}), which asserts existence if and
only if that form is positive and does not assume its positivity.
\end{itemize}
For \(f\in\mathscr A=C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)\), use
\(\widetilde f=f^*\) and \(\widehat f=\mathcal F_+f\).
Then the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent to the archimedean Tate
positivity conjecture
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:eq:ATP}
W_{\infty}(f*\widetilde{f})\;\leq\;
\widehat{f*\widetilde{f}}(\tfrac{1}{2i})+
\widehat{f*\widetilde{f}}(-\tfrac{1}{2i})-
W_{\mathrm{fin}}(f*\widetilde{f}),
\end{equation}
for every \(f\in\mathscr A\)
(Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:ATP-equals-weil-positivity} proves the
biconditional A-TP \(\Leftrightarrow\) Weil positivity on admissible
Weil test functions. Weil positivity is equivalent to RH by the
classical Weil 1952 equivalence
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:cor:weil-positivity-RH}. The remaining assertions
of Chapters~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:chapter}--\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter} place this RH-equivalent inequality on the stated
sufficient path under their own hypotheses. They do not close the
non-A-TP obstructions recorded later.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Reduction to A-TP]
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:rem:honest-reduction}
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-synth:thm:RH-equiv-ATP} is a reduction, not a
proof of RH. Its content is the isolation of an archimedean
positive-trace obstruction equivalent to RH. The
arithmetic-chiral-algebra framework relocates the Hilbert--P\'{o}lya
and Weil-positivity obstructions from their classical form as an
positive-trace inequality on the compact convolution algebra to the
full inequality comparing the signed gamma form with polar and prime terms, preserving the
logical equivalence with RH.  It does not assert that the other
arithmetic comparison problems have disappeared.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[relation to A-TP, Connes, and VB\(^{*}\)]
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:rem:ATP-equivalences}
The three RH-strength formulations are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A-TP: the full signed inequality on
\(\mathscr A=C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)\)
(Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity});
\item Connes absorption-trace condition: existence of a
self-adjoint operator on the GNS space of the KMS\({}_{1}\) state
whose normal zero-divisor operator has determinant divisor
\(\mathrm{div}\,\xi_{\mathbb R}\) and Weil--Connes trace equal to the
zero distribution
(Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:hyp:twist-existence});
\item the de Branges/Hermite--Biehler, B\'aez--Duarte, and
determinant spectral-regularity conditions on \(\xi_{\mathbb{R}}\)
(Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:w5-j:cor:equivalent-forms},
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:hpvbconv:thm:VBstar-converse-spectral-regularity} under Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:hpvbconv:hyp:operator-quantitative-comparison}).
\end{enumerate}
The full Weil criterion and the classical BD closure condition are
criteria for zero placement on their complete test domains. The
sharp BD rate is a stronger quantitative request, and the
arithmetic operator formulations additionally require actual
carriers, domains and comparison maps. The conditional
VB\(^{*}\)/(Reg)/(HB+)/(BD+) comparison uses all the data of
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:hpvbconv:hyp:operator-quantitative-comparison};
it does not identify bare RH with those unconstructed data.
\end{remark}

has spectrum \(\{\log m:m\ge1\}\), by the weighted occupation
embedding of Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:lem:zero-mode-embeds-gns}.
Neither construction identifies a zeta-zero spectrum.
\item \textbf{Riemann--von Mangoldt density.} The density estimate
holds with the stated Titchmarsh bound (Ch.~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter}).
\item \textbf{Weil at Gaussians.} The exact signed Gaussian formula
holds in the stated normalization; it does not assert positivity of
the full Weil expression (Ch.~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter}).
\item \textbf{Block-additivity and formal comparisons.} The
F1.a.3.A block-additivity and generic-block Ext\({}^{1}\) results hold
on their stated block domains (Ch.~\ref{v4-arith:w5-f:F1a3-AB}). The R1.formal.b/c.2 comparisons
require an actual mapping-colimit theorem and a construction of the
compatible flat subcategory (Ch.~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Conjectural Conditions}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:residual-conjectures}

The unresolved conjectural assertions are four:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A-TP} (archimedean Tate positivity): strictly
equivalent to RH.
\item \textbf{Connes absorption trace} (twist existence):
strictly equivalent to RH.
\item \textbf{HB\(^{+}\)/\(\mathrm{EC}_\pi\)/(Det) on \(\xi_{\mathbb{R}}\)}:
HB\(^{+}\) is RH-equivalent by Lagarias 1999; \(\mathrm{EC}_\pi\) and (Det)
are spectral-regularity inputs.
\item \textbf{Maass Ramanujan conjecture}: external open problem
in the Langlands programme, independent of RH.
\end{enumerate}

The adjacent spectral assertions are:

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{4}
\item \textbf{(AG.det.transition) higher-centre gluing} on an
actual categorical descent diagram, together with the separate
arithmetic centre comparison. The local-model morphism and its
relative dimensions must be constructed before asserting a
codimension bound.
\item \textbf{(BCglue.local)} \(\ell=p\) local transition theorem
for coherent Springer sheaves.
\item \textbf{(BCglue.glue)} prismatic-flat cross-prime coherent
sheaf descent.
\end{enumerate}

The Paskunas/F1.a local categorical conditions are (R.A, R.B.1,
R.B.2, F1.a.1 non-ordinary, F1.a.2 class-match, R1.formal.c.1/c.3),
which are finite theorem conditions outside the RH-equivalent
analytic residual.

\section{Domain of Chapters~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:chapter}--\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter}}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:does-not-do}

Chapters~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:chapter}--\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter} operate within the following domain boundaries.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A-TP and RH.}
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:ATP-equals-weil-positivity} establishes the
strict equivalence A-TP \(\Leftrightarrow\) RH
(Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity}). The
arithmetic inequality retains the full compact convolution test
algebra, the polar term, and the prime term. Its form is
\(W_\infty(f*f^*)\le P(f*f^*)-W_{\mathrm{fin}}(f*f^*)\),
which preserves the logical equivalence to RH.
\item \textbf{\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) on the cuspidal sector.}
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:chapter} closes the cuspidal sector. The Eisenstein extension
requires Langlands--Shahidi intertwining theory in coherent-categorical
form, or a Mo{\oe}glin--Waldspurger residual-spectrum
categorification; these are open conditions outside the domain of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:chapter}.
\item \textbf{(BCglue.local) and (BCglue.glue).}
The transition theorem across the \(\ell\neq p\) / \(\ell=p\) divide
for coherent Springer sheaves (BCglue.local) is not in the literature.
The prismatic-flat cross-prime descent lifted to the coherent-sheaf
level (BCglue.glue) requires extensions beyond
Bhatt--Scholze 2022 and Bhatt--Lurie 2022. Both are primary-source
theorem conditions.
\item \textbf{Maass Ramanujan.}
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:full-vprim-P3} uses the Kim--Sarnak bound \(\theta \leq 7/64\) only as a
local exponent estimate. It gives no Dirichlet-summable Euler error on
the Maass sector. The arithmetic pairing maps are still required. Maass temperedness
additionally depends on the Ramanujan conjecture, an external open problem in the Langlands
programme.
\item \textbf{Hilbert--P\'{o}lya operator with zeta-zero determinant divisor.}
The full GNS generator \(N_{\mathrm{BC}}\) of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:thm:N-BC-self-adjoint} has point spectrum
\(\tfrac12\log\mathbb Q_{>0}\) and closed spectrum \(\mathbb R\).
Its reducing occupation restriction \(N_{\mathrm{occ}}\) on \(K_2\)
has spectrum \(\{\log m:m\ge1\}\), as in
Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:lem:zero-mode-embeds-gns}.
Neither construction supplies the zeta-zero determinant divisor.
The twist to an operator with determinant divisor
\(\mathrm{div}\,\xi_{\mathbb R}\) and Weil--Connes zero trace is the
Connes absorption-trace conjecture, equivalent to the
Hilbert--P\'olya/RH obstruction.
\item \textbf{\(\mathrm{HB}^{+}\), \(\mathrm{EC}_\pi\), (Det) on \(\xi_{\mathbb{R}}\).}
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-j:VBstar-spectral-positivity} proves the implication
\(\mathrm{(SH)}+\mathrm{(Det)}+\mathrm{(HB}^{+})+\mathrm{EC}_\pi
\Rightarrow \mathrm{RH}\). The proof of each of the four hypotheses on
\(\xi_{\mathbb{R}}\) lies outside the domain of Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-j:VBstar-spectral-positivity}.
\item \textbf{Remaining primary-source conditions.}
The non-RH categorical hypotheses are (AG.det.transition),
(BCglue.local), (BCglue.glue), the Eisenstein extension of
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\), the Paskunas narrow residuals, the
non-ordinary F1.a.1/F1.a.2 extensions, and the R1.formal extensions.
The analytic hypothesis A-TP is RH-equivalent and is not absorbed by
these categorical hypotheses.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{remark}[Structure of the obstruction set]
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:rem:irreducible}
Chapters~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:chapter}--\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:chapter} separate structural comparison data from analytic hardness.
The obstruction set decomposes into four classes: (i) theorems on their

\end{remark}

\section{Further Obstructions}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:path-forward}

The remaining obstructions are as follows.

\subsection{Direct Positivity for A-TP}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:direct-ATP}

A-TP compares the signed gamma contribution with both arithmetic
terms. To retain its normalization, take \(f\in\mathscr A=C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)\),
\(f^*(t)=\overline{f(-t)}\), and
\(h_f(u)=\mathcal F_+f(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(t)e^{iut}\,dt\).
The signed density and the auxiliary digamma density are respectively
\[
 k(u)=\log\pi-\Re\psi_\Gamma(1/4+iu/2),\qquad
 g(u)=\Re\psi_\Gamma(1/4+iu/2)+\log\pi.
\]
Define the auxiliary form
\[
 G(f)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}g(u)|h_f(u)|^2\,du.
\]
The plus-sign density \(g/(2\pi)\) is distinct from the signed
archimedean density \(k/(2\pi)\). Both integrals converge because
the densities have logarithmic growth and \(h_f\) decreases rapidly.

\begin{proposition}[the full inequality in the auxiliary normalization]
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:prop:auxiliary-gamma-identity}
Put
\[
 a(f)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(t)e^{-t/2}\,dt,\qquad
 b(f)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(t)e^{t/2}\,dt,
\]
and, with \(\Lambda\) the von Mangoldt function, put
\[
 Q_{\mathrm{fin}}(f)=\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
 \bigl((f*f^*)(\log n)+(f*f^*)(-\log n)\bigr).
\]
Then
\begin{align*}
 W_\infty(f*f^*)&=2\log\pi\,\|f\|_2^2-G(f),\\
 W(f*f^*)&=2\Re\bigl(a(f)\overline{b(f)}\bigr)
   -Q_{\mathrm{fin}}(f)-2\log\pi\,\|f\|_2^2+G(f).
\end{align*}
Consequently A-TP, and hence RH, is equivalent to
\[
 G(f)+2\Re\bigl(a(f)\overline{b(f)}\bigr)
 \ge Q_{\mathrm{fin}}(f)+2\log\pi\,\|f\|_2^2
 \qquad(f\in\mathscr A).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The identity \(k+g=2\log\pi\) and Plancherel in the specified
Fourier convention give the first equality.
The convolution transform is \(h_f(z)\overline{h_f(\bar z)}\).
Its evaluations at \(z=\pm i/2\) sum to
\(a(f)\overline{b(f)}+b(f)\overline{a(f)}\).
The prime sum is finite because \(f*f^*\) is compactly supported.
Substitute these evaluations into
\(W=P-W_\infty-W_{\mathrm{fin}}\) to obtain the second equality.
Nonnegativity of its right side for every \(f\in\mathscr A\) is
exactly the displayed inequality. Weil's criterion in
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:cor:weil-positivity-RH} identifies this condition
with RH.
\end{proof}

The Rankin--Selberg kernel of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:RS-integral}
and the density \(|\Gamma(1/4+iu/2)|^2\) remain possible comparison
objects. A comparison proving A-TP must establish this full inequality,
including its polar, prime, and norm terms. Positivity of an isolated
digamma or gamma-absolute-value form supplies no such identity.

\subsection{Langlands--Shahidi Extension of \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:LS-extension}

The Eisenstein/continuous-spectrum extension of
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) can be attempted via
Shahidi 2010 (AMS Colloquium 58) normalisation theory. The
required object is a categorical lift of the intertwining operators
\(M(s)\) of Moeglin--Waldspurger 1995 \S IV, realising
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) on the continuous spectrum compatible
with the Weil-positive extension. The alternative domain-restricted
comparison of Ch.~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:full-vprim-P3} supplies a
conditional discrete pairing. Its Li use requires both the stated
H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) realization and the explicit trace-placement map; the Langlands--Shahidi extension reduces the full
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) identity to the same measure
identification.

\subsection{Paskunas Wild-Prime Class Match}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:paskunas-wild}

Residuals (R.A), (R.B.1), (R.B.2) of Ch.~\ref{v4-arith:w5-f:F1a3-AB} remain open. (R.A) is
the per-block BH projective-to-closed-point matching and is
block-local over each Bernstein block. (R.B.1) is the
very-special Breuil--Mezard multiplicity. (R.B.2) is
the Paskunas block-centraliser structure theorem lifted from
\(p\geq 5\) to \(p\in\{2,3\}\). None is RH-equivalent;
each is a finite explicit computation.

\subsection{(AG.det.transition) Direct Obstruction}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:AG-det-transition}

The proposed (AG.det.transition) comparison requires an actual
arithmetic singular-support space, its categorical descent diagram,
and compatible morphisms of higher centres.
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-h:thm:agdet-reduction} supplies an internal
centre identity only after those data and the stated preservation
of the diagram are assumed. The automorphic centre comparison
requires its own morphism and equivalence proof.

A codimension bound additionally requires a local-model morphism
and its relative dimension calculation.
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w6-g:prop:codim-2} computes the regular nilpotent
orbit in $\mathfrak{sl}_2$: it has dimension two and codimension
one in the three-dimensional Lie algebra.
That calculation does not establish the dimension of an arithmetic
Selmer transition. The singular-support, cellular and geometric
centre formalisms must be connected by actual arithmetic comparison
maps before they can prove the intended global result.

\subsection{Connes Absorption-Trace Realisation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:connes-absorption}

The Connes absorption-trace conjecture
(Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:hyp:twist-existence}) is the operator-algebraic
formulation of the Hilbert--P\'olya obstruction. A direct obstruction
requires: (i) a Hilbert space \(\mathcal{H}_{\xi}\) carrying a natural
action of the BC automorphism group; (ii) an identification of the
twist data \((U,A)\) with the action of a specific Wilson-line or
intertwining operator on the archimedean adelic sector.
Consani--Marcolli 2006 supply the adele-class space framework; the
realisation of the zero-divisor operator as a twisted
\(N_{\mathrm{BC}}\) on a specific Hilbert-space completion is the
missing content.

\subsection{Hermite--Biehler Positivity on \(\xi_{\mathbb{R}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:HB-verification}

Hermite--Biehler positivity for
\(E_{\xi}(z)=A_{\xi}(z)-iB_{\xi}(z)\) is equivalent to RH by
Lagarias 1999. An obstruction requires either: (i) a direct proof that
the real part \(A_{\xi}\) has only real zeros and
\(\mathrm{div}(A_{\xi})=\mathrm{div}(\Xi)\) (de Branges 1968
Thm.~22); (ii) explicit construction of a
self-adjoint multiplication operator on \(\mathcal{H}(E_{\xi})\)
with spectrum \(\mathcal{Z}_{\xi}\cap\mathbb{R}\) (Conrey--Li 2000 Thm
2 negative direction); or (iii) a Conrey--Li-style positive
construction that avoids the Siegel-zero obstruction documented in
Conrey--Li 2000 for certain \(L\)-functions.

\section{Boundary}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:summary}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The sufficient path in
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:rh-reformulation} requires arithmetic Positselski
comparison, Koszul--Petersson compatibility, a Hilbert--P\'olya
determinant and trace realization, and HP--Li positivity. The conditions
of Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:fle-completion-table} retain their separate
categorical and spectral hypotheses.
\item The arithmetic Positselski equivalence on the abelian sector
requires the categorical hypotheses and actual comparison maps of
Question~\ref{v4-arith:w5-d:combined:thm:closure}; the selected scalar character
does not construct them.
\item The Koszul--Petersson transfer on the selected discrete carrier
requires the arithmetic placement, local pairing maps and adelic extension
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity}).
\item The Hilbert--P\'{o}lya operator skeleton is constructed via the
Bost--Connes log-modular operator
\(N_{\mathrm{BC}}\) (Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-b:thm:N-BC-self-adjoint}).
\item A positive Hilbert realization of the full Weil form exists
exactly when that form is positive
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:hpli-form}). The coefficient multiplier
\(\Upsilon_F\) supplies no such pairing identity.
\item RH is equivalent to the archimedean Tate positivity conjecture
A-TP (Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity}),
on the stated path
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-synth:thm:RH-equiv-ATP}).
\item The reductions leave no additional numerical identity hidden in
the comparison chain; the remaining content is analytic positivity or
categorical gluing.
\item A-TP is the full inequality comparing the signed gamma form with
the polar and prime terms.
\item The remaining obstructions are: direct positivity for A-TP;
Langlands--Shahidi extension of \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\);
Paskunas wild-prime class match; (AG.det.transition) sheaf-gluing;
Connes absorption-trace realisation; and the proof of
\(\mathrm{HB}^{+}\)/\(\mathrm{EC}_\pi\)/(Det) on \(\xi_{\mathbb{R}}\).
\end{enumerate}

The arithmetic branch contains a mathematically explicit RH-equivalent
archimedean statement: the archimedean Tate positivity conjecture A-TP.
It also contains the comparison residuals recorded in the obstruction
decomposition above.
