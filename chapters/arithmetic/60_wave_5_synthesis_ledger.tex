\chapter{The Archimedean Tate Positivity Reduction}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:chapter}

Chapters 49--59 closed or scope-refined every residual obligation
on the minimal path of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:rh-reformulation}, inherited from
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:fle-completion-ledger}. The arithmetic
Positselski biconditional
\(\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}=0
\Leftrightarrow \xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\)
upgrades from Theorem-waiting to Theorem on the abelian Heisenberg
sector; the Koszul--Petersson compatibility closes on the
discrete-spectrum core; the Hilbert--P\'{o}lya operator skeleton
\(H_{\mathrm{HP}}\) is constructed unconditionally; HP--Li
positivity closes on the holomorphic cuspidal restricted
Paley--Wiener class up to the archimedean correction. Every
remaining residual either closes unconditionally or reduces to a
single statement: the archimedean Tate positivity conjecture A-TP of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-a:chapter}, strictly equivalent to the
Riemann Hypothesis and the Vol~IV-native form of the Weil
positive-trace obstruction.
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-synth:thm:RH-equiv-ATP} records the
equivalence.

\begin{theorem}[RH is equivalent to A-TP]
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:thm:RH-equiv-ATP}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. Assume the closures of Chapters 49--59:
\begin{itemize}
\item P1 on the abelian Heisenberg sector
(\Cref{v4-arith:w5-d:combined:cor:G-row-upgrade});
\item KP on the discrete-spectrum core
(\Cref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity}), unconditional on
the holomorphic cuspidal and residual sectors;
\item HP (W) weight existence
(\Cref{v4-arith:w5-b:thm:N-BC-self-adjoint}), unconditional;
\item HP--Li restricted closure
(\Cref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:hpli-status}) on
\(\mathrm{PW}^{\mathrm{hol}}_{F}\) up to the archimedean correction.
\end{itemize}
Then the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent to the archimedean Tate
positivity conjecture
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:eq:ATP}
W_{\infty}(f*\widetilde{f})\;\leq\;\sum_{\rho}|\widehat{f}(\gamma_{\rho})|^{2}+\widehat{f*\widetilde{f}}(\tfrac{1}{2i})+\widehat{f*\widetilde{f}}(-\tfrac{1}{2i})-W_{\mathrm{fin}}(f*\widetilde{f}),
\end{equation}
for every \(f\in\mathrm{PW}(\mathbb{R})\)
(\Cref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\Cref{v4-arith:w5-a:thm:ATP-equals-weil-positivity} proves the
biconditional A-TP \(\Leftrightarrow\) Weil positivity on admissible
Weil test functions. Weil positivity is equivalent to RH by the
classical Weil 1952 equivalence
\Cref{v4-arith:w5-a:cor:weil-positivity-RH}. Under the closures of
Chapters 49--59 for P1, KP, HP-(W), HP--Li-restricted, every other
ingredient on the minimal path of
\Cref{v4-arith:rh:thm:minimal-path} (combined with the VB\(^{*}\)
main-direction closure \Cref{v4-arith:w5-j:thm:VBstar-main-direction})
is either closed unconditionally or reduced to a sub-statement
strictly implied by A-TP on the Li-sufficient discrete core. The
conjunction yields the equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Reduction to A-TP]
\label{v4-arith:w5-synth:rem:honest-reduction}
\Cref{v4-arith:w5-synth:thm:RH-equiv-ATP} is a reduction, not a
proof of RH. Its content is the precise isolation of the single
remaining archimedean positive-trace obstruction. The
arithmetic-chiral-algebra framework relocates the Hilbert--P\'{o}lya
and Weil-positivity obstructions from their classical form as an
opaque positive-trace inequality on the full Paley--Wiener cone to a
single archimedean Tate distributional inequality, preserving the
full logical equivalence with RH. The relocation decouples the
unconditionally closed portions of the minimal path (prime-side
positivity, discrete-spectrum Koszul--Petersson, operator skeleton,
\(\mathrm{HB}^{+}\) implication) from the single remaining
irreducible obstruction.
\end{remark}
