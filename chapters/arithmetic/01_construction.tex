\section{The Arithmetic Curve}
\label{v4-arith:construction:curve}

Write \(\overline{C}:=\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\) for the
Arakelov compactification: closed points are primes \(p\in\mathbb{P}\)
plus the archimedean place \(\infty\). At \(p\) the completed local ring
is \(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\), with uniformizer \(p\), residue field
\(\mathbb{F}_{p}\), and Frobenius
\(\mathrm{Frob}_{p}\colon\bar{\mathbb{F}}_{p}\to\bar{\mathbb{F}}_{p}\),
\(x\mapsto x^{p}\). At \(\infty\) the completion is \(\mathbb{R}\) with
complex conjugation on \(\mathbb{C}\). The generic stalk is
\(\mathbb{Q}\).

A smooth complex curve has local coordinates on open neighborhoods,
with transition maps on their overlaps. It need not have one global
coordinate. An adele
\((x_{\infty};x_{2},x_{3},x_{5},\ldots)\in\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}\)
is instead a tuple of local elements, integral at almost every finite
place. It becomes an insertion coordinate only if a geometric
construction supplies that interpretation and its transition maps.
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:coefficients:chapter} constructs continuous
arithmetic coefficient complexes without assuming such an identification.

\section{\texorpdfstring{\(\Lambda\)}{Lambda}-Ring Input}
\label{v4-arith:construction:lambda-ring}

\begin{definition}[after Grothendieck, Borger]
\label{v4-arith:def:lambda-ring}
For a \(\mathbb Z\)-torsion-free commutative ring \(R\), a
\emph{\(\Lambda\)-structure} is equivalently given by
ring endomorphisms \(\psi_{n}\colon R\to R\), \(n\in\mathbb{N}^{\times}\),
such that \(\psi_{1}=\mathrm{id}\), \(\psi_{m}\psi_{n}=\psi_{mn}\), and
\(\psi_{p}(x)\equiv x^{p}\pmod{p}\) for every prime \(p\).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Borger, arXiv:0801.1691; arXiv:0906.3146]
\label{v4-arith:thm:lambda-ring-descent}

Borger interprets the forgetful functor from \(\Lambda\)-spaces to
ordinary spaces as base change from \(\mathbb F_1\) to \(\mathbb Z\).
For flat affine objects the structure is specified by commuting
Frobenius lifts. This categorical interpretation does not construct a
new tensor product or a descent datum on an unsupplied arithmetic object
(Borger0906.3146, \S\S1.1--1.2).
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The universal \(\Lambda\)-ring on one generator is the ring
\(\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}[h_{1},h_{2},\ldots]\) of symmetric functions. On
Newton power sums the Adams operation acts by
\(\psi_{n}(p_{m})=p_{mn}\) (Macdonald, \emph{Symmetric Functions and
Hall Polynomials}, Ch.~I~\S8).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
The big Witt vector functor \(W\colon\mathrm{CRing}\to\mathrm{Ring}_{\Lambda}\)
is right adjoint to the forgetful functor. \(W(R)\) is the cofree
\(\Lambda\)-ring on \(R\). The ghost map \(W(R)\to R^{\mathbb{N}^{\times}}\) is injective
when \(R\) is \(\mathbb Z\)-torsion-free; injectivity is not asserted
for arbitrary torsion rings (Borger0801.1691, \S1.14).
\end{remark}

On \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\), local translations are replaced by a
monoid \(\mathbb{N}^{\times}\) of commuting Adams operations \(\{\psi_p\}_{p\in\mathbb{P}}\),
one per prime. An action on a proposed arithmetic chiral object is additional data.
The polynomial index-shift action and the different divisor substitutions
are constructed in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:gelfand-borger}; neither alone
supplies an action on the arithmetic trace class.

\section{The Finite Heisenberg Cover}
\label{v4-arith:construction:primon-va}

\begin{definition}[finite Heisenberg cover]
\label{v4-arith:def:primon-va-naive}
For each prime \(p\), let \(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) be the rank-\(2\)
Heisenberg vertex algebra with generators \(a^{(p)},b^{(p)}\) and OPE
\begin{align}
a^{(p)}(z)\,a^{(p)}(w)\;&\sim\;\frac{1}{(z-w)^{2}}, \\
b^{(p)}(z)\,b^{(p)}(w)\;&\sim\;\frac{1}{(z-w)^{2}}, \\
a^{(p)}(z)\,b^{(q)}(w)\;&\sim\;0\quad\forall p,q.
\end{align}
Each orthogonal current has level one. Use the ordinary rank-two
conformal vector, of central charge two,
\[
 \omega_p=\tfrac12\bigl((a^{(p)}_{-1})^2+(b^{(p)}_{-1})^2\bigr)|0\rangle_p.
\]
Its integer oscillator weight is distinct from the energy
\(H_p=(\log p)L_0^{(p)}\). The latter is an operator, not a rescaled
conformal vector. The algebraic restricted tensor product
\(\bigotimes_p'\mathcal H^{(p)}\) uses the vacuum at almost every prime.
It is a proposed cover; a map from it to the arithmetic trace class
is additional data. An infinite sum of its conformal vectors is not a
state in this algebraic restricted tensor product.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[full and selected finite-place Euler factors]
\label{v4-arith:prop:character-match-naive}
On the Hall Hilbert completion at one prime \(p\), let \(Q_p\) act on a monomial by the number of
\(a\)-oscillators minus the number of \(b\)-oscillators; write
\(N_p=Q_p\) when this occupation charge is used below. For real
\(\theta_p\) and \(\operatorname{Re}s>0\),
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(e^{-sH_p}e^{i\theta_pQ_p})
 =\prod_{k\ge1}
 \frac1{(1-e^{i\theta_p}p^{-ks})(1-e^{-i\theta_p}p^{-ks})}.
\]
Selecting only the two modes \(a_{-1}^{(p)},b_{-1}^{(p)}\) gives
\[
 \frac1{1-2\cos\theta_p\,p^{-s}+p^{-2s}}.
\]
The product over all primes converges absolutely for
\(\operatorname{Re}s>1\). The selected factor agrees with the
trigonometric primon Euler factor; it is not the full two-species
oscillator trace. The selected space is not preserved by the
substitutions of Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:gelfand-borger} for \(n>1\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If the occupations are \(r_k,t_k\), their energy is
\(\sum_k k(r_k+t_k)\log p\) and their charge is
\(\sum_k(r_k-t_k)\). Summing each occupation independently gives the
geometric factors. Absolute convergence follows from the same geometric
bounds as in Section~\ref{v4-arith:ado:hilbert}; two species only double the
bound on the logarithm. Keeping only \(k=1\) gives the second formula.
In the vacuum Heisenberg module the zero modes \(a_0,b_0\) vanish;
\(Q_p\) is the explicitly defined occupation operator, not their
difference. The first generator maps to mode \(n\) under both
substitutions, proving the final assertion.
\end{proof}

\section{The Divisor-Substitution Obstruction to \(GL_2\)-Hecke}
\label{v4-arith:construction:hecke-adams-naive}

\begin{proposition}[divisor substitutions and Hecke recursion]
\label{v4-arith:claim:hecke-adams-naive}
On the polynomial Fock carrier over \(\mathbb Z\), the formula
\[
 T_n(a^{(p)}_{-k})=\sum_{d\mid n,\ d\mid k}d\,a^{(p)}_{-nk/d}
\]
extends as a unital ring map. It satisfies coprime multiplicativity,
but not \(T_mT_n=T_{mn}\) for all indices, and not the recursion
\(T_{p^2}=T_p^2-p\,\mathrm{id}\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The divisor \(d=1\) always contributes. Consequently
\[
 T_p a^{(p)}_{-1}=a^{(p)}_{-p},\quad
 T_p^2a^{(p)}_{-1}=a^{(p)}_{-p^2}+p a^{(p)}_{-p},\quad
 T_{p^2}a^{(p)}_{-1}=a^{(p)}_{-p^2}.
\]
The Adams discrepancy is \(p a^{(p)}_{-p}\); the displayed Hecke
recursion has discrepancy \(p(a^{(p)}_{-p}-a^{(p)}_{-1})\).
Both are nonzero in this free integral carrier. The coprime statement
and the complete composition law are
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:ado:global-composition}. The index-shift operations
\(\psi_n(a^{(p)}_{-k})=a^{(p)}_{-nk}\) instead form an Adams action.
A comparison with an arithmetic Hecke representation must specify its
operators and preserve their actual relations.
\end{proof}

\section{Polynomial Averaging and the Arithmetic Comparison}
\label{v4-arith:construction:averaging-naive}

\begin{definition}[finite occupation-phase averaging]
\label{v4-arith:def:averaging-naive}
Let \(S\) be a finite set of primes, and let \(P_{S,\mathbb C}\) be
the polynomial span of the two-species oscillator states at those
primes. The occupation charges \(N_p=Q_p\) defined above act
diagonally on this carrier. Define the complex-linear operator
\begin{equation}
 A_S(v)=\int_{[0,\pi]^S}
 \exp\!\left(i\sum_{p\in S}\theta_pN_p\right)v\,
 \prod_{p\in S}\nu_p(\theta_p)\,d\theta_p,
 \qquad v\in P_{S,\mathbb C},
\end{equation}
where
\[
 \nu_p(\theta)=\frac{2}{\pi}
 \frac{p(1+p)\sin^2\theta}{1+p^2-2p\cos2\theta}.
\]
The integral is defined coefficient by coefficient: each polynomial
has finite support in the occupation basis, and the displayed density
is bounded on \([0,\pi]\) for every prime \(p\). Thus \(A_S\)
is an endomorphism of the polynomial vector space. No quotient or
arithmetic factorization structure is used in its definition.
\end{definition}

\begin{warning}[arithmetic averaging requires a comparison]
\label{v4-arith:warning:averaging-is-plancherel}
The proposed arithmetic map
\[
 \mathrm{av}^{\mathrm{arith}}:
 \mathcal V^{E_1,\mathrm{arith}}\longrightarrow
 \mathcal V^{\mathrm{Hecke}}
\]
requires the construction of its arithmetic \(E_1\)-chiral carrier,
its Hecke quotient, and the map between them. These are additional
requirements in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:costello-gwilliam}.
The formula for \(A_S\) does not identify those carriers or construct
that arithmetic map. Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:radial:transform} constructs the spherical
transform on radial tree functions and preserves its root state.
Section~\ref{v4-arith:radial:finite} proves that the one-prime
occupation-phase average has a nonreal eigenvalue. Thus it cannot
be unitarily intertwined with the self-adjoint tree adjacency operator. The operadic
averaging from ordered to symmetric chiral cohomology in Vol~I has
its own carriers and structure maps; the shared integral notation
supplies no comparison between these operations.
\end{warning}

\section{The Depth Analogy}
\label{v4-arith:construction:archetypes-heuristic}

\emph{Heuristic.} The following four rows name possible
arithmetic shadows of the Vol~I depth filtration. They do not classify
\(\Lambda\)-rings.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Depth \(2\):} trivial \(\psi_{n}\) beyond \(\psi_{1}\);
\emph{archetype G analogue} (primon Heisenberg only);
\item \textbf{Depth \(3\):} finitely generated image in
\(\prod_{p}\mathbb{Z}\); \emph{archetype L analogue} (arithmetic
Kac--Moody / Iwasawa-type \(\Lambda\)-module);
\item \textbf{Depth \(4\):} higher-derived \(\Lambda\)-structure
engaging Witt filtration; \emph{archetype C analogue}
(arithmetic \(\beta\gamma\));
\item \textbf{Depth \(\infty\):} full Connes--Consani arithmetic site;
\emph{archetype M analogue} (arithmetic Virasoro).
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}
The depth numerology \(\{2,3,4,\infty\}\) on the Vol~I side is forced
by explicit shadow-tower computation (Vol~I, shadow-tower chapter). On
the arithmetic side the corresponding derivation via a
\(\Lambda\)-ring analogue of Mok's shadow-tower rigidity remains open; the classification
above is a heuristic pattern match.
\end{remark}

\section{Boundary Conditions for the Five Arithmetic Analogues}
\label{v4-arith:construction:theorems-naive}

\begin{conjecture}[\(A^{\mathrm{arith}}\)]
\label{v4-arith:conj:A-naive}
There is an adjunction
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{ch,arith}}\dashv B^{\mathrm{ch,arith}}\) between
the \(\infty\)-category of \(\Lambda\)-ring-enhanced \(E_{1}\)-algebras
on \(\overline{C}\) and the \(\infty\)-category of conilpotent
\(\Lambda\)-coalgebras. On \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) this
adjunction is an equivalence on the subcategory of \(\Lambda\)-ring
objects with prime-adic convergence at every finite place.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{question}[\(B^{\mathrm{arith}}\)]
\label{v4-arith:conj:B-naive}
Construct the arithmetic adjunction of
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:conj:A-naive} and determine its domain of
coalgebra recovery. For a coaugmented coalgebra \(C\), the required
unit has the type
\[
 C\longrightarrow
 B^{\mathrm{ch,arith}}\Omega^{\mathrm{ch,arith}}(C).
\]
Specify the coefficient topology and weak equivalences under which
this map is an equivalence. Conilpotence concerns iterated reduced
coproducts. A module-theoretic \(\mu=0\) hypothesis, when defined,
is separate, by Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:coefficients:prop:mu}.
\end{question}

\begin{conjecture}[\(C^{\mathrm{arith}}\)]
\label{v4-arith:conj:C-naive}
Define the arithmetic \(\kappa\) of a \(\Lambda\)-chiral algebra as
its Arakelov first Chern class (sum of finite-place \(p\)-adic
orders plus archimedean logarithmic defect). On the Koszul
self-dual arithmetic subcategory,
\(\kappa+\kappa^{!}\in\{0,\log 2,\log 3,\ldots\}\cup\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\)
with the precise ceiling unknown.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{warning}[Sharpened in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:closure}]
The correct arithmetic \(\mathsf{G}\)-row ceiling is \(0\), and its
content is the Riemann functional equation
\(\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\).
\end{warning}

\begin{conjecture}[\(D^{\mathrm{arith}}\)]
\label{v4-arith:conj:D-naive}
The genus-\(g\) obstruction for the arithmetic Maurer--Cartan element
\(\Theta_{A}^{\mathrm{arith}}\) equals \(\kappa\cdot h_{g}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\)
where \(h_{g}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) is the Arakelov height of the level-\(g\)
component of \(\overline{M}_{g,n}\).
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[\(H^{\mathrm{arith}}\)]
\label{v4-arith:conj:H-naive}
\(\mathrm{HH}^{\bullet}(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}})\) is concentrated
in degrees \(\{0,1,2\}\):
\begin{align}
\mathrm{HH}^{0}&\;=\;\mathbb{Z}\quad(\Lambda\text{-ring augmentation}), \\
\mathrm{HH}^{1}&\;=\;\bigoplus_{p}\log p\cdot\mathbb{Z}\quad(\text{Bost--Connes KMS generators}), \\
\mathrm{HH}^{2}&\;=\;\text{a }\Lambda\text{-torsor related to Witt cotangent}.
\end{align}
\end{conjecture}

\section{Boundary Conditions}
\label{v4-arith:construction:open-technical}

Four boundary conditions govern the construction above.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{\(p\)-adic locality.} The OPE locality axiom \([a(z),b(w)]=0\)
off-diagonal requires an interpretation of the formal variable \(z-w\)
at finite places of \(\overline{C}\): see
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:drinfeld-kapranov}.
\item \textbf{Adelic factorization.} Beilinson--Drinfeld
factorization on a curve employs \(C^{n}/\mathfrak{S}_{n}\). On
\(\overline{C}\) the two candidate analogues are
\(W_{n}(\mathbb{Z})/\mathfrak{S}_{n}\) and
\(\mathrm{Spec}(\Lambda^{\otimes n})^{\mathfrak{S}_{n}}\); their
comparison is settled in
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:costello-gwilliam}.
\item \textbf{Real place.} The archimedean place carries an
\(\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{R})\) conformal quantum mechanics
(Hartnoll--Yang); the non-archimedean places carry
\(\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{Q}_{p})\). The adelic
\(\mathrm{SL}(2)\)-automorphic picture gluing these is developed in
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:modular-functor}.
\item \textbf{Four-archetype derivation.} The shadow-tower depth
numerology on the arithmetic side would require a \(\Lambda\)-ring
analogue of Mok's shadow-tower rigidity. No such rigidity theorem is
used below.
\end{enumerate}
