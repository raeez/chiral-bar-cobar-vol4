\chapter{Arithmetic Comparison Hypotheses}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:chapter}

The local, trace and spectral constructions contribute to the G-row
biconditional
\[
\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}
  +(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\xi(s)=\xi(1-s).
\]
The comparisons require compatible sign normalizations, ambient
categories, hypotheses and functorial maps. The three groups of conditional
inputs described below retain their stated assumptions; the other
statements retain the hypotheses of their respective chapters.

\section{The Eleven Local Comparisons}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:local-comparisons}

\subsection{F1.a.1, F1.a.2, and \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch49}

The Taylor--Wiles ordinary comparison uses Clozel--Harris--Taylor,
Kisin, Gee--Kisin, Emerton--Gee, and Hida on the derivation side, and
Dotto--Emerton--Gee together with Fargues--Scholze on the categorical
side. Berger--Colmez fixes the
Hodge--Tate convention, and the block-diagonal Hecke action preserves
compactly generated presentable stable \(\infty\)-categories.  The
non-ordinary extension, the non-semisimple class comparison, and the
Eisenstein extension remain the named conditional statements
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:conj:f1a1-full},
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conj:Paskunas-wild-ext}, and
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:conj:LACD-Eisenstein}.

The Dotto--Emerton--Gee input is used only in its fixed-central-character
\(p\geq5\) fully faithful form.  No essential-surjectivity theorem and
no global variation of the central character is imported.

\subsection{F1.a.3 (A+B)}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch50}

The block-additivity argument is an \(\infty\)-categorical statement in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{L}_{st}\).  Bernstein, Bushnell--Henniart, Paskunas,
Ben-Zvi--Francis--Nadler, and Lurie supply inputs for the block
decomposition and the tensor formalism.  The co-Hochschild convention is
the one of HA~5.5.3 and is compatible with Costello--Gwilliam.

The ordinary block is controlled only after the stated deformation
hypotheses.  The wild-prime centraliser and per-block residuals are not
deduced from Paskunas alone; they are precisely the narrower conjectural
pieces named in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-f:F1a3-AB}.

\subsection{F1.a.3.C}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch51}

Emerton--Gee gives the ambient Noetherian formal stack.  It does not by
itself give a finite nilpotent stratification, wild-prime quiver local
models, or residual-gerbe generators.  The compact-generation theorem is
therefore conditional on the local-model input stated in
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-g:f1a3-C:thm:main}.  Under that input, recollement and
compact-generation are formal consequences of
Beilinson--Bernstein--Deligne, Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum, and
Arinkin--Gaitsgory.

The equality \(2=2\) in the \(p=3\) block is a tangent-dimension
comparison.  It is not a class-level identification of the two gerbe
directions.  The class-level statement belongs to the Paskunas--wild
comparison and is not smuggled into the compact-generation proof.

\subsection{Chenevier and \(F2.c\)}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch52}

Chenevier's determinant formalism constructs complete local deformation
rings and the restriction homomorphism of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-h:thm:ch1-closure}.
Using this diagram for arithmetic gluing still requires the Selmer-ring
identification, geometric realization, trace compatibility and ordinary
equivariant sheaf comparison of
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:w5-h:cor:ch1-closes-pairwise}.
The derived comparison requires its own datum or both external-product
and pullback Tor-independence hypotheses.

The $(AG.det)$ problem separately requires the arithmetic derived stack,
its conical singular-support condition, and an actual categorical gluing
diagram. The affine-line and triangular-algebra models of
Section~\ref{v4-arith:w5-h:agdet} prove particular gluing statements
and a higher-centre restriction obstruction; they do not construct that
arithmetic geometry. The higher-centre maps and their compatibility with
the chosen homotopy limit must then be supplied, followed by the global
automorphic comparison. The internal centre identity in
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-h:thm:agdet-reduction} is conditional on
these spectral data and does not supply the global comparison.

The $F2.c$ transport theorem retains its central idempotent pointed-unit
hypotheses and does not require perfectoid descent. Prismatic and
pro-\'{e}tale comparisons remain separate inputs to the arithmetic
realization, with their coefficient and geometric hypotheses.

\subsection{The Elementary Closure}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch53}

The five elementary constructions of Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-direct:closure-path}
are consequences of the earlier chapters and of the explicit formula.
They do not add a new equivalence to RH.  The only RH-equivalent
content is the Weil positivity inequality already isolated as
A-TP.  The elementary chapter is therefore a normal form for the
implication chain, not an independent proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.

\subsection{Arithmetic Positselski}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch54}

Pontryagin duality, Greenlees--May duality, Matlis duality, and the
Bost--Connes/Consani--Marcolli formalism close the four abelian
Positselski subitems at the stated domain.  The upgrade of the abelian
Heisenberg G-row is conditional on the precise adele-class/cobar-sheaf
identification in Consani--Marcolli.  Without that primary-source
identification the result is a conditional theorem, not a theorem in
the absolute sense.

\subsection{Koszul--Petersson}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch55}

A specified injective arithmetic placement into the discrete
\(L^2\) space pulls back its positive Petersson form. If the
arithmetic Koszul form is identified with that pullback, P3 follows
without a Ramanujan hypothesis, by
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:positive-pullback}.
The separate route through the tempered local models requires
temperedness, and on the Maass sector uses the stated additional
Ramanujan input. Both routes retain the actual arithmetic pairing
maps. The Eisenstein continuum has its separate measure and
form-comparison problem.

\subsection{H-P\({}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch56}

The GNS construction supplies the Hilbert-space skeleton.  It does not
construct the Hilbert--Polya operator.  The twist-existence condition is
the classical zero-line obstruction in operator form.  Thus the chapter
separates construction of the representation from positivity of the
spectral measure.

\subsection{HP--Li Positivity}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch57}

The Mellin--Rankin--Selberg isometry reduces the restricted Li
positivity statement to the archimedean Tate distribution.  The
archimedean Tate positivity statement is equivalent to RH and is not
proved there.  The chapter identifies the exact positivity functional
and does not replace Weil positivity by positivity on a smaller test
class.

\subsection{VB\({}^{*}\)}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch58}

The strict derivative-kernel criterion concerns zero placement.
The positive moment functional and determinacy concern a different
construction. Exponential completeness \(\mathrm{EC}_\pi\) gives
uniqueness in \(PW_\pi\); transfer to a de Branges space requires
the specified evaluation-compatible map, with additional bounds for
stable sampling. These comparisons remain distinct from the sharp
fractional-part rate (BD+).

\subsection{\(\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{analytic}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:ch59}

The finite-cutoff moment calculation in
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-i:prop:R-analytic-density} proves that
positive-power zero sums diverge. Gaussian evaluation of the explicit
formula does not construct their regularization or prove Weil
positivity. The analytic moment task is to construct a finite positive
functional and prove a sufficient determinacy estimate, such as linear
even-moment root growth, together with the arithmetic operator and
support comparison.

\section{Three Groups of Conditional Inputs}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:conditional-points}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The wild-prime compact-generation theorem of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-g:f1a3-C} is conditional on finite nilpotent
local models and class-level residual-gerbe generators.  Emerton--Gee
alone supplies the ambient formal stack.

\item The arithmetic gluing comparisons of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-h:bcglue-core-and-F2c} retain distinct inputs:
the Selmer-ring and trace comparisons and equivariant sheaf descent;
the additional derived comparison or its two Tor-independence conditions;
the arithmetic support geometry and categorical descent diagram;
the higher-centre maps with their homotopy-limit compatibility; and the
global automorphic comparison. Formal restriction, the coherent gluing
models and the ordinary scalar-centre constructions supply none of these
arithmetic identifications by themselves.

\item The abelian Heisenberg G-row upgrade of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w5-d:chapter} is conditional on the exact
Consani--Marcolli adele-class/cobar-sheaf comparison used there.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{v4-arith:w6-j:summary}

These groups of conditional comparison hypotheses qualify the reductions
of F1, of
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\), of the abelian Positselski sector, and of
the discrete Koszul--Petersson identity.  The RH-equivalent assertion is
not hidden in any of these reductions; it remains the archimedean Tate
positivity, equivalently Weil positivity.
