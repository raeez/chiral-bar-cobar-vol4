\chapter{Full \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) P3 and the Koszul--Petersson Compatibility on the Discrete Core}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:full-vprim-P3}

The prime Gibbs model of Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns-full}
constructs a state, its Hilbert--Schmidt GNS space, closed modular
operators and an actual comparison with a global state's prime
cyclic subspace. The Koszul--Petersson problem additionally asks
for the arithmetic placement and pairing maps of
Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}.
This chapter describes conditional transport across the cuspidal
sectors once those maps are supplied. Classical temperedness
results identify some of the relevant domains; they do not prove
the pairing comparison. The full arithmetic comparison remains
Question~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:question:arithmetic-pairing}.

\section{Tempered Cuspidal Pairing Comparison}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:recap-tempered}

Throughout the pairing consequences below, the notation
\(\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}\) means the pullback
\(\langle\iota v,\iota w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}\) along the specified
injective arithmetic placement. No placement is inferred from a
spectral decomposition of the automorphic target.

\begin{theorem}[conditional tempered cuspidal comparison]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:tempered-recap}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing},
including all local ramified comparisons and the adelic extension,
on the chosen tempered cuspidal arithmetic domain
\(\mathcal V^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,temp}}\). Then
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:tempered-recap}
 \langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}
 =\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}
\end{equation}
on that domain. Temperedness alone is not the comparison hypothesis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The local maps identify the pairings with the same normalized
Hilbert forms. Their identities multiply on elementary restricted
tensors and extend by the explicitly assumed continuity or
regularization, as proved in
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:thm:ramified-cuspidal-tempered-extension}.
The prime cyclic unitary in
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:thm:KMS1-GNS-BC-identification}
intertwines only the specified prime algebra and state. It does
not replace the arithmetic or Whittaker comparison maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[temperedness and modular domains]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:baseline}
Temperedness is a representation-theoretic condition on the
selected automorphic sector. It is not a boundedness hypothesis
for modular theory. In the actual one-prime Gibbs GNS model,
\(\Delta E_{ij}=p^{-\beta(i-j)}E_{ij}\), and both \(\Delta\) and
its inverse are unbounded; their maximal domains are given in
Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:lem:Delta}.
The GNS space is \(\operatorname{HS}(\ell^2(\mathbb N_0))\).
The proved unitary reaches a prime cyclic subspace, without
identifying the full critical GNS representation, the phase
operators or a Petersson space with an occupation tensor product.
\end{remark}

\section{Non-Tempered Cuspidal Representations: Deligne and Kim--Shahidi}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:non-tempered}

\subsection{The Non-Tempered Locus}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:non-tempered:locus}

Cuspidal automorphic representations of \(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\) decompose
as \(\pi=\bigotimes'_{v}\pi_{v}\), with archimedean component \(\pi_{\infty}\)
of one of three types: a holomorphic discrete series \(D_{k}\) of
weight \(k\geq 2\); a Maass cusp form with Laplace eigenvalue
\(\lambda=1/4+r^{2}\) (\(r\in\mathbb{R}\) tempered, \(r\in i\mathbb{R}\)
non-tempered); or a character twist. Finite places carry Satake
parameters \((\alpha_{p},\beta_{p})\) with \(\alpha_{p}\beta_{p}=1\)
(unitarity) and \(\max(|\alpha_{p}|,|\beta_{p}|)=p^{\theta_{p}}\).
Temperedness is the condition \(\theta_{p}=0\) at all \(p\); the
Ramanujan conjecture asserts temperedness for all cuspidal \(\pi\).

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Holomorphic sector.} \(\pi_{\infty}=D_{k}\) for
\(k\geq 2\). Deligne 1974 \emph{Publ.~IHES}~43, La conjecture de Weil
I, gives the Ramanujan bound unconditionally: \(|a_{p}|\leq 2 p^{(k-1)/2}\)
for the Hecke eigenvalue \(a_{p}\) of a holomorphic weight-\(k\) newform,
which is temperedness
\(|\alpha_{p}|=|\beta_{p}|=p^{(k-1)/2}\cdot 1\) after normalisation
(in the analytic normalisation \(\pi\) unitary,
\(|\alpha_{p}|=|\beta_{p}|=1\)). Weight-\(1\) holomorphic forms are
covered by Deligne--Serre 1974 \emph{Ann.~Sci.~ENS.~7}, via the
Artin conjecture for two-dimensional odd Galois representations
attached to weight-\(1\) newforms.
\item \textbf{Maass sector.} \(\pi_{\infty}\) is a Maass cusp form with
complementary-series parameter \(r=it\), \(0<t\leq 1/2\), at \(\infty\);
temperedness at \(\infty\) is \(t=0\). At finite places, non-temperedness
is \(\theta_{p}>0\). The Ramanujan conjecture for Maass forms is open:
the best current bound is Kim--Sarnak 2003 Duke~Math.~J.~119
\(\theta_{p}\leq 7/64\), improving earlier Luo--Rudnick--Sarnak 1995
and Kim--Shahidi 2002 \emph{Ann.~Math.}~155, which used the
symmetric-square and symmetric-fourth-power lifts to bound
\(\theta_{p}\leq 1/9\) via Jacquet--Piatetski-Shapiro--Shalika~1983.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Holomorphic Temperedness and the Pairing Comparison}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:non-tempered:holomorphic}

\begin{theorem}[conditional Koszul--Petersson comparison on the holomorphic sector]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:holomorphic-cuspidal}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
Let \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,hol}}\subset\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\)
denote the subspace spanned by holomorphic cuspidal automorphic
representations of \(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\), namely those \(\pi\) with
\(\pi_{\infty}=D_{k}\) for some \(k\geq 1\). For all
\(v,w\in\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,hol}}\),
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:holomorphic}
\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}
\;=\;\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}.
\end{equation}
The identification is conditional on the arithmetic comparison maps.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Deligne 1974 (weight \(k\geq 2\)) and Deligne--Serre 1974
(weight \(k=1\)), every holomorphic cuspidal representation of
\(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\) is tempered at every finite place:
\(|\alpha_{p}|=|\beta_{p}|=1\) at each \(p\). Temperedness at \(\infty\)
for \(\pi_{\infty}=D_{k}\) is automatic (discrete-series
representations are tempered by Harish-Chandra's definition).
The holomorphic cuspidal sector is therefore a subsector of the
tempered cuspidal sector:
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,hol}}\subset\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,temp}}\).
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:tempered-recap} gives
(\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:holomorphic}) under its arithmetic comparison
hypotheses. Deligne's bound supplies temperedness, not those maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[domain of Deligne 1974: holomorphic sector only]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:deligne}
Deligne 1974 establishes the Ramanujan--Petersson bound for the
Hecke eigenvalues of a holomorphic weight-\(k\) cusp form via the
Riemann hypothesis for \(\ell\)-adic cohomology of a suitable
Kuga--Sato variety. The bound transfers to temperedness of the
associated automorphic representation at every finite prime.
Deligne 1974 applies to holomorphic cuspidal forms; for Maass cusp
forms, no algebraic variety whose Frobenius eigenvalues give the
Hecke eigenvalues is available, and the Ramanujan bound in the Maass
sector is an open problem.
The current state of the art on the Maass sector is Kim--Sarnak
2003 \(\theta\leq 7/64\); this is a sub-convex Ramanujan bound, strictly
weaker than full temperedness.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The Maass Sector: Conditional on Ramanujan}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:non-tempered:maass}

\begin{theorem}[Koszul--Petersson on the Maass cuspidal sector, conditional]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:maass-cuspidal}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
\emph{(on the Ramanujan bound for Maass cusp
forms of \(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\))}. Let
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,Maass}}\subset\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\)
denote the subspace spanned by Maass cuspidal automorphic
representations of \(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\). Assume the Ramanujan bound
\(\theta_{p}=0\) at every finite prime, and assume archimedean
temperedness for every Maass form in the chosen domain. For
all \(v,w\in\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,Maass}}\),
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:maass}
\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}
\;=\;\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Under the stated finite-place and archimedean temperedness assumptions, \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,Maass}}\subset\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,temp}}\);
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:tempered-recap} then applies.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[local error bounds and adelic summability]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:KS-partial}
The Satake exponent bound \(\theta_p\leq7/64\) gives
\(|\alpha_p|,|\beta_p|\leq p^{7/64}\) in the unitary unramified
normalization. An estimate of the arithmetic pairing discrepancy
such as
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:KS-local}
\left|\langle v_p,w_p\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}_p
-\langle v_p,\sigma_p(w_p)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}_p\right|
\leq C_p p^{-25/64}
\end{equation}
requires separate control of the actual comparison maps and their
local norms. Even a uniform bound on \(C_p\) would not make this
majorant summable over all primes.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first inequality restates the exponent bound. The series
\(\sum_p p^{-25/64}\) diverges, since \(p^{-25/64}\geq p^{-1}\)
and the prime harmonic series diverges. Thus this proposed
majorant cannot justify absolute adelic summability. No estimate
of the displayed pairing difference is deduced from the Satake
bound alone.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[the selected residual sector]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:CAP-residual}
A chosen residual line with a normalized vector gives a direct
one-dimensional pairing calculation once its arithmetic placement
and Koszul normalization have been specified. This does not
identify every residual automorphic representation with one Fock
vacuum. The full discrete comparison requires a placement and
orthogonal pairing decomposition for every included residual
summand, in addition to the cuspidal comparisons.
\end{remark}

\section{The Eisenstein Density Mismatch and Domain Restriction}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eisenstein}

\subsection{The Mismatch}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eisenstein:mismatch}

We recall the mismatch identified in Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns-full}:

\begin{proposition}[the fixed scalar continuum models]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:mismatch}
On the domain \(U\) of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:lem:maass-selberg}, the proposed
scalar coefficients, with \(d(s)=|\xi(2s)|^{-2}>0\), are
\begin{align}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:Pet-density}
 P(s)&=(1+|M(s)|^2)|\xi(2s)|^{-2}=2d(s),\\
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:Koszul-density}
 K(s)&=\frac{\xi(2s)}{\xi(2s-1)}|\xi(2s)|^{-2}=M(s)^{-1}d(s).
\end{align}
They satisfy \(d(s)\leq|P(s)-K(s)|\leq3d(s)\).
Their realization as coefficients of actual continuous pairings is
an additional construction, not part of this scalar calculation.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
This is Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:thm:eisenstein-discrepancy},
using the functional equation, conjugation and triangle inequalities.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Domain Restriction}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eisenstein:domain}

\begin{definition}[discrete-spectrum core of \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\)]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:def:discrete-core}
Fix the placement of Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:prop:Vprim-placement}
and a specified residual line with normalized vector. The
\emph{selected discrete-spectrum core} is
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:discrete-core-def}
\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\;:=\;\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp}}\;\oplus\;\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{res}},
\end{equation}
the chosen arithmetic carrier placed inside
\(L^{2}_{\mathrm{disc}}([GL_{2}])=L^{2}_{\mathrm{cusp}}\oplus L^{2}_{\mathrm{res}}\)
under the placement map
\(\iota\) of Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:prop:Vprim-placement}.
No full arithmetic placement into a continuous automorphic
spectrum is inferred from this chosen discrete placement.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Koszul--Petersson identity on the discrete-spectrum core]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
\emph{(conditional on the arithmetic comparison maps on every sector;
with the additional Ramanujan assumption on the Maass sector)}. For all
\(v,w\in\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\),
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:discrete-core-identity}
\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}
\;=\;\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}.
\end{equation}
The identification holds:
\begin{itemize}
\item on
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,temp}}\) under the comparison hypotheses
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:tempered-recap});
\item on
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,hol}}\subset\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,temp}}\)
under the comparison hypotheses
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:holomorphic-cuspidal});
\item on
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,Maass}}\) conditional on
Ramanujan
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:maass-cuspidal});
\item on
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{res}}\) (one-dimensional,
vacuum-generated) by direct calculation at the Fock vacuum.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}=\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp}}\oplus\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{res}}\)
decomposes orthogonally under the adelic inner product (Langlands 1966
\S6; Moeglin--Waldspurger Ch.~II). On
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp}}\), split by archimedean
type:
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp}}=\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,hol}}\oplus\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,Maass}}\)
(Gelfand--Fomin--Piatetski-Shapiro 1969 \emph{Representation Theory
and Automorphic Functions} Ch.~II). The holomorphic piece is closed
by Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:holomorphic-cuspidal} under the comparison hypotheses;
the Maass piece by
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:maass-cuspidal} under Ramanujan.
For the specified residual line, let \(v_0\) be its chosen unit
vector and assume the arithmetic comparison sends its normalized
vacuum to \(v_0\). Then
\(\langle av_0,bv_0\rangle=a\overline b\), and the stipulated
Koszul normalization gives the same value. This calculation uses
the unit-vector datum, not an assertion that the whole automorphic
quotient has volume one.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[discrete and continuous domains]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:eisenstein-placement}
The conditional identity
\eqref{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:discrete-core-identity} concerns the selected
discrete carrier with its assumed placement. It makes no assertion
about the continuous automorphic spectrum. The scalar models
\(P\) and \(K\) above differ in the fixed convention; identifying
them with actual pairings and constructing a measure transport
remain separate continuous-spectrum requirements.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Sufficiency for Li's Criterion}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eisenstein:Li}

\begin{proposition}[domain restriction for the Li trace]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li}
\emph{(conditional on the
H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) determinant-trace realisation and on the trace map placing
Li's zero sum in the discrete sector)}. Li's coefficients
\(\lambda_{n}=\sum_{\rho}(1-(1-1/\rho)^{n})\)
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:rh:thm:Li-criterion}) sum over the discrete
non-trivial zeros of \(\zeta\). The non-trivial zeros of \(\zeta\) contributing to the Li sum are
exactly the zeros of \(\xi_{\mathbb{R}}\), all of which are discrete;
the Eisenstein continuous spectrum contributes no term once the
H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) trace has been identified with this zero sum.
Eisenstein-series poles in \(\xi\) at \(s=0,1\) are Tate poles
(Remark~\ref{v4-arith:rh:rem:trivial-zero}), cancelled by the factor
\(s(s-1)/2\) in
\(\xi_{\mathbb R}=(1/2)s(s-1)\Lambda_{\zeta}\). The Hilbert--P\'olya
operator \(\Theta_{\xi}\) of Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:rh:hyp:HPAr}, if it exists,
has regularised determinant divisor equal to the non-trivial zero
divisor, whose ordinate multiset is discrete.
The regularised trace
\(\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{reg}}(\mathcal L_{n})\) of
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:rh:prop:Li-is-Virasoro} then integrates against
the discrete zero distribution: the Eisenstein continuous spectrum is
not in the domain of the trace.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Li's sum is over non-trivial zeros of \(\zeta\), equivalently zeros of
\(\xi_{\mathbb R}\). The non-trivial zeros are a countable discrete set
of density \(\sim T\log T/(2\pi)\) in \([0,T]\) (von Mangoldt--Riemann;
Titchmarsh 1986 Ch.~II). No Eisenstein-series pole contributes to
this set: Eisenstein poles at \(s=0,1\) in \(\Lambda_{\zeta}\) are
exactly the Tate poles eliminated by the factor \(s(s-1)/2\) in the
Li normalisation
(Remark~\ref{v4-arith:rh:rem:trivial-zero}). The zero divisor of
\(\xi_{\mathbb R}\) is therefore discrete. Under the
H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) hypothesis it identifies with the determinant
divisor and Weil--Connes trace of \(\Theta_{\xi}\)
(Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:rh:hyp:HPAr}). The Li-transform trace
\(\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{reg}}(\mathcal L_{n})\) equals the sum over
this discrete distribution only after that determinant-trace
identification.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[conditional Li path on the discrete core]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:cor:Li-path-closes}
Assume also the determinant-trace realization and the explicit
trace-placement comparison of
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li},
placing the Li trace on this same discrete domain.
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
\emph{(conditional on Ramanujan for Maass
forms at the cuspidal Maass boundary, on the H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\)
determinant-trace realisation, and on the Tamagawa-regularised assembly;
with arithmetic pairing comparisons also assumed on the holomorphic
and residual sectors)}. The sufficient RH path of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:rh:thm:sufficient-path} receives
a conditional P3 input from \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\)
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity}); the Li criterion
draws on discrete zero sums (Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li})
from the same domain, and the Eisenstein continuum is outside the
domain of both inputs.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
P3 on \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\) is the conditional
identity of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity}. The Li path of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:rh:thm:VB-implies-F-RH} uses \(\lambda_{n}\) which
are discrete-zero sums after the H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) spectral
identification (Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li}).
The Eisenstein continuum is outside the resulting trace domain.
\end{proof}

\section{Langlands--Shahidi Normalisation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:LS}

The Koszul--Petersson identity can be extended to the full
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\), including the Eisenstein continuum,
only after the Koszul pairing on principal-series inductions is
identified with the positive Langlands--Shahidi Plancherel measure.
The scalar \(N^{\mathrm{LS}}\) of Definition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:def:LS-factor}
is not by itself such an identification. The discrete-core reduction
of Section~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:eisenstein} is the construction used for the
Li trace; the present section records the conditional continuum
extension.

\subsection{The Langlands--Shahidi Normalisation Factor}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:LS:factor}

\begin{definition}[Langlands--Shahidi correction factor]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:def:LS-factor}
The \emph{Langlands--Shahidi normalisation factor} on the
principal-series induction \(\mathrm{Ind}_{B}^{G}(\chi_{s})\) is
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:LS-factor}
N^{\mathrm{LS}}(s)\;:=\;\frac{\xi(2s-1)}{\xi(2s)}\qquad(\mathrm{Re}(s)=1/2).
\end{equation}
On \(U\), \(|N^{\mathrm{LS}}(s)|=1\). In the scalar models above,
\(N^{\mathrm{LS}}K=d=P/2\); multiplication by the phase alone
does not identify the two coefficients. The scalar coming from
the Maass--Selberg truncation is part of the Plancherel convention, not
part of the Langlands--Shahidi normalising operator.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Koszul normalisation absorption]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:LS-absorption}
\emph{(on the identification of the bar--cobar
Koszul spectral measure on the Eisenstein continuum with the Shahidi
2010 normalised Eisenstein measure; see Remark~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:LS-gap})}. Under the substitution
\(\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}\to\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul},\mathrm{LS}}=N^{\mathrm{LS}}(\cdot)\cdot\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}\)
on the Eisenstein continuum and the independent identification of the
resulting quadratic form with the Shahidi \(L^{2}\)-normalised measure,
the corrected Koszul normalisation is transported to the
Maass--Selberg Plancherel normalisation:
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:LS-match}
\mathsf{Sh}_{\mathrm{LS}}\!\left(\operatorname{lead}^{\mathrm{Koszul}}_{\mathrm{cont}}(s)\right)
\;=\;\operatorname{lead}^{\mathrm{Pet}}_{\mathrm{cont}}(s).
\end{equation}
Here \(\mathsf{Sh}_{\mathrm{LS}}\) denotes the Shahidi Plancherel
transport. No raw equality of scalar densities is asserted before this
transport is supplied.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On \(\mathrm{Re}(s)=1/2\), \(|M(s)|=1\) (Moeglin--Waldspurger \S IV.3.10)
gives \(|N^{\mathrm{LS}}(s)|=1\). Hence the Langlands--Shahidi factor
changes the scattering phase but does not by itself turn the Koszul
coefficient into a positive Plancherel density. The equality
(\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:LS-match}) is exactly the additional
identification of the bar--cobar Eisenstein measure with the Shahidi
2010 \(L^{2}\)-normalised Eisenstein measure (Shahidi 2010
Thm~3.5.1). The scalar \(1+|M(s)|^{2}\) belongs to the
Maass--Selberg truncation convention and is not inserted into
\(N^{\mathrm{LS}}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Koszul--Shahidi measure identification]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:LS-gap}
The ratio \(\xi(2s)/\xi(2s-1)\) is a non-constant unit-modulus function
on \(\mathrm{Re}(s)=1/2\); it equals \(1/M(s)\) with \(|M(s)|=1\).
The equality of Plancherel normalisations holds only when the Koszul
Eisenstein measure is identified with the Shahidi 2010
\(L^{2}\)-normalised measure. Whether the bar--cobar Koszul definition
(Beilinson--Drinfeld 2004 \S3.5) assigns exactly this normalisation to
the Eisenstein sector is an open problem. This gap does not affect
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:main}: the main theorem uses the domain
restriction of Section~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:eisenstein}, not the
Langlands--Shahidi extension.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[full-\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) Koszul--Petersson compatibility via Langlands--Shahidi]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:full-vprim-LS}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
\emph{(on the Ramanujan bound for Maass cusp
forms of \(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\); on the identification of the bar--cobar
Koszul spectral measure on the Eisenstein sector with the Shahidi 2010
\(L^{2}\)-normalised Eisenstein measure
(Remark~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:LS-gap}); and on the Langlands--Shahidi
correction \(N^{\mathrm{LS}}\) as a canonical ingredient of the arithmetic
Koszul pairing on principal-series inductions)}. Assume Ramanujan for
Maass forms. For all \(v,w\in\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\),
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:full-vprim-LS}
\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}
\;=\;\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul},\mathrm{LS}},
\end{equation}
with \(\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul},\mathrm{LS}}\)
the Langlands--Shahidi-corrected Koszul pairing of
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:LS-absorption}. The identification extends
the discrete-core identity
(\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:discrete-core-identity}) to the Eisenstein
continuum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On the discrete core, \(N^{\mathrm{LS}}|_{\mathrm{disc}}=1\)
(the correction factor is supported on the Eisenstein continuum),
so Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity} applies without
modification. On the Eisenstein continuum,
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:LS-absorption} gives the conditional
Plancherel-normalisation match.
Adelic extension on the continuous carrier is part of the assumed
measure transport and factorization datum. It is not deduced from
the scalar identity for \(N^{\mathrm{LS}}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[domain of the Langlands--Shahidi correction]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:LS-cost}
The full \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) identity
(\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:full-vprim-LS}) extends the domain of the
Koszul pairing to the Eisenstein sector only under the measure
identification of Remark~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:LS-gap}; it is not obtained
by the bare insertion of
\(N^{\mathrm{LS}}\). The factor \(N^{\mathrm{LS}}\) is the
Langlands--Shahidi normalised intertwining operator
(Shahidi 2010 Ch.~2--3; Moeglin--Waldspurger \S IV.3), the natural
normalisation for the Eisenstein functional equation, and its insertion
is a definitional addition to the arithmetic Koszul pairing of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:arakelov-bar}, which uses the Bost--Connes
involution alone. The domain restriction of
Section~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:eisenstein} requires no such addition and
is sufficient only for the discrete trace normalisation of the Li path
under the hypotheses of
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li}.
\end{remark}

\section{Full \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) P3 on the Discrete Core}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:P3-statement}

\begin{proposition}[Pullback of the discrete Petersson form]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:positive-pullback}
Let \(i:V\to L^2_{\mathrm{disc}}([GL_2])\) be a specified injective
linear placement. Then \(h(v,w)=\langle i(v),i(w)\rangle_{L^2}\)
is positive definite. If \(V\) has a specified bilinear form \(B\)
and antilinear map \(J\) with \(B(v,Jw)=h(v,w)\), this transported
Koszul form is positive definite as well. No temperedness assumption
is needed for this implication.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For \(v\ne0\), injectivity gives \(i(v)\ne0\), and hence
\(h(v,v)=\|i(v)\|_{L^2}^2>0\). The form identity gives the
second assertion. Constructing \(i,B,J\) and the identity is the
separate arithmetic comparison problem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[P3 on the discrete core]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:P3-discrete-core}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
The comparison maps are assumed on every sector, including the
Maass sector, with the specified adelic assembly. The
discrete-spectrum core
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\subset\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\)
carries a positive-definite Hermitian form
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:P3-form}
\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}\colon\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\otimes\overline{\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}}\to\mathbb{C}
\end{equation}
given by the adelic Petersson--Tamagawa inner product, compatible
with arithmetic Koszul self-duality via
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:P3-compatibility}
\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}\;=\;\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}
\end{equation}
on \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\otimes\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\).
The Eisenstein continuous sector is placed outside the P3 identity
by the discrete-core domain restriction of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:def:discrete-core}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The arithmetic comparison hypothesis supplies the injective placement
and identifies the form with the Petersson form on its discrete
image. Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:positive-pullback}
therefore gives positivity and the asserted pairing identity.
This applies to ordinary Maass \(L^2\) vectors regardless of their
Satake bounds. Ramanujan is needed only for the separate route that
first restricts every Maass representation to the tempered model;
it does not replace or follow from the arithmetic pairing maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[P3 input for the sufficient RH path]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:cor:P3-input}
Assume also the determinant-trace realization and the explicit
trace-placement comparison of
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li},
placing the Li trace on this same discrete domain.
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
The P3 input of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:rh:thm:sufficient-path} is supplied by
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:P3-discrete-core} on the discrete core,
under the full comparison hypotheses on every sector. Combined with the discrete-support of the Li
sum (Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li}), the minimal
RH path of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:rh:thm:sufficient-path} receives a complete
Koszul--Petersson compatibility input drawn entirely from the
discrete-spectrum core.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:P3-discrete-core} + Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li}.
\end{proof}

\section{Koszul--Petersson Compatibility on the Domain-Restricted Discrete Core: Main Theorem}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:main-theorem}

\begin{theorem}[full-\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) Koszul--Petersson compatibility on the discrete core]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:main}
Assume Hypothesis~\ref{v4-arith:kms-gns:hyp:arithmetic-pairing}
on the stated sector, with its injective placement, local pairing maps
and adelic extension.
\emph{(domain-restricted to
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\); conditional on the arithmetic comparison maps on each sector;
with Ramanujan additionally assumed on the Maass sector)}. For all \(v,w\in\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\),
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:eq:main}
\langle v,w\rangle^{\mathrm{Pet}}\;=\;\langle v,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(w)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}.
\end{equation}
In particular, the Koszul pairing of
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\) with
a specified Koszul-dual carrier,
composed with the specified involution
\(\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}\), equals the adelic Petersson--Tamagawa
inner product on the discrete-spectrum image of
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\) inside
\(L^{2}_{\mathrm{disc}}([GL_{2}])\).
An identification of that dual carrier with \(\mathbb A^{\mathrm{BC}}\)
requires a separate dg algebra comparison; equality of pairings
does not establish it.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The sector-by-sector identities of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:discrete-core-identity} are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,temp}}\):
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:tempered-recap} (under the comparison hypotheses).
\item \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,hol}}\subset\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,temp}}\):
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:holomorphic-cuspidal} (temperedness from
Deligne and Deligne--Serre; pairing maps separately assumed).
\item \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{cusp,Maass}}\):
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:maass-cuspidal} (conditional on Ramanujan
for Maass forms).
\item \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{res}}\): direct evaluation
at the vacuum.
\end{itemize}
Orthogonality of the direct-sum decomposition
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}=\bigoplus_{\alpha}\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\alpha}\)
(Langlands 1966 \S6) extends the sector-by-sector identities to the
full discrete core. The domain restriction
(Definition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:def:discrete-core}) defines the domain of the
identity as \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\); the scalar discrepancy of Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:mismatch}
concerns the proposed continuous coefficients, not an established
arithmetic continuous pairing.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[carriers and pairing conventions for Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:main}]
\label{v4-arith:w5-c:rem:source-disjointness-comparison}
The pairing comparison uses the following carriers and conventions.

\textit{Koszul and automorphic constructions.} The Koszul side uses
Beilinson--Drinfeld 2004 (bar--cobar), Positselski 2009
(comodule--contramodule), and Bost--Connes 1995 (Koszul dual as
\(C^{*}\)-algebra). The Petersson side uses Deligne 1974 (Weil
I, holomorphic Ramanujan), Deligne--Serre 1974 (weight~1),
Kim--Shahidi 2002 and Kim--Sarnak 2003 (sub-convex Maass Ramanujan),
Langlands 1966 and Moeglin--Waldspurger 1995 (spectral decomposition),
Selberg 1956 (Petersson positivity). Relating these constructions
requires the map from the Koszul primitive carrier to the specified
automorphic realization and its pairing compatibility; the
bibliographic distinction is not such a map.

\textit{Sign and convention consistency.} \(\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}\) is
\(*\)-antilinear; Petersson is sesquilinear conjugate-in-second-slot;
Koszul is bilinear. The composition
\(\langle\,\cdot\,,\sigma^{\mathrm{BC}}(\cdot)\rangle^{\mathrm{Koszul}}\)
has the correct antilinearity. Tamagawa measure is Weil-normalised
(Weil 1967 Ch.~VII).

\textit{Ambient category.} Both sides are sesquilinear forms on
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\otimes\overline{\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}}\),
a subspace of the adelic Hilbert space \(L^{2}_{\mathrm{disc}}([GL_{2}])\).

\textit{Sector hypotheses.} Every arithmetic pairing conclusion retains the placement and
pairing hypotheses. The tempered-reduction argument for the Maass
sector additionally assumes Ramanujan; the positive-pullback argument
of Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:positive-pullback} does not.

\textit{Functoriality of the decomposition.} The discrete-core
decomposition is orthogonal under Petersson (Langlands 1966 \S6);
Koszul factorises by place (BD 2004 \S3.5) and the Bost--Connes
involution respects the decomposition.

\textit{Domain of the holomorphic and Maass closures.} The holomorphic
temperedness input is supplied by Deligne and Deligne--Serre; the
pairing maps are still required. Maass temperedness is conditional
on Ramanujan;
the Kim--Sarnak 2003 bound controls Satake exponents at each
prime (Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:KS-partial}) but not adelic closure.
The arithmetic comparison and adelic extension are additional
requirements on the Maass sector.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Conditional boundary.}
An additional representation-theoretic input of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:thm:main} is the Ramanujan bound for Maass cusp
forms of \(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\). The arithmetic placement and pairing maps are separate open
construction requirements. The Satake exponent bound does not supply a pairing-error estimate
or an absolutely convergent adelic comparison; see
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:KS-partial}.
The Li sum is over the discrete non-trivial zeros
(Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:prop:domain-suffices-for-Li}) after the
H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\) determinant-trace realisation; the Li path has
this trace condition in addition to the Maass boundary.

\paragraph{Eisenstein continuum.}
The domain restriction places the Eisenstein continuous spectrum
outside the identity's domain (Definition~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:def:discrete-core}).
The discrete-spectrum restriction suffices for the discrete trace
normalisation of the Li path under the stated H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\)
determinant-trace hypothesis
(Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:cor:Li-path-closes}). The Langlands--Shahidi
correction of Section~\ref{v4-arith:w5-c:LS} extends the identity to
the continuum only after the Koszul Eisenstein measure has been
identified with the Shahidi \(L^{2}\)-normalised measure.

\paragraph{The sufficient RH path.}
The P3 input of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:rh:thm:sufficient-path} is supplied by
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}_{\mathrm{disc}}\), conditional on the arithmetic comparison maps,
Ramanujan for Maass cusp forms and on the H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\)
determinant-trace realisation used to place the Li trace. The remaining inputs
are the conditional arithmetic Positselski, H-P\(^{\mathrm{Ar}}\), and
HP--Li positivity hypotheses recorded later.

\paragraph{Comparison of the two pairings.}
The Koszul constructions of Beilinson--Drinfeld (2004),
Positselski (2009) and Bost--Connes (1995) and the automorphic
constructions of Deligne (1974, 1980), Deligne--Serre (1974),
Kim--Shahidi (2002), Kim--Sarnak (2003), Langlands (1966),
Moeglin--Waldspurger (1995), Selberg (1956), Weil (1967),
Jacquet--Langlands (1970), Godement--Jacquet (1972), JPSS (1983),
Casselman--Shalika (1980), Bushnell--Henniart (2006), Iwaniec (2002),
Arthur (1978--1979) and Gelbart (1975) concern different carriers.
A comparison requires a specified map from the chosen Koszul
primitive space to the stated automorphic realization, with its
coefficient field, completion and domain fixed, and a proof that
it transports the Koszul pairing to the Petersson pairing with
the stated normalization. The cited constructions alone do not
supply such a map or its isometry property.
