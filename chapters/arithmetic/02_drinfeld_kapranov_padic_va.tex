\chapter{The \(p\)-adic Vertex Algebra Locality Problem}
\label{v4-arith:drinfeld-kapranov}

\emph{In the voice of Drinfeld + Kapranov. Drinfeld built vertex
algebras on curves via chiral/factorization algebras with Beilinson;
Kapranov (1995, unpublished ``Analogies between the Langlands
correspondence and topological quantum field theory'') proposed
loops-at-a-point for \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\) and Kapranov--Vasserot
(Publ.~IHES~100, 2004, arXiv:math/0107143) built the formal-loop
chiral framework for curves over fields. No published construction
over an arithmetic base exists.}

\section{The Algebraic Step: Formal VA over \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\)}
\label{v4-arith:dk:algebraic-step}

A vertex algebra over a field \(k\) of characteristic zero is a
vector space \(V_{k}\) with a state-field map
\begin{equation}
Y\colon V_{k}\otimes V_{k}\to V_{k}((z))
\end{equation}
satisfying axioms that are polynomial identities in formal variables
(Kac, \emph{Vertex Algebras for Beginners}, 2nd ed., 1998, \S1.3).
These polynomial identities are field-independent, so replacing \(\mathbb{C}\)
by \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\) as the coefficient field is immediate
at the algebraic level: the Heisenberg Fock space
\(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}=\mathrm{Sym}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}[t]\cdot a)\)
with modes \([a_{m},a_{n}]=m\delta_{m+n,0}\) and the operator product expansion (OPE)
\(a(z)a(w)\sim 1/(z-w)^{2}\) (singular part of the product) are identities of formal power series in
\(\mathrm{End}(\mathcal{H}^{(p)})[[z^{\pm 1},w^{\pm 1}]]\).

\begin{proposition}
\label{v4-arith:prop:formal-padic-va}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere (citation-level; Kac Thm~4.3a). For any
field \(k\) of characteristic zero, the rank-\(2\) Heisenberg VA
\(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}_{k}=\mathrm{Sym}_{k}(k[t](a\oplus b))\) with standard
OPE exists as a formal VA over \(k\).
\end{proposition}

The algebraic step is purely formal; the arithmetic content
resides in the conformal-vector scaling, the diagonal completion, and
the comparison with the local automorphic object on each finite place.
The three obstructions are: conformal-vector scaling (\S\ref{v4-arith:dk:conformal-vector-scaling}),
coefficient-field mismatch (\S\ref{v4-arith:dk:restricted-tensor-not-field}), and
diagonal non-thickening (\S\ref{v4-arith:dk:padic-diagonal-no-thickening}).

\section{Conformal Vector Scaling: Archimedean, Not \(p\)-adic}
\label{v4-arith:dk:conformal-vector-scaling}

The natural conformal vector for a local Heisenberg factor at place \(p\)
would weight the \(L_0\)-grading by \(\log p\), the archimedean size of
\(p\). The question is whether \(\log p\) has a \(p\)-adic interpretation.

\begin{observation}
\label{v4-arith:obs:log-p-not-padic}
The proposed conformal vector
\(\omega_{p}=\tfrac{1}{2}(\log p)(a_{-1}^{2}+b_{-1}^{2})|0\rangle\)
has a complex scalar \(\log p\in\mathbb{R}\). If
\(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) is a \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\)-VA, then
\(\omega_{p}\in\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) requires \(\log p\in\mathbb{Q}_{p}\).
The only candidate is the Iwasawa \(p\)-adic logarithm
\(\log_{p}\colon\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\times}\to\mathbb{Q}_{p}\), which by
convention sets \(\log_{p}(p)=0\) (Iwasawa, \emph{Lectures on p-adic
L-functions}, Princeton 1972, \S1; Koblitz, \emph{p-adic Analysis},
GTM~58, Ch.~IV).
\end{observation}

Under Iwasawa's \(\log_{p}\), the \(p\)-adic value of \(\omega_{p}\)
has zero conformal-vector coefficient. The scalar \(\log p\in\mathbb{R}\) is the \emph{scale} at which
\(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) contributes to the global partition function
(Hartnoll--Yang arXiv:2502.02661~\S3); it is an archimedean datum external to the \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\)-algebra structure. The central charge of
\(\omega_{p}\) is \(2\) regardless; the \(L_{0}\)-grading carries the
\(\log p\) through the archimedean completion.

\begin{convention}
\label{v4-arith:conv:log-p-archimedean}
\(\log p\) is archimedean-real scaling data, external to the
\(p\)-adic algebra structure. At finite place \(p\) the VA is
\(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}_{\mathbb{Q}_{p}}\) with vacuum conformal vector
\(\tfrac{1}{2}(a_{-1}^{2}+b_{-1}^{2})|0\rangle\); the rescaling
enters through external grading data at the archimedean
completion.
\end{convention}

\section{The Restricted Tensor Product: Coefficient-Field Mismatch}
\label{v4-arith:dk:restricted-tensor-not-field}

To assemble the local Heisenberg factors \(\{\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\}_{p}\)
into a global object over \(\overline{C}\), one would form a restricted
tensor product over unramified vectors. The obstruction is that
\(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) and \(\mathcal{H}^{(q)}\) for \(p\neq q\) live
over different coefficient fields.

\begin{observation}
\label{v4-arith:obs:no-single-field}
The restricted tensor product \(\bigotimes_{p}^{!}\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\)
has a coefficient-field obstruction: \(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) is a vector
space over \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\), and the fields \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\) for
distinct primes \(p\) have no common field extension in the filtered
colimit of finite tensor products. Forming a single vector space from
\(\{\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\}_{p}\) requires passing to a ring (the adele
ring) rather than a field.
\end{observation}

Three approaches address the coefficient mismatch.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{(Base-extend to \(\mathbb{C}\)):} Each
\(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) is base-extended to \(\mathbb{C}\) before
tensoring, recovering the standard complex Heisenberg VA.
The result is a complex VA carrying only archimedean data,
with the \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\)-structure collapsed by base extension.
\item \textbf{(Base-extend to \(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}\)):} Tensor
over the adele ring \(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}\), which is a ring (not a field).
The result is an adelic module carrying Hecke-module structure at each
finite place; its algebraic structure is that of a module over
\(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}\), distinct from a classical vertex algebra.
\item \textbf{(Form functor):} Let \(\mathrm{Fields}/\mathbb{Q}\) denote
the category of field extensions of \(\mathbb{Q}\). Define
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\colon\mathrm{Fields}/\mathbb{Q}\to\mathrm{VA}\)
with \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})=\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\)
and \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}(\mathbb{C})=\) complex Heisenberg.
This is a form (in the sense of Galois descent) of the complex Heisenberg,
requiring a Galois-action datum and Frobenius-compatibility to be
well-defined.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{warning}
Option~(a) recovers only complex data. Option~(b) produces an adelic
module with Hecke-module structure at each finite place, studied further
in \Cref{v4-arith:thm:adelic-automorphic-placement}. Option~(c)
requires arithmetic input (Galois action, Frobenius compatibility)
beyond the naive algebraic construction.
\end{warning}

\section{The \(p\)-adic Diagonal: Trivial Formal Neighbourhood}
\label{v4-arith:dk:padic-diagonal-no-thickening}

The Beilinson--Drinfeld chiral bracket is a residue at the diagonal of
\(C \times C\). On a complex curve, the formal neighbourhood of the diagonal
\(\Delta \hookrightarrow C \times C\) is a formal disk \(\mathrm{Spf}\,\mathbb{C}[[t]]\),
which supports the full OPE. At a finite place \(p\), the corresponding
question is whether the diagonal of \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}_{p} \times_{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}_{p}\)
admits a non-trivial formal thickening. The completed tensor product
answers this directly.

\begin{proposition}
\label{v4-arith:prop:padic-diagonal-collapse}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. The formal completion
\(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\,\widehat{\otimes}_{\mathbb{Z}}\,\mathbb{Z}_{p}\cong\mathbb{Z}_{p}\),
because \(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\) is \(p\)-adically complete and the diagonal
is already an isomorphism after completion.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write \(\mathbb{Z}_{p}=\varprojlim_{n}\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}\). Since
\(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\) is flat over \(\mathbb{Z}\), the tensor product
\((-)\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}_{p}\) commutes with this sequential
inverse limit (Weibel, \emph{An Introduction to Homological Algebra},
Cor.~3.2.10); and \(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\) is a discrete valuation ring with
uniformizer \(p\), so
\(\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}_{p}
=\mathbb{Z}_{p}/p^{n}\mathbb{Z}_{p}=\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}\). Hence
\[
\mathbb{Z}_{p}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}_{p}
= \varprojlim_{n}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}_{p}\bigr)
= \varprojlim_{n}\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}
= \mathbb{Z}_{p}.
\]
The result is already \(p\)-adically complete, so the completed and
ordinary tensor products coincide. The diagonal is the identity map,
and \(\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}_{p})
=\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}_{p}\) is one-dimensional; the diagonal is an
isomorphism, so the formal neighbourhood is trivial.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Locally constant structure at finite places]
\label{v4-arith:cor:no-padic-ope}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. At each finite place \(p\), the
Beilinson--Drinfeld chiral bracket
\(\mu^{\mathrm{ch}}\colon j_{*}j^{*}(\mathcal{A}\boxtimes\mathcal{A})
\to\Delta_{!}\mathcal{A}\) degenerates to the locally constant structure
on \(\mathrm{Spf}\,\mathbb{Z}_{p}\): the formal neighbourhood of the
diagonal is trivial (\Cref{v4-arith:prop:padic-diagonal-collapse}),
so the residue-at-diagonal construction collapses. The archimedean
place supports the full holomorphic OPE singularity.
\end{corollary}

\section{The Locally Constant (\texorpdfstring{\(E_{0}\)}{E0}) Local Model at Each Finite Place}
\label{v4-arith:dk:E0-local-model}

The absence of a formal thickening of the diagonal forces the local
factor at each finite place to carry only the structure available on
a locally constant prestack: commutative multiplication and Hecke
endomorphisms, with no OPE singularity.

\begin{definition}
\label{v4-arith:def:E0-local-factor}
The local factor of \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) at a finite place
\(p\) is the locally constant prestack
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}|_{p}\) on \(\mathrm{Spf}\,\mathbb{Z}_{p}\)
whose constant stalk is \(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\) equipped with the
\(p\)-adic filtration and a compatible Hecke--Adams endomorphism
\(T_{p}\colon\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\to\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\). This is a
factorization algebra in the degenerate (\(E_{0}\)) sense: the
``fusion'' \(\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\otimes\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\to\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\)
is the commutative multiplication (constant along \(\mathrm{Spf}\,\mathbb{Z}_p\)), with no singular diagonal term.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[global structure at archimedean place]
\label{v4-arith:thm:archimedean-is-holomorphic}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. At \(\infty\), \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}|_{\infty}\)
is the ordinary Heisenberg vertex algebra on a germ
\((\mathbb{C},0)\), a genuine Beilinson--Drinfeld/Costello--Gwilliam
factorization algebra with non-trivial diagonal singularity.
\end{theorem}

\section{The Adelic Automorphic Placement of \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:dk:true-hidden-structure}

\begin{theorem}[adelic automorphic placement]
\label{v4-arith:thm:adelic-automorphic-placement}
\ClaimStatusConjectured. \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) is an adelic
automorphic object: a compatible system of local factors
\(\{\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}|_{v}\}_{v\in|\overline{C}|}\) with
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}|_{\infty}\) a genuine Heisenberg VA at
the archimedean place and \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}|_{p}\) an
\(E_{0}\)-algebra (Fock module with Hecke-Adams endomorphism) at
each finite place. Under Tate's restricted tensor product over
spherical vectors \(|0\rangle_{p}\) (Tate~1950; Weil~1967,
\emph{Basic Number Theory}, Ch.~VII), the system assembles into an
automorphic representation of \(GL_{2}(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}})\)
with trivial ramification at unramified primes.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The classical chiral-algebras formalism of Beilinson--Drinfeld
(AMS~Colloq.~51, 2004) applies to a curve over a field. Over the
arithmetic curve \(\overline{C}=\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\)
(Arakelov compactification, with archimedean place \(\infty\) adjoined),
the appropriate structure is a compatible system: a factorization-algebra
factor at \(\infty\) and a locally constant factor at each finite place,
tied together by automorphic compatibility (Tate-style restricted tensor
product). The global object is a spectral datum; its OPE singularity
is supported entirely at \(\infty\).
\end{remark}

\section{Kapranov--Vasserot: Formal Loop Spaces over Fields}
\label{v4-arith:dk:kapranov-built}

\begin{remark}
Kapranov--Vasserot's four-paper series \emph{Vertex algebras and
the formal loop space} (arXiv:math/0107143, Publ.~IHES~100, 2004;
and subsequent math/0612371, etc.) introduces the ind-scheme
\(\mathcal{L}X\) of formal loops into a finite-type scheme \(X\) over
a field and shows that factorization structure on \(\mathcal{L}X\) recovers the
VA structure on chiral de Rham. The series does not extend to \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\).
Formulating the arithmetic analogue over \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\) requires the adelic automorphic structure of \Cref{v4-arith:thm:adelic-automorphic-placement}.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
Kapranov's 1995 unpublished preprint ``Analogies between the
Langlands correspondence and topological quantum field theory''
\emph{motivates} the search for an arithmetic chiral-algebra
formalism but does not construct one. Kapranov--Smirnov's
\emph{Cohomology determinants and reciprocity laws: number field
case} (also unpublished, ca.~1995) gives an \(\mathbb{F}_{1}\)-style
analogy via cohomology determinants feeding reciprocity laws; this
is the foundational informal reference for the \(\mathbb{F}_{1}\)
literature (Manin arXiv:1312.5160; Thas, \emph{F\(_{1}\) for
Everyone}, 2018; Lorscheid survey).
\end{remark}

\section{Summary of This Chapter}
\label{v4-arith:dk:summary}

\begin{enumerate}
\item A formal VA over \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\) exists algebraically
(\Cref{v4-arith:prop:formal-padic-va}); the arithmetic content
enters through the archimedean \(\log p\) scaling, which is external
to the \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\)-algebra structure (\Cref{v4-arith:obs:log-p-not-padic}).
\item The restricted tensor product \(\bigotimes^{!}_{p}\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\)
has a coefficient-field obstruction
(\Cref{v4-arith:obs:no-single-field}); it is an adelic module
or a form-functor \(\mathrm{Fields}/\mathbb{Q}\to\mathrm{VA}\).
\item The \(p\)-adic diagonal is trivial after completion
(\Cref{v4-arith:prop:padic-diagonal-collapse}); the Beilinson--Drinfeld
chiral bracket degenerates to the locally constant structure at each finite
place (\Cref{v4-arith:cor:no-padic-ope}).
\item Each finite place carries an \(E_{0}\)-algebra
(\Cref{v4-arith:def:E0-local-factor}); the archimedean place
carries a genuine Heisenberg VA
(\Cref{v4-arith:thm:archimedean-is-holomorphic}).
\item \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) is an adelic automorphic datum
(\Cref{v4-arith:thm:adelic-automorphic-placement}): its OPE
lives at \(\infty\); at each finite place \(p\) it carries an
\(E_{0}\)-algebra factor with Hecke--Adams endomorphism.
\end{enumerate}

The three arithmetic obstructions identified (conformal-vector
scaling, coefficient-field mismatch, and diagonal non-thickening)
force the adelic automorphic structure of
\Cref{v4-arith:thm:adelic-automorphic-placement}.
