\section{Identity}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:identity}

The arithmetic branch of Volume~IV carries the extension of the
chiral-bar-cobar programme from smooth complex curves
\(C/\mathbb{C}\) to the Arakelov-compactified arithmetic curve
\(\overline{C} = \overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\). Its object of
study is the Hochschild trace class of the identity on the global
arithmetic Iwahori Hecke category,
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:00:def:Vprim}
\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}
\;:=\;
\mathrm{HH}_{\bullet}\bigl(\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_{2},\,\mathrm{glob}}\bigr),
\end{equation}
studied through five conditional technical windows: (i) a classical
Kim arithmetic Chern--Simons torsor/line (arXiv:1510.05818) whose
possible boundary factorization shadow is a comparison problem for
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\); (ii) an automorphic/spectral window
controlled by a Selmer-derived global deformation stack with fixed
residual representation, determinant, and local conditions; (iii) a
Bethe-algebra heuristic in which primes are lattice sites and Hecke
operators are transfer matrices, with the RTT/Yangian Hopf structure
absent at the arithmetic level; (iv) a Heisenberg enhancement of the
Bost--Connes quantum statistical system; and (v) a factorization
algebra on the archimedean place \(\mathbb{C}_{\infty}\) valued in
\(\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}\)-modules with commuting Adams
operations~\(\psi_{p}\). Each window is a comparison problem for
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\); their mutual equivalence is
the conditional content of the Hochschild-trace layer.

This branch states the five theorems \(A\)--\(D\)+\(H\) in their
arithmetic avatar and isolates three precise open conjectures whose
resolution closes the cross-Langlands loop. The arithmetic avatars
\(A^{\mathrm{arith}}\)--\(D^{\mathrm{arith}}\),
\(H^{\mathrm{arith}}\), and \(F1\)--\(F3\) form the realization diagram
over \(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\). Agreement over both
\(C/\mathbb{C}\) and \(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\) is a
structural assertion, not a metaphor.

\section{Form Discipline}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:form}

Every claim in the arithmetic branch carries one of five tags.

\begin{description}
\item[Theorem.] A claim stated with a proof body. In the
arithmetic branch this is reserved for statements whose proof
transports from the complex case with no new input beyond
replacement of \(C/\mathbb{C}\) by the archimedean fibre of
\(\overline{C}\).
\item[Conditional.] A claim with a complete strategy of proof
and all ingredients in the primary literature, whose full write-up
requires a substantial treatment.
\item[Conjecture.] A claim plausible on the strength of multiple
cross-consistency checks but without a complete proof strategy.
\item[Heuristic.] A pattern match between classical and arithmetic
structures with no derivation. Heuristic claims are essential
only as guidance, never as premises.
\item[Obstruction.] A failed candidate whose failure is a theorem:
the obstruction is a named computation, missing structure, or
non-vanishing class.
\end{description}

Only theorem, conditional theorem, conjecture, heuristic, and
obstruction enter the stable language of the branch. A failed
candidate remains in the text only after its failure has been made
mathematical.

\section{Local-to-Global Form}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:shape}

The branch has three layers: local obstruction theory, the
Hochschild-trace construction of \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\), and
the three conjectural comparison maps completing the
cross-Langlands loop.

\subsection*{Local obstruction theory}

Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:construction} isolates the finite Heisenberg
cover
\(\mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{cov}}_{\mathbb P}=\bigotimes^{!}_{p}\mathcal{H}^{(p)}\)
from the Hochschild trace class \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\).
The next five chapters compare this cover with independent
source families
(Drinfeld+Kapranov, Kazhdan+Beilinson, Gelfand+Borger,
Connes+Soibelman, Costello+Gwilliam). Each comparison gives one
obstruction and one surviving structural core:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The arithmetic curve is Arakelov-compactified
\(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\), not \(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}\).
\item Each finite place carries only locally-constant (\(E_{0}\))
factorization structure; the \(E_{1}\)-chiral OPE lives at the
archimedean place.
\item The Arnold relation lifts to the Steinberg relation
\(\{p,1-p\}=0\) in \(K_{2}^{M}(\mathbb{Q})\); the chiral bar complex
is built on the Parshin--Gersten complex on \(\overline{C}\).
\item The Hecke-Adams lift is \(GL_{1}\) Adams, not \(GL_{2}\) Hecke;
the \(GL_{2}\) structure lives in the rank-\(2\) Heisenberg via the
Satake eigenvalue pair \((\alpha_{p},\beta_{p})\) with
\(\alpha_{p}\beta_{p}=p\).
\item The correct local model is the Iwahori-equivariant affine
Hecke category, not the spherical Mirkovi\'c--Vilonen category;
spherical collapses the OPE.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Hochschild trace synthesis}

The central theorem of the branch places
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) as the Hochschild trace class of the
identity endofunctor on the global affine Iwahori Hecke category
(Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:hochschild-trace}). Under this placement,
ten classical positions receive arithmetic analogues: Segal sewing
is occupied by Euler-product factorization (Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:modular-functor});
the Verlinde scalar becomes the class-number residue; the mapping-class
position is occupied by Galois labels and Hecke symmetries; the modular
\(S\)-matrix position is occupied by the \(\Gamma\)-factor plus functional
equation; the conformal-block position is occupied by Tate integral lines.

Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:closure} isolates the
essential mathematical content of the branch: the arithmetic
\(\mathsf{G}\)-row complementarity
\[
\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}
+\bigl(\kappa^{!}\bigr)^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}
\;=\;0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\xi(s)\;=\;\xi(1-s),
\]
stated as a conditional whose proof fuses Koszul duality of
the arithmetic Heisenberg with Tate's adelic Poisson summation.

\subsection*{Comparison conjectures}

Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:open-frontiers} isolates the three
conjectures whose proof closes the cross-Langlands loop:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Global arithmetic Arinkin--Gaitsgory equivalence, linking
\(\mathrm{D}([GL_{2}])^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\) to
\(\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\) on the Selmer-derived global
spectral stack attached to a fixed \((\bar\rho,\det,\mathcal{L})\).
\item \(E_{2}\)-enhancement of the global Iwahori Hecke category via
Scholze diamonds, supplying Dunn additivity for the trace.
\item Arithmetic Ben-Zvi--Nadler identification of
\(\mathrm{HH}_{\bullet}(\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_{2},\,\mathrm{glob}})\)
with \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) as an \(E_{1}\)-algebra over the
full Weil/\((\varphi,\Gamma)\) target, with \(B\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}\)
retained only as an inertia shadow.
\end{enumerate}

These three conjectures link Kim's classical 3D-CS input
(arXiv:1510.05818), Bezrukavnikov--Bernstein's Hochschild
presentation, and Beilinson's bar--cobar presentation.
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) is a Hochschild trace class
over \(E_1\)-algebras; the global arithmetic VOA structure, if
any, lives at the complex archimedean fibre alone.

\section{Realization over \texorpdfstring{\(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\)}{Spec Z bar}}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:role}

The arithmetic branch puts the same bar--cobar spine, Koszul
complementarity, and obstruction tower on the Arakelov curve

arithmetic bases carry the same identities after source-disjoint

The Beilinson principle is the governing rule: a statement enters the
realized corpus only after an independent comparison has separated its
proof source from its verification source.

\section{Conventions}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:conventions}

\begin{description}
\item[Base.] \(\overline{C}=\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\)
denotes the Arakelov compactification: closed points are rational
primes \(p\in\mathbb{P}\) plus the archimedean place \(\infty\). Local
rings: \(\mathbb{Z}_{p}\) at \(p\) (uniformizer~\(p\), residue
\(\mathbb{F}_{p}\), Frobenius \(x\mapsto x^{p}\)); \(\mathbb{R}\) at
\(\infty\) (with complex conjugation on~\(\mathbb{C}\)).
\item[Iwasawa.] \(\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}_{p}[[\Gamma]]\) with
\(\Gamma=\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})/\mathbb{Q})\cong\mathbb{Z}_{p}\).
\item[Adeles.] \(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}=\mathbb{R}\times\prod'_{p}\mathbb{Q}_{p}\)
(restricted product); \(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}^{\times}\) the idele
group; \(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}}^{\times}/\mathbb{Q}^{\times}\) the
idele class group.
\item[Hecke.] \(\mathcal{H}_{p}^{\mathrm{sph}}=C_{c}(GL_{n}(\mathbb{Z}_{p})\backslash GL_{n}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})/GL_{n}(\mathbb{Z}_{p}))\);
Satake isomorphism
\(\mathrm{Sat}_{p}:\mathcal{H}_{p}^{\mathrm{sph}}\xrightarrow{\sim}
\mathbb{Z}[\alpha_{1}^{\pm},\dots,\alpha_{n}^{\pm}]^{S_{n}}\).
\item[Satake pair (GL\({}_{2}\)).] \(\alpha_{p},\beta_{p}\in\mathbb{C}^{\times}\)
with \(\alpha_{p}\beta_{p}=p\); trace
\(a_{p}=\alpha_{p}+\beta_{p}=2p^{1/2}\cos\theta_{p}\) on unitary
sector.
\item[Kesten--McKay.] \(\nu_{p}(\theta)=(2/\pi)\cdot p(1+p)\sin^{2}\theta/(1+p^{2}-2p\cos 2\theta)\),
\(\theta\in[0,\pi]\); this is the Plancherel density of unramified
\(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})\).
\item[Arakelov degree.] \(\widehat{\deg}\,\mathcal{O}(s\cdot[\infty])=s\in\widehat{\mathrm{CH}}^{1}(\overline{C})\otimes\mathbb{R}\cong\mathbb{R}\).
\item[Completed zeta.] \(\xi(s)=\tfrac{1}{2}s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)\)
with functional equation \(\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\).
\end{description}

All references to Vols~I--III use the native cross-volume citation.
The branch has no internal dependency cycle; chapters are readable in
order.

\section{Imports and Assembly}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:out-of-domain}

This branch imports local theorems from the arithmetic Langlands
programme: local geometric Satake (Fargues--Scholze,
arXiv:2102.13459), the Emerton--Gee stack
(arXiv:2012.12403), and Caraiani--Scholze vanishing.
It assembles them into the \(E_{1}\)-chiral-algebra presentation
natural to the bar--cobar programme; the novel content is the
assembly. Caraiani--Scholze, Fargues--Scholze, and Emerton--Gee
develop the arithmetic Langlands programme independently; this
branch is a consequent consumer.

\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\) is a Hochschild trace class whose
character is an \(L\)-function. The vertex-operator-algebra (VOA)
structure, if sought, is available at the complex Heisenberg on
the archimedean fibre; the global object is an \(E_1\)-algebra,
not a VOA.

The Deninger \(\mathbb{F}_{1}\)-geometry supplies
motivation and is cited as a consequent reference. At the overlaps
(the adelic norm form \(\prod_{\nu}|x|_{\nu}=1\), the
Connes--Consani arithmetic site as a categorical base, tropical
semirings for the structure sheaf) the arithmetic branch is a formal
consequence of Deninger's comparison geometry.

\section{Arithmetic-Modular Extension: All-Genus Axis}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:all-genus}

The three-layer arithmetic branch treats the
\(g=0\) row: Riemann \(\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\) as the functional-equation
witness of Heisenberg \(\mathsf{G}\)-row complementarity
\(\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar}}=0\). A six-source-family
comparison extends the branch to arbitrary \(g\geq 0\). Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:all-genus-koszul}
defines the \emph{arithmetic genus-\(g\) datum} \(\mathcal{D}_g\) admitting
six convergent presentations: Arakelov fibration (Faltings, Gillet--Soulé,
Bost--Künnemann); Minkowski torus over totally imaginary \(K\) of
degree \(2g\) (Deninger, Connes--Consani, Bost--Connes--Marcolli); ordinary
crystalline Emerton--Gee stack \(\mathcal{X}_{2g}^{\mathrm{crys,ord}}\)
(Fargues--Scholze, Bhatt--Scholze, Caraiani--Scholze); Hodge--Arakelov
point of arithmetic Teichmüller \(\mathcal{T}_g^{\mathrm{arith}}\) with
theta-structure (Mochizuki); chiral-bar-cobar interior
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim,surf},g}=\mathrm{HH}_\bullet(\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw,surf}}_{GL_{2g},\mathrm{glob}})\)
with \(E_2\)-structure via Dunn additivity (Beilinson--Drinfeld,
Francis--Gaitsgory, Ben-Zvi--Nadler); Kudla--Rapoport Shimura--Siegel
datum with \(g\)-fold arithmetic intersection of special cycles
(Kudla, Bruinier, Li--Liu).

The essential conditional of the extension
(\Cref{v4-arith:ag:thm:all-genus}) states: the Koszul self-duality
identity \(\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar},g}+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar},g}=0\) is
preserved at every \(g\geq 0\) and is equivalent to the motivic
functional equation of the corresponding row. The genus enters the
functional equation through the archimedean \(\Gamma\)-factor
\(\Gamma_{\mathbb{C}}(s)^g\), the rank \(2g\) of the Heisenberg Fock
oscillators, and the motivic weight \(w_{\mathcal{D}_g}\in\{1,2,2g+1\}\)
setting the functional-equation center (Dedekind zeta, Hasse--Weil
\(L\)-function of the Jacobian, Siegel spin \(L\)-function of a
\(\mathrm{GSp}_{2g}\) cusp form); the Siegel-spin entry \(w=2g+1\)
inherits the spinor \(L\)-function normalisation convention for
vector-valued \(\mathrm{GSp}_{2g}\) cusp forms (Arthur 2013) from
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:all-genus-koszul}. The \(g=0\) specialisation
recovers the zero-placement axis of
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:rh-reformulation}. The all-genus F-RH corollary
(\Cref{v4-arith:ag:cor:all-genus-FRH}) is the common form of the
zero-placement clauses for the classical zeta function, Hasse--Weil
\(L\)-functions of Jacobians, Dedekind zeta functions of totally
imaginary fields, and Siegel-spin \(L\)-functions for
\(\mathrm{GSp}_{2g}\) automorphic representations; it is not a proof
of these hypotheses without the corresponding spectral positivity
input.

The almost-complex-structure candidate is obstructed in every source
class at a category-theoretic joint: an arithmetic scheme has no real
tangent bundle, and Newlander--Nirenberg integrability is a theorem on
real two-manifolds. The surviving operator is Frobenius
\(\varphi_g\) on primitive cohomology, namely archimedean Frobenius,
prismatic \(\varphi\), or the chiral Koszul operator
\(J^{\mathrm{Koz}}\); see
\Cref{v4-arith:ag:rem:almost-complex}. The extension is
arithmetic-modular in the sense that the six windows are modular
presentations of a single object \(\mathcal{D}_g\), each supplying an
independent verification path satisfying the independence of sources
criterion. The residual conditions of
\Cref{v4-arith:ag:residual} are ten genus-\(g\) comparison morphisms;
each is a finite theorem condition in the corresponding source family.

\section{Arithmetic Gravity and the \(E_1\) Ordered Bar}
\label{v4-arith:manifesto:arith-gravity}

The conformal-vector content of the all-genus extension sources an
arithmetic Kac--Moody enhancement of \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\),
non-critical by construction via the Koszul self-duality
\(\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}=0\)
(\Cref{v4-arith:3d-acs:thm:km-lift}). The arithmetic Kac--Moody in turn
sources a conjectural 3D quantum Chern-Simons topological gauge theory
\(\mathrm{ACS}^{\mathrm{qu}}_{k^{\mathrm{Ar}}}\) on the étale 3-space
\(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\) (Mazur 1973 étale cohomological
dimension; Morishita 2012 primes-as-knots), extending Kim 2015
(arXiv:1510.05818) from classical torsor to finite state sums and then
conjectural profinite TQFT. The boundary comparison with
\(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\), the zeta scalar character, and the
Beilinson--Drinfeld/Costello--Gwilliam (BD/CG) chiral-homology
comparison are conditional. What is closed in
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:3d-acs-E1bar} is the ordered incidence-bar
calculation on the auxiliary ordered place set
\(\mathbb{P}\cup\{\infty\}\)
(\Cref{v4-arith:3d-acs:thm:chi-hom-bar}).

Over the arithmetic base the 3D topological gauge theory is
Chern-Simons rather than holomorphic-twist: \(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\)
has étale cohomological dimension \(3\) without a differential-geometric
holomorphic structure. The complex Vol~II Part~VI 3D HT QFT bulk
becomes the 3D arithmetic Chern-Simons bulk; the complex-boundary
\(\mathcal{V}\) becomes the arithmetic-boundary \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\).
The zero-placement clause of F-RH (\Cref{v4-arith:conj:F-RH}) becomes
a spectral statement on the partition-function zero locus of the bulk TQFT
(\Cref{v4-arith:3d-acs:thm:arith-gravity-FRH}); the all-genus
F-RH corollary (\Cref{v4-arith:ag:cor:all-genus-FRH}) lifts to the
bulk partition function valued in \(\Lambda_{\mathcal{D}_g}(k^{\mathrm{Ar},g})\)
for every row of the arithmetic-modular family
(\Cref{v4-arith:3d-acs:cor:all-genus-arith-gravity}).

The arithmetic-gravity closure adds a non-formal theorem condition of
residual condition to the all-genus programme
(\Cref{v4-arith:3d-acs:residual}): quantum ACS construction on the
étale site; bulk-boundary correspondence; ordered-incidence bar
calculation plus conditional BD/CG comparison; and the positive
Hilbert--P\'olya/VB input. Together with Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:all-genus-koszul}'s
all-genus residual list, the extension of the arithmetic branch from
the \(g=0\) axis to the all-genus arithmetic-modular family plus
arithmetic-gravity bulk takes a non-formal theorem condition in four to
six mathematical blocks.
