\section{The Four-Input Conditional Spine, Made Explicit}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-B:four-input-spine}

After the status corrections of this chapter, the minimal closure
path to RH within Vol~IV's arithmetic branch is the conjunction of

\begin{remark}[the four-input conditional spine]
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-B:thm:four-input-spine}
\ClaimStatusConjectured. The Platonic RH-completion theorem
(\Cref{v4-arith:platonic:thm:RH-completion}) assembles P1, P2, P3,
\(\mathrm{H}\text{-}\mathrm{P}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\),
\(\mathrm{VB}^{*}\), and Hamburger determinacy into an RH-closure
package. After the Spine~B corrections of this chapter, the open
inputs reduce to the following four:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{arith.~Positselski}: the \(\Lambda\)-coefficient
Positselski equivalence of
\Cref{v4-arith:thm:arithmetic-positselski} on the abelian primary
Heisenberg scope, conditional on the five open sub-items
(completed continuous duals; derived completion; faithful lifting;
primitive zeta-character theorem; Koszul-dual Bost--Connes).
\item \textbf{Koszul--Petersson compatibility}: the identification
\Cref{v4-arith:kp:thm:main-full} scope-restricted to the normalized
sph-fin cuspidal core, with the open \(\mathrm{KMS}_{1}\)/GNS
identification and full-sector Rankin--Selberg extension.
\item \textbf{\(\mathrm{H}\text{-}\mathrm{P}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\)}: the
arithmetic Hilbert--P\'olya hypothesis in the spectral-realisation
form of \Cref{v4-arith:hpvbconv:thm:HPAr-equivalence}; its open
content is the zeta-zero spectral matching (Hilbert--P\'olya itself).
\item \textbf{\(\mathrm{VB}^{*}\) main direction}: the
operator-positivity statement
\Cref{v4-arith:rh:thm:VB-implies-F-RH} as a standalone residual.
\end{enumerate}
The first two are scope-restricted reductions of Spine~B Positselski
and adelic compatibility; the third is a Friedrichs extension plus
Hilbert--P\'olya; the fourth is the operator-positivity residual.
When all four hold, F-RH follows from
\Cref{v4-arith:platonic:thm:RH-completion}. No converse necessity is
claimed for the individual inputs. The logical independence of the
\(\mathrm{H}\text{-}\mathrm{P}^{\mathrm{Ar}}\)/\(\mathrm{VB}^{*}\)
residuals is \Cref{v4-arith:hpvbconv:prop:HPAr-det-independent};
the Positselski and Koszul--Petersson residuals are separated in
\Cref{v4-arith:pos:residual-scope} and
\Cref{v4-arith:kp:rem:paper-length-reestimate}.

\section{Status of the Load-Bearing Biconditional}
\label{v4-arith:w3-audit-B:load-bearing}

The load-bearing biconditional
\begin{equation*}
\kappa^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}+(\kappa^{!})^{\mathrm{Ar}}_{\mathsf{G}}
\;=\;0\;\Longleftrightarrow\;\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)
\end{equation*}
(\Cref{v4-arith:thm:G-row-Riemann-functional-equation},
\Cref{v4-arith:thm:P1-resolution}) asserts that arithmetic Koszul
self-duality for the abelian Heisenberg archetype is equivalent to
the Riemann functional equation. The forward direction
\((\Rightarrow)\) is established in
\Cref{v4-arith:cl:cor:P1-unconditional-forward} via Tate's 1950
adelic Poisson formula. The full biconditional requires arithmetic
Positselski (\Cref{v4-arith:thm:arithmetic-positselski}) and
therefore inherits the five open sub-items of OC1.

The Spine~B corrections preserve the biconditional's status as
\emph{Theorem-waiting}: the forward direction is unconditional; the
reverse direction is conditional on arithmetic Positselski in the
scope of OC1. This is consistent with the Platonic capstone's
