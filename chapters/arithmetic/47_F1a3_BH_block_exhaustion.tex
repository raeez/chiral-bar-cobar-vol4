\chapter{F1.a.3: Bushnell--Henniart Block Exhaustion at Wild Primes}
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH}

Block exhaustion asks whether the wild block functor reaches every
object of the proposed spectral category. The conditional argument
uses three inputs: a BH type-block comparison, an actual
extension-class comparison, and compact generation by the specified
spectral objects. Finite lists and equal local dimensions alone do
not imply essential surjectivity.

The local arithmetic identifications used below are hypotheses,
including the coefficient-compatible block models and maps in
Questions~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2}
and~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3}.
The finite-algebra resolutions in that chapter do not classify the
arithmetic blocks. In particular, the degree-one dimensions do not
construct a map to an Emerton--Gee fibre. Whenever a comparison
uses the three-vertex radical-square-zero model, its nonzero
Yoneda product in degree two must also be preserved.

\section{Statement}
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:statement}

\subsection{Setup}

Fix \(p\in\{2,3\}\), a specified common coefficient ring \(R\),
a smooth full central character
\(\chi:\mathbb Q_p^\times\to R^\times\), and a conductor bound
\(N\). Any use of a field \(k\) in a finite-algebra model includes
a specified coefficient map and base-change comparison to this
arithmetic setting. No identification of prime-to-\(p\) coefficients
with the integral Emerton--Gee coefficients is presumed.
Let \(\mathbf T^{\mathrm{BH}}_{p,\chi}\) be the chosen type-index
set in that setting. Assume the wild enhancement (H2) and wild
block-functor datum (H3) of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1a-conditional}, including
presentable stable categories \(\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}}\),
\(\mathcal D_\tau\), exact colimit-preserving fully faithful
functors \(\Phi_\tau\), and specified categorical decompositions
\[
 \mathcal C\simeq\prod_\tau\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}},
 \qquad \mathcal D\simeq\prod_\tau\mathcal D_\tau.
\]
The notation for the arithmetic source and spectral support category
below includes their supplied identifications with \(\mathcal C\)
and \(\mathcal D\). The componentwise functor
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi}=\prod_\tau\Phi_\tau\) exists
under these data. A type list alone supplies neither these
categorical products nor a geometric coproduct decomposition of
an Emerton--Gee stack. The proposed formal components and their
singular support remain part of the enhancement and comparison data.

\subsection{The BH Block-Exhaustion Statement}

\begin{definition}[BH-exhausted block]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:def:BH-exhausted}
Fix \(p\in\{2,3\}\), \(\chi\) and \(\tau\). Let
\(\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}}\) be the specified presentable stable
realization of the representation block, and put
\(\mathcal D_\tau=\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}
(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi,\tau}_{GL_2})\).
For the chosen functor
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi,\tau}:\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}}
\to\mathcal D_\tau\), the block is BH-exhausted if that functor
is essentially surjective. Generation by spectral objects alone is
not this condition; their images through the functor must be identified.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BH block exhaustion under the complete comparison data]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:thm:main}
Fix \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and \(\chi\). Assume the wild-enhancement
and block-functor data of
Conjectures~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement} and
\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:wild-block-functor}, including exact
colimit-preserving full faithfulness and compatible categorical
decompositions. Assume further:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(A)] For each block, a compact object
\(P_\tau\in\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}}\), an actual spectral
object \(Q_\tau\in\mathcal D_\tau\), and an equivalence
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi,\tau}(P_\tau)\simeq Q_\tau\),
as required by Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conj:BH-packaging}.
The proposed \(Q_\tau\) is the derived structure sheaf of the BH
closed point, with the stated singular support.
\item[(B)] The actual arithmetic extension-class comparison of
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conj:Paskunas-wild-ext} is supplied
for these objects and functors.
\item[(C)] The category \(\mathcal D_\tau\) is compactly generated,
\(Q_\tau\) is compact, and
\(\mathcal D_\tau^c=\operatorname{thick}(Q_\tau)\), as in
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conj:wild-compact-generation}.
\end{itemize}
Then every specified block is BH-exhausted, and the componentwise
functor on the supplied categorical decompositions is an equivalence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Condition (C) makes \(Q_\tau\) a localizing generator: its
localizing closure contains the thick closure and every colimit of
compact objects. Condition (A) places it in the chosen functor's
image. Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:lifted-generators}
therefore makes the block functor an equivalence, and the supplied
categorical decompositions assemble these componentwise.
Condition (B) retains the arithmetic extension compatibility.
Full faithfulness in all degrees comes from the block-functor datum,
not from a degree-one comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[why F1.a.3 is the smallest residual]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:rem:smallest-residual}
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a} records the following local residuals
for F1.a:
F1.a.1 (block exhaustion at \(p\geq 5\)), F1.a.2 (wild-EG
enhancement at \(p\in\{2,3\}\)), the wild block functor datum,
and F1.a.3. The explicit finite-algebra calculations in
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion} remain available. Their
transport to arithmetic blocks requires the local-model identifications,
class maps and generator comparisons stated below; a type list or an
equality of dimensions supplies none of these maps.
\end{remark}

\section{Proof Reduction and Source Comparison}
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:table}

The following reductions distinguish type enumeration, extension
classes, geometric components and compact generation.

\begin{description}
\item[\textbf{BH tame-wild enumeration at \(p=2\).}]
\emph{Obstruction.} Enumerate Bushnell--Kutzko types in
\(\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{2,\chi}\). Show that wild types at \(p=2\)
are classified in conductor \(\leq N\) by the unramified principal
series, the tame supercuspidal, the wild supercuspidal types of
bounded conductor,
and the spherical (Iwahori-fixed) type absorbing the
Ext\({}^{1}\)-extension of
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2}.
\emph{Calculation.} The BH classification
(Bushnell--Henniart 2006 \S 11--13) gives the exact list; the wild
conductor-exponent bound is \(a(\chi)+2\), cf.\ BH \S 12.1.
\emph{Obstruction: (A), the BH type-block comparison conjecture.}

\item[\textbf{BH tame-wild enumeration at \(p=3\).}]
\emph{Obstruction.} Enumerate Bushnell--Kutzko types in
\(\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{3,\chi}\). Show that the wild primary block
at \(p=3\) has three restricted-weight simples \(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2}\) with
the \(A_{2}\)-linear-quiver Ext\({}^{1}\)-structure of
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3}.
\emph{Required comparison.} Identify the proposed three BH types with the
three restricted-weight simples; the two quiver edges
\(\alpha_{01}\in\mathrm{Ext}^{1}(L_{0},L_{1})\),
\(\alpha_{12}\in\mathrm{Ext}^{1}(L_{1},L_{2})\) lift to two
non-split extensions in the category, each corresponding to a
derived-structure fibre in the wild-enhanced EG component.
\emph{Residual: (B), the Paskunas wild \(\mathrm{Ext}^{1}\)-compatibility.}

\item[\textbf{Essential surjectivity via type-by-type exhaustion.}] \emph{Obstruction.} Fix a hypothetical
\(\mathcal{F}\in\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}})\)
not in the essential image of \(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi}\).
Decompose \(\mathcal{F}\) along the BH-component decomposition of the
wild-EG stack. Each summand \(\mathcal{F}_{\tau}\) lives on a single
component \(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi,\tau}_{GL_{2}}\).
\emph{Calculation.} By (C), \(\mathcal{F}_{\tau}\) is a colimit of shifts of
the derived structure sheaf of \(x_{\tau}\); by (A), each such shift
is in the image of \(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi,\tau}\); hence
\(\mathcal{F}_{\tau}\) itself is, and the full \(\mathcal{F}\) is.
Contradiction.
\emph{Residual: (C), wild-EG compact-generator identification.}

\item[\textbf{Comparison with the wild-EG decomposition of Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a}.}] \emph{Obstruction.} Does the BH-component
decomposition coincide with the Bushnell--Henniart
fibre-correspondence decomposition of
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement}(3)? \emph{Calculation.}
A comparison must identify both indexing sets with
\(\mathbf T^{\mathrm{BH}}_{p,\chi}\) and construct the proposed
derived-fibre identifications
(\(1\) at \(p=2\) matching
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2},
\(2\) at \(p=3\) matching
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3}).
The local model and its comparison remain additional inputs.

\item[\textbf{Unconditional closure test.}]
\emph{Obstruction.} Can (A), (B), (C) collectively close on existing
primary literature? \emph{Calculation.} (A) is known for \(p\geq 5\) by Paskunas
2013 in the Breuil--Mezard framework; its transport to \(p\in\{2,3\}\)
via Bushnell--Henniart types requires a direct match of types to
structure sheaves not yet stated. (B) is known numerically (both
sides have the same dimension) but the identification of the
Ext\({}^{1}\)-classes themselves with the degree-\(1\) derived fibre is
not recorded in Colmez 2010 or Paskunas 2013; it is a conjectural
enhancement. (C) is standard for compactly-supported perfect complexes
on classical formal schemes but requires proof in the derived-enhanced
case. None of the three is closable from currently-stated primary
literature; all three are individually tractable.
\emph{Final obstruction: (A), (B), (C), three named conjectures.}
\end{description}

The source comparison for each reduction step is recorded in
\S\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:residuals-traces}. The first four analyses
close conditionally on (A), (B), (C); the fifth identifies the three
as the minimal conjectural set.

\section{Bushnell--Henniart Tame-Wild Types at \(p=2\)}
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:BH-p2}

\subsection{The Classification}

\begin{proposition}[Bushnell--Henniart 2006 classification at \(p=2\)]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:prop:BH-types-p2}
Fix a central character
\(\chi\colon\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{\times}\to k^{\times}\) and a conductor
bound \(N\). The bounded set
\(\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{2,\chi}\) of Bushnell--Kutzko types with
central character \(\chi\) and conductor exponent at most \(N\)
consists of three sorts of types:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Unramified principal-series types.} One type per
unramified character \(\psi\colon\mathbb{Q}_{2}^{\times}\to k^{\times}\)
with \(\psi|_{\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{\times}}\) square-rooting \(\chi\). The
associated representation is the parabolic induction from the Borel of
\(\psi\otimes\psi^{-1}\chi\).
\item \textbf{Tame supercuspidal types.} One type per tame character
\(\psi\) of a quadratic unramified extension \(\mathbb{Q}_{2}(\sqrt{u})\)
with \(u\in\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{\times}\setminus(\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{\times})^{2}\),
inducing to a supercuspidal representation of \(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{2})\).
\item \textbf{Wild supercuspidal types.} One type per admissible wild
character of a ramified quadratic extension \(E/\mathbb{Q}_{2}\), with
conductor exponent in the range imposed by \(\chi\) and by \(n\leq N\).
\end{enumerate}
The bounded set is finite:
\(|\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{2,\chi}|<\infty\), bounded in terms of
\(N\) and \(a(\chi)\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Source]
Bushnell--Henniart 2006 \emph{The Local Langlands Conjecture for
\(GL(2)\)}, Grundlehren~335, Springer. Chapter~11 for the principal series
and tame supercuspidal classification; Chapter~12 for the wild
supercuspidal classification and conductor exponents at \(p=2\);
Chapter~13 for the counting formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[the Ext\({}^{1}\)-carrying type at \(p=2\)]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:rem:ext1-type-p2}
The proposed degree-one contribution at two requires an actual
arithmetic block and extension class, as in
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2}.
In characteristic two, trivial and sign characters coincide.
Their names therefore do not identify two distinct constituents of
a parabolic induction. Locating the desired class in a principal
series block, and any vanishing assertion for supercuspidal blocks,
requires a calculation in those specific coefficient categories.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Matching to Spectral Components}

\begin{proposition}[type-to-component match at \(p=2\)]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:prop:type-to-component-p2}
Assume the BH type-block comparison
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conj:BH-packaging} and the wild-EG enhancement
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement}. Then each
\(\tau\in\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{2,\chi}\) corresponds to a unique
component \(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},2,\chi,\tau}_{GL_{2}}\) of the
wild-enhanced EG stack, with:
\begin{itemize}
\item Unramified principal-series type \(\tau\): classical component
indexed by a reducible residual Galois representation \(\rho\) with
\(\det\rho=\chi\cdot\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\); for the trivial \(\psi=\mathbf{1}\),
the component carries the degree-\(1\) derived fibre of dimension \(1\)
(the wild-EG enhancement).
\item Tame supercuspidal type \(\tau\): classical component, no derived
enhancement; corresponds to an irreducible residual \(\rho\) induced
from a tame character of a quadratic unramified extension.
\item Wild supercuspidal type \(\tau\): classical component, no derived
enhancement; corresponds to an irreducible residual \(\rho\) induced
from a wild character of a quadratic ramified extension.
\end{itemize}
The type-to-component map is a bijection.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\textbf{Step 1: residual-Galois match.} Each BH type \(\tau\) has an
associated \(L\)-parameter
\(\phi_{\tau}\colon W_{\mathbb{Q}_{2}}\to GL_{2}(k)\) by the local
Langlands correspondence of Bushnell--Henniart 2006 \S 18, with
determinant \(\det\phi_{\tau}=\chi\cdot\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\).
The residual representation \(\rho_{\tau}\) is the reduction mod-\(p\) of
\(\phi_{\tau}\). The EG component
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},2,\chi,\rho_{\tau}}_{GL_{2}}\) is the formal
neighbourhood of \(\rho_{\tau}\).
\textbf{Step 2: enhanced fibre at reducible \(\rho\).}
The local-model hypothesis must supply the proposed extension class,
its arithmetic coefficient carrier, and its comparison to the
one-dimensional derived direction. This is the input asked for in
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2}; it is not
a consequence of a dimension count or of distinct trivial and sign
characters in characteristic two.
\textbf{Step 3: semisimplicity at irreducible \(\rho\).} For \(\tau\) a
tame or wild supercuspidal, \(\rho_{\tau}\) is irreducible; the
deformation functor is smooth (Emerton--Gee arXiv:1908.07185 \S 6.2);
no derived enhancement is needed.
\textbf{Step 4: bijectivity.} Finiteness on both sides follows from
Bushnell--Henniart \S 13 (left-hand) and the connected-component
decomposition of \(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},2,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\)
(right-hand). The bijection is the local Langlands correspondence
for \(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{2})\) at the residual level.
\end{proof}

\section{Bushnell--Henniart Tame-Wild Types at \(p=3\)}
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:BH-p3}

\subsection{The Classification and the \(A_{2}\)-Quiver Structure}

\begin{proposition}[Bushnell--Henniart 2006 classification at \(p=3\)]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:prop:BH-types-p3}
Fix
\(\chi\colon\mathbb{Z}_{3}^{\times}\to k^{\times}\) and a conductor
bound \(N\). The bounded set \(\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{3,\chi}\)
consists of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Unramified principal series.} Parabolically induced
from the Borel of \(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{3})\).
\item \textbf{Tame supercuspidals.} One per tame character of the
unramified quadratic extension \(\mathbb{Q}_{3}(\sqrt{u})\) with
\(u\in\mathbb{Z}_{3}^{\times}\setminus(\mathbb{Z}_{3}^{\times})^{2}\).
\item \textbf{Wild supercuspidals.} One per admissible wild character
of a ramified quadratic extension \(E/\mathbb{Q}_{3}\), with conductor
exponent \(a(\chi)+1\leq n\leq N\).
\item \textbf{Additional principal-block local-model hypothesis.}
Specify three arithmetic simples and an exact equivalence taking them
to the three simple right modules of
\(B=k(0\xrightarrow{a}1\xrightarrow{b}2)/(ab)\), in the coefficient category of
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3}.
This identification is additional data, not a consequence of a
classification of tame or wild types.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Source]
Bushnell--Henniart 2006 \S 11 (principal series), \S 12 (tame
supercuspidal), \S 13 (wild supercuspidal), \S 15 (wild types at
small primes). The conductor bound cuts the infinite admissible
character families to a finite Bushnell--Kutzko set.
The fourth item is a separate local-model hypothesis. The cited type
classification does not supply that equivalence or its extension maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[the three-vertex extension pattern]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:prop:A2-quiver-p3}
For right modules over
\(B=k(0\xrightarrow a1\xrightarrow b2)/(ab)\), the adjacent
extension groups \(\operatorname{Ext}^1_B(S_0,S_1)\) and
\(\operatorname{Ext}^1_B(S_1,S_2)\) are one-dimensional. Their
Yoneda product is nonzero in \(\operatorname{Ext}^2_B(S_0,S_2)=k\),
whereas \(\operatorname{Ext}^1_B(S_0,S_2)=0\).
An exact arithmetic block equivalence taking its three simples to
these right modules would transport these statements and products.
Such an equivalence is an additional hypothesis.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The projective resolution in
Example~\ref{v4-arith:wild:three-vertex} computes the adjacent and
degree-two groups and their product. The length-one path count
in Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:wild:path-count} gives the vanishing
between non-adjacent simples. An exact equivalence transports
extensions and their Yoneda compositions.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Matching to Spectral Components}

\begin{proposition}[type-to-component match at \(p=3\)]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:prop:type-to-component-p3}
Assume (A) and (B). Each
\(\tau\in\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{3,\chi}\) corresponds to a unique
component \(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},3,\chi,\tau}_{GL_{2}}\) of the
wild-enhanced EG stack, with the principal-block types carrying a
degree-\(1\) derived fibre of dimension \(2\) (per
Remark~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:wild-EG-p3}), and all other types carrying
no derived enhancement.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\textbf{Step 1: classical-component match.} Same as
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:prop:type-to-component-p2} Step~1,
replacing \(p=2\) by \(p=3\). Each type has an \(L\)-parameter
\(\phi_{\tau}\) by BH 2006 \S 18; the residual \(\rho_{\tau}\)
determines the component.
\textbf{Step 2: principal-block derived-fibre match.} For \(\tau\) a
principal-block type, \(\rho_{\tau}\) is reducible with constituents
matching the Steinberg-decomposed restricted-weight simples
\(L_{0},L_{1},L_{2}\). The two proposed arithmetic Ext\({}^{1}\)-classes in
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3} must be identified by the local-model map with a
two-dimensional Ext\({}^{1}\)-space in the deformation functor of
\(\rho_{\tau}\). By Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement}(2),
this is realised as the degree-\(1\) derived fibre of dimension \(2\) in
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},3,\chi,\tau}_{GL_{2}}\).
\textbf{Step 3: semisimplicity at irreducible \(\rho\).} Same as the
\(p=2\) case. All supercuspidal types give smooth classical components.
\textbf{Step 4: bijectivity.} Same as the \(p=2\) case.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Exhaustion Argument}

\begin{theorem}[Exhaustion of a specified BH block]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:thm:single-block-exhaustion}
Under all the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:thm:main},
the chosen functor \(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi,\tau}:
\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}}\to\mathcal D_\tau\) is an equivalence.

\begin{proof}
Its image contains the generator \(Q_\tau\) by (A) and (C).
The exact colimit-preserving full faithfulness supplied by (H3)
allows Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:lifted-generators}
to apply. The specified degree-one comparison (B) is preserved;
it is not used as a substitute for higher mapping-spectrum data.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Assembly on the specified categorical blocks]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:cor:BH-assembly}
Under all the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:thm:main},
the componentwise functor on the supplied block decompositions is
an equivalence of presentable stable categories.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The inverse is the componentwise inverse of the block equivalences,
transported through the specified categorical decompositions.
\end{proof}

in Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2}

\section{The Three Conditional Inputs}
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conjectures}

\subsection{(A) BH Type-Block Comparison}

\begin{conjecture}[BH type-block comparison]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conj:BH-packaging}
\ClaimStatusConjectured. Fix \(p\in\{2,3\}\), \(\chi\), and
\(\tau\in\mathbf{T}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{p,\chi}\). The projective indecomposable
generator \(P_{\tau}\in\mathrm{Block}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{\tau}\) is a compact
object of \(\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})])_{\chi}\),
and its image under \(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi}\) is the derived
structure sheaf of the BH closed point
\(x_{\tau}\in\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi,\tau}_{GL_{2}}\).
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[evidence for the BH type-block comparison]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:rem:BH-packaging-evidence}
Three lines of evidence:
\begin{itemize}
\item Paskunas 2013 establishes the analogous statement for \(p\geq 5\)
(Publ.~IHES~118 \S 6): each projective indecomposable of
\(\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{sm},\chi}_{GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})}\) maps under
Colmez--Montreal to the structure sheaf of a residual-Galois closed
point. At \(p\in\{2,3\}\), the Paskunas argument requires modification
because the block structure is more complex; the comparison at
\(p\in\{2,3\}\) is the extension of Paskunas' result to the BH type
list, with the same projective-to-structure-sheaf identification.
\item Bushnell--Henniart 2006 \S 18 establishes the type-to-parameter
match: each BH type \(\tau\) has an associated \(L\)-parameter
\(\phi_{\tau}\). The projective generator \(P_{\tau}\) is the type's
indicator-character-module; the BH comparison conjecture asserts that this
module maps to the derived structure sheaf of the closed point
\(x_{\tau}\) determined by \(\phi_{\tau}\pmod{p}\).
\item Compactness of \(P_{\tau}\): the block is a finitely-generated
module over its local endomorphism algebra (BH 2006 \S 12.3), hence
\(P_{\tau}\) is compact in the derived category of the block.
\end{itemize}
The conjectural content is the precise image identification (second
bullet), not compactness or finiteness.
\end{remark}

\subsection{(C) Wild-EG Compact-Generator Identification}

\begin{conjecture}[Wild spectral compact generation]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:conj:wild-compact-generation}
For the specified wild block, \(\mathcal D_\tau\) is compactly
generated. Its proposed derived closed-point structure sheaf
\(Q_\tau\) is an actual compact object of the nilpotent-support
category and \(\mathcal D_\tau^c=\operatorname{thick}(Q_\tau)\).
The thick closure includes shifts, finite cones and retracts.
Its localizing closure is consequently the whole category.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Support and the generator]
\label{v4-arith:f1a3-BH:rem:wild-compact-generation-evidence}
A closed-point structure sheaf does not generate an arbitrary
ambient derived category. For example, in \(D(k[t])\), localization
at \(t\) kills \(k=k[t]/(t)\) and its entire localizing closure,
but does not kill \(k[t]\). Thus \(k\) cannot generate \(D(k[t])\).
The chosen support category, compactness of the proposed image, and
its thick generation in (C) are essential data. Formal neighbourhoods
or a derived enhancement alone do not verify these assertions for
the arithmetic target.
\end{remark}

fully faithful colimit-preserving functor and its lifted compact
