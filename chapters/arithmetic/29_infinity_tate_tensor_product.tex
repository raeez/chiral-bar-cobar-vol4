\chapter{The \(\infty\)-Categorical Tate Restricted Tensor Product}
\label{v4-arith:tate-tensor}

The restricted-tensor assertion F2.c is a formal assembly theorem.
Its inputs are stable presentable categories with specified
operadic structures and the actual pointing
maps of Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}(D2). Finite tensor
products of these maps form an operadic diagram. HA~3.2.3.1
constructs its filtered colimit on the underlying categorical
colimit. No arithmetic input is used in that construction.

Applying it to arithmetic Iwahori categories still requires the
local \(E_2\)-structures and the specified pointing maps. Neither
an invariant vector nor a central idempotent alone supplies those
maps. The assembly theorem does not construct geometric collision
comparisons or an identification with an independently defined
global Hecke category.

\section{Statement of (F2.c)}
\label{v4-arith:tate:statement}

\subsection{Setup and Definition}

Fix a small one-coloured \(\infty\)-operad \(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}\) (the chief cases
are \(\mathcal{O}=E_{n}\) for \(n\geq 1\)). Fix an indexing set \(V\) of
\emph{places}: in the target application \(V=|\overline{C}|\) is the
set of places of \(\overline{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}}\), but the
statement is place-agnostic.

Write \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\) for the
\(\infty\)-category of stable presentable \(\infty\)-categories and
colimit-preserving functors. Its tensor product represents
functors preserving colimits in each input. The tensor unit is
\(\mathrm{Sp}\), the \(\infty\)-category of spectra, and
\(\mathbb S\in\mathrm{Sp}\) is the sphere spectrum. In particular,
an empty tensor of categories means \(\mathrm{Sp}\).

\begin{setup}[F2.c data]
\label{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}
\leavevmode
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(D1)] For each \(v\in V\), a stable presentable \(\infty\)-category
\(\mathcal{C}_{v}\in\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\) equipped
with an \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal structure compatible with colimits in
each variable: \(\mathcal{C}_{v}^{\otimes}\to\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}\)
exhibits \(\mathcal{C}_{v}\) as an \(\mathcal{O}\)-algebra in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\).
\item[(D2)] A cofinite subset \(V^{\mathrm{sph}}\subseteq V\) and,
for each \(v\in V^{\mathrm{sph}}\), a specified morphism
\[
 \eta_v:\mathrm{Sp}\longrightarrow\mathcal C_v
 \quad\text{in}\quad
 \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal O}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}).
\]
Write \(u_v=\eta_v(\mathbb S)\). The morphism includes compatibility
with every operation, including nullary operations, and all their
coherences. Throughout this chapter, pointed spherical data means
these maps, not merely their values on \(\mathbb S\).

In the unital
\(E_n\) cases, \(n\geq1\), necessarily
\(u_v\simeq\mathbb 1_{\mathcal C_v}\). A non-unit central idempotent
under \(\mathbb 1_{\mathcal C_v}\) does not give such a map into
\(\mathcal C_v\). A construction using it would require a different
unital target or a different notion of morphism.
\end{enumerate}
Write \(\mathrm{Fin}(V)\) for the filtered poset of finite subsets of
\(V\) ordered by inclusion, and \(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\) for
the cofinal subposet consisting of finite \(S\subseteq V\) with
\(V\setminus S\subseteq V^{\mathrm{sph}}\).
\end{setup}

\begin{definition}[Tate restricted tensor product]
\label{v4-arith:tate:def:restricted-tensor}
Under Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}, the \emph{Tate restricted
tensor product} of \(\{\mathcal{C}_{v}\}_{v}\) with pointed spherical
data \(\{(\eta_{v},u_{v})\}_{v\in V^{\mathrm{sph}}}\) is the colimit
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:tate:eq:restricted-def}
\bigotimes_{v\in V}^{\prime}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})
\;:=\;
\varinjlim_{S\in\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)}
\Bigl(\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal{C}_{v}\Bigr)
\end{equation}
in \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\). The transition
morphism for \(S\subseteq S'\) uses the pointing at each
\(v\in S'\setminus S\):
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:tate:eq:unital-extension}
\iota_{S\subseteq S'}\;:\;
\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal{C}_{v}
\;\xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{id}\otimes\bigotimes_{v\in S'\setminus S} \eta_{v}\;}\;
\bigotimes_{v\in S'}\mathcal{C}_{v},
\qquad
X\mapsto X\otimes\Bigl(\bigotimes_{v\in S'\setminus S}u_{v}\Bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

The right-hand side uses the Lurie tensor product in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}\) of HA~4.8.1. It restricts to a
symmetric monoidal structure on \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\)
by HA~4.8.2.18.

\begin{theorem}[F2.c: \(\infty\)-categorical Tate restricted \(\mathcal{O}\)-tensor]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Fix a small coherent \(\infty\)-operad
\(\mathcal O^{\otimes}\). This includes \(E_n\) for \(n\geq1\)
and \(E_\infty\). Under Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data},
the restricted tensor product~\eqref{v4-arith:tate:eq:restricted-def}
carries a canonical \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal structure such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(M1)] For each \(S\in\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\), the
canonical map
\[
\iota_{S}\;:\;\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal{C}_{v}\;\longrightarrow\;\bigotimes_{v\in V}^{\prime}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})
\]
is an \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal functor.
\item[(M2)] If \(V\setminus\{v\}\subseteq V^{\mathrm{sph}}\), the
canonical functor
\(\mathcal{C}_{v}\to\bigotimes'_{w\in V}(\mathcal{C}_{w},u_{w})\)
is \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal. For a general \(v\), the same
construction requires specified \(\mathcal O\)-algebra maps
\(\mathrm{Sp}\to\mathcal C_w\) at the finitely many remaining
places \(w\in V\setminus(V^{\mathrm{sph}}\cup\{v\})\).
\item[(M3)] Fix the full diagram of finite tensor products and
specified pointing maps, and fix its underlying colimit cocone in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\). The space of lifts of
this entire cocone, including all higher coherences, to a colimit
cocone in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal O}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}})\)
is contractible.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Applied with \(\mathcal{O}=E_{2}\) and
\(\mathcal{C}_{v}=\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_{2},v}\) and
the pointing maps of (D2), this proves the formal transport step
(F2.c) in Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:thm:F2-reduction}. Both the local
Iwahori \(E_2\)-structures and any identification
\(|0\rangle_v\simeq\eta_v(\mathbb S)\) remain required inputs.

\subsection{Tensor Products and Factorization Homology}
The tensor-product construction of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}
uses Lurie~HA~4.8 and the integral \(E_n\)-operads discussed by Fresse.
Dunn additivity (HA~5.1.2.4; Dunn~1988), Ayala--Francis factorization
homology, and Ginot's account of factorization algebras provide related
descriptions of the monoidal structures
(Section~\ref{v4-arith:tate:verification}). Applying these descriptions
requires the same units, coefficient category and colimit conventions.

\section{Obstructions and Their Resolutions}
\label{v4-arith:tate:table}

Six questions isolate the ingredients of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}: operadic insertion, filtered
colimits, discrete factorization, additivity, cocone uniqueness
and the cofinite pointing condition.

\subsection{The Pointing Required for Unit Insertion}

\begin{obstruction}[operadic insertion]
Does a distinguished object \(u_v\), or a central idempotent under
the unit, suffice to make insertion an \(\mathcal O\)-monoidal
functor?
\end{obstruction}

\begin{correction}[actual pointing maps]
The required datum is the morphism \(\eta_v\) in
Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}(D2). Tensoring these morphisms
with identities gives the insertion functors, including their
nullary and higher compatibilities. For unital \(E_n\), a strong
\(E_n\)-monoidal functor preserves the unit, so its value on
\(\mathbb S\) cannot be a non-unit central idempotent. For a nonzero
field \(k\), the object \((k,0)\) of \(\mathcal D(k\times k)\)
is compact, central and idempotent under the unit \((k,k)\).
Insertion of \((k,0)\) from \(\mathrm{Sp}\) is not unital monoidal.
Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:tate:lem:unital-incl-is-monoidal} uses the
specified maps, not an implication from central idempotence.
\end{correction}

\subsection{\(\mathcal{O}\)-Algebra Structure under Filtered Colimits}

\begin{obstruction}[filtered colimit of \(\mathcal{O}\)-algebras]
\eqref{v4-arith:tate:eq:restricted-def} is a filtered colimit in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\). Does the
\(\mathcal{O}\)-algebra structure propagate through such a colimit?
\end{obstruction}

\begin{correction}[colimit preservation]
Lurie~HA~3.2.3.1, applied to the \(\infty\)-operad
\(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}\) and the symmetric monoidal
\(\infty\)-category \((\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}},\otimes)\),
which admits filtered colimits preserved by tensoring in each variable:
the forgetful functor
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\to\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\)
preserves filtered colimits. Hence the colimit
\(\varinjlim_{S}(\otimes_{v\in S}\mathcal{C}_{v})\) taken in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\)
coincides with the colimit in \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\),
and the \(\mathcal{O}\)-algebra structure is preserved. Detailed in
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:colim-preserves-O-alg}.
\end{correction}

\subsection{Factorization for Disjoint Subsets of \(V\)}

\begin{obstruction}[factorization axiom]
Does the finite tensor diagram underlying
\eqref{v4-arith:tate:eq:restricted-def} respect disjoint unions
of place labels, and what geometric data does this supply?
\end{obstruction}

\begin{correction}[discreteness of \(V\)]
The associator identifies the tensor for \(S\sqcup T\) with the
tensor of the two separate values whenever \(S\cap T=\varnothing\).
Spherical-unit insertions respect these identifications on the
inclusions for which they are defined. This is the discrete
factorization of Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:cor:no-nontrivial-factorization}.
It specifies no position diagonal or normal coefficient. A comparison
with a geometric factorization system must supply those data separately.
\end{correction}

\subsection{Dunn Additivity and the Restricted Tensor Product}

\begin{obstruction}[Dunn additivity compatibility]
For \(\mathcal{O}=E_{n_{1}}\) and \(\mathcal{O}'=E_{n_{2}}\) with
\(n_{1}+n_{2}=n\), Dunn additivity (HA~5.1.2.4) gives
\(E_{n_{1}}\otimes E_{n_{2}}\simeq E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\), with the tensor
product of \(\infty\)-operads. The
restricted tensor product of \(E_{n_{1}}\)-categories composed with
the restricted tensor product of \(E_{n_{2}}\)-categories should give
the restricted tensor product of \(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-categories. Does
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} respect this composition?
\end{obstruction}

\begin{correction}[operad-variable functoriality]
Dunn additivity concerns the operad \(E_n\) itself (HA~5.1.2.4).
For an operad product \(\mathcal O\otimes\mathcal O'\), the
restricted-tensor construction agrees with its iterated form.
Both supplied structures and their interchange coherences must
be retained. The corresponding algebra equivalence identifies
the finite tensor diagrams and their filtered colimits.
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:dunn-compat} gives the
comparison for the little-cubes operads.
\end{correction}

\subsection{Cofinality Invariance of the \(\mathcal{O}\)-Structure}

\begin{obstruction}[uniqueness modulo cofinal filtration]
The proof of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}(M3) claims uniqueness
of the \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal structure up to contractible choice.
Does this uniqueness hold unconditionally, or only modulo the choice
of cofinal filtration \(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\)?
\end{obstruction}

\begin{correction}[cofinality invariance]
A cofinal replacement of the filtered indexing category does not
change the colimit of its operadic diagram. The resulting
equivalence is the one compatible with the complete colimit
cocones. Colimit cocones are initial objects in the appropriate
cocone category, so their space of choices is contractible.
This uses the universal property of colimits, not an
\(\infty\)-topos structure on \(\mathrm{Cat}_\infty\).
See Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:uniqueness}.
\end{correction}

\subsection{Ramified Places without a Spherical Unit}

\begin{obstruction}[ramified archimedean places]
Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data} asks for a distinguished
spherical unit \(u_{v}\) at \emph{almost all} places \(v\in V\). At a
finite number of ramified places (for \(GL_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\) typically
the archimedean place and a finite set of primes where the Iwahori
level is nontrivial), no spherical unit need exist. Does
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} still apply?
\end{obstruction}

\begin{correction}[cofinite spherical locus]
Every \(S\in\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\) contains the finite
complement \(V\setminus V^{\mathrm{sph}}\). Thus every newly added
place has a specified pointing map, and the finite-stage diagram
is defined without choosing pointing maps at the remaining
places. This does not supply every singleton map: the precise
condition is (M2). An archimedean invariant vector does not by
itself verify (D2). An actual operadic pointing is still required
before that place can be inserted.
\end{correction}

\section{\(\infty\)-Operadic Preliminaries}
\label{v4-arith:tate:operadic-prelim}

The proof of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} draws on three
ingredients from Lurie~HA and Fresse, recorded here with their
precise references. Numbering follows the 2017 version of
Lurie's \emph{Higher Algebra}.

\subsection{Symmetric Monoidal Structure on \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:tate:prelim-prl}

\begin{theorem}[HA~4.8.1.15, HA~4.8.2.18]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:prl-symmetric-monoidal}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere The \(\infty\)-category
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}\) of presentable \(\infty\)-categories and
colimit-preserving functors carries a canonical symmetric monoidal
structure. Its tensor product is characterized by the universal property
\[
\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{L}}(\mathcal{C}\otimes\mathcal{D},\mathcal{E})
\;\simeq\;
\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{L},\mathrm{L}}(\mathcal{C}\times\mathcal{D},\mathcal{E})
\]
(colimit-preserving functors of tensor products \(=\)
bi-colimit-preserving functors of products). The full subcategory
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\) of stable presentable
\(\infty\)-categories is closed under this tensor product and carries
a canonical symmetric monoidal structure with unit \(\mathrm{Sp}\) (the
\(\infty\)-category of spectra).
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[HA~4.8.1.24 and HA~4.8.2.18]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:prl-admits-colimits}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere The \(\infty\)-category
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\) admits small colimits, and
the tensor product \(\otimes\) preserves colimits separately in each
variable. In particular, \(\otimes\) preserves filtered colimits in
each variable.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Algebras over an \(\infty\)-Operad in a Presentable Symmetric Monoidal \(\infty\)-Category}
\label{v4-arith:tate:prelim-alg}

\begin{theorem}[HA~3.2.3.1 and 3.2.3.5]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:alg-O-presentable}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere For a small coherent \(\infty\)-operad
\(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}\) and a presentable symmetric monoidal
\(\infty\)-category \(\mathcal{E}^{\otimes}\) with
\(\otimes_{\mathcal{E}}\) preserving colimits separately in each
variable:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{E})\) is presentable;
\item the forgetful functor
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{E})\to\mathcal{E}\) preserves
sifted colimits (and in particular filtered colimits).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Stability of \(\mathcal E\) does not imply stability of its algebra
category. For example, for a nonzero field \(k\), the initial object
of \(\mathrm{Alg}_{E_1}(\mathcal D(k))\) is \(k\) and its terminal
object is the zero algebra. They are not equivalent, so this
category is not pointed and cannot be stable.

For the restricted tensor product, we need only filtered colimits.
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:prl-admits-colimits} supplies these
in \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\), compatibly with tensor
products. Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:filtered-colim-operadic}
then constructs the colimit of the algebra diagram on its underlying
colimit. This application uses no presentability assertion about
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\) itself.

\subsection{\(E_{n}\)-Operad and Integral Structure}
\label{v4-arith:tate:prelim-En}

\begin{theorem}[Fresse~2017 \emph{Homotopy of Operads} I.4.1; Lurie HA~5.1.0.4]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:En-operad}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere For each \(n\geq 0\), let
\(E_n^{\otimes}\) be the little \(n\)-cubes \(\infty\)-operad.
Its \(k\)-ary operations are parametrized by the space
\(\mathrm{Conf}_{k}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\) of configurations of \(k\) ordered
little \(n\)-cubes in the unit \(n\)-cube. This \(\infty\)-operad is
coherent in the sense of HA~3.3.1.1. The standard inclusion
\(E_n^{\otimes}\to E_{n+1}^{\otimes}\) induces restriction from
\(E_{n+1}\)-algebras to \(E_n\)-algebras. The relation with iterated
algebra structures is the Dunn additivity equivalence below,
not a forgetful morphism \(E_{n+1}\to E_n\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Dunn additivity; HA~5.1.2.4; Dunn~1988 JPAA~50]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:dunn-additivity}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere For any \(n_{1},n_{2}\geq 0\),
\[
E_{n_{1}}^{\otimes}\otimes E_{n_{2}}^{\otimes}\;\simeq\;E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}^{\otimes}
\]
as \(\infty\)-operads, where \(\otimes\) is the tensor product of
\(\infty\)-operads (HA~2.2.5.6). In particular, an
\(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-algebra in \(\mathcal{E}\) is the same datum as an
\(E_{n_{1}}\)-algebra in \(E_{n_{2}}\)-algebras in \(\mathcal{E}\).
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Compatibility of a Specified Pointing}
\label{v4-arith:tate:prelim-central}

\begin{theorem}[operadic pointing compatibility]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:unit-central}
Let \(\eta:\mathrm{Sp}\to\mathcal C\) be a morphism in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal O}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}})\).
For every \(\mathcal O\)-operation, its compatibility with
\(\eta\), together with all substitution coherences, is part of
this morphism. For unital \(E_n\), \(n\geq1\), its nullary
compatibility gives \(\eta(\mathbb S)\simeq\mathbb 1_{\mathcal C}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A morphism of operadic algebras commutes with the operation maps
and their coherences. The nullary operation of the canonical
unital \(E_n\)-algebra \(\mathrm{Sp}\) is the identity
\(\mathrm{Sp}\to\mathrm{Sp}\). That of \(\mathcal C\) is its unit
functor. Compatibility identifies \(\eta\) with that unit functor
and gives the stated equivalence on \(\mathbb S\).
\end{proof}

This is the pointing compatibility used in
Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:tate:lem:unital-incl-is-monoidal}. It asserts
no mapping-space identity involving the identity functor as an
object of \(\mathcal C\).

\subsection{Filtered Colimits of \(\infty\)-Operadic Algebras}

\begin{proposition}[HA~3.2.3.1]
\label{v4-arith:tate:prop:filtered-colim-operadic}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere Let \(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}\) be a small
one-coloured coherent \(\infty\)-operad, and let
\(\mathcal{E}^{\otimes}\) be a symmetric monoidal \(\infty\)-category
admitting small filtered colimits. Suppose
\(\otimes_{\mathcal{E}}\) preserves these colimits in each variable.
Then the forgetful functor
\[
U_{\mathcal{O}}\;:\;\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{E})\;\longrightarrow\;\mathcal{E}
\]
preserves and creates filtered colimits. Concretely: a filtered
diagram \(F:I\to\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{E})\) has colimit
whose underlying object in \(\mathcal{E}\) is \(\varinjlim_{i}U_{\mathcal{O}}F(i)\),
with \(\mathcal{O}\)-algebra structure induced levelwise.
\end{proposition}

This is the mechanism of colimit-preservation
(\S\ref{v4-arith:tate:colim-preservation}).

\section{Unital Inclusion Functors}
\label{v4-arith:tate:unital-inclusion}

Inserting a spherical unit at a new place gives an
\(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal functor; the following lemma makes this precise.

\begin{lemma}[unit insertion is \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal]
\label{v4-arith:tate:lem:unital-incl-is-monoidal}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Let \(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}\) be a coherent
\(\infty\)-operad, and let \(\mathcal{C}_{1},\ldots,\mathcal{C}_{k}\)
and \(\mathcal{C}_{k+1},\ldots,\mathcal{C}_{m}\) be
\(\mathcal{O}\)-algebras in \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\)
for some \(k\leq m\). For \(i>k\) choose a spherical unit
\(u_{i}\in\mathcal{C}_{i}\) in the sense of
Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}(D2). Then the functor
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:tate:eq:unital-incl}
\iota_{\{1,\ldots,k\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}}\;:\;
\bigotimes_{i=1}^{k}\mathcal{C}_{i}\;\longrightarrow\;\bigotimes_{i=1}^{m}\mathcal{C}_{i}
\end{equation}
defined by inserting \(u_{i}\) at position \(i\) for \(i>k\) is an
\(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal functor.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The symmetric monoidal tensor product on
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal O}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}})\)
is induced by the ambient tensor product. The insertion is the
morphism
\[
 \bigotimes_{i\leq k}\mathcal C_i
 \simeq
 \Bigl(\bigotimes_{i\leq k}\mathcal C_i\Bigr)
       \otimes\mathrm{Sp}^{\otimes(m-k)}
 \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{id}\otimes\bigotimes_{i>k}\eta_i\ }
 \bigotimes_{i\leq m}\mathcal C_i.
\]
Every \(\eta_i\) is a specified morphism of \(\mathcal O\)-algebras,
so this is also such a morphism. In particular, writing \(A\) and
\(B\) for the source and target, respectively, each operation
\(c\in\mathcal O(r)\) satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:tate:eq:unital-incl-commute}
 \iota\circ\mu_c^A\simeq\mu_c^B\circ\iota^{\otimes r}.
\end{equation}

For \(r=0\) this is unit compatibility. The higher compatibilities
are inherited from the supplied morphisms and the symmetric
monoidal structure, rather than chosen independently for each
operation. Tensor associativity supplies the compatible
composites for every chain of finite subsets.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[restriction of \(\iota\)]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:iota-restriction}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Assume the hypotheses of
Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:tate:lem:unital-incl-is-monoidal}. For every
sub-triangle \(\{1,\ldots,k\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k'\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}\)
the composite \(\iota_{\{1,\ldots,k\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}}\) equals
\(\iota_{\{1,\ldots,k'\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}}\circ\iota_{\{1,\ldots,k\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k'\}}\)
as \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal functors under the comparison supplied
by tensor associativity and the fixed pointing maps. These
comparisons are coherent for longer chains.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Both sides are tensor products of the same identities and supplied
maps \(\eta_i\) at the same positions. The associativity and unit
coherences of the symmetric monoidal algebra category identify
these composites, compatibly with longer chains. This is the
specified coherent comparison, not an assertion that arbitrary
homotopies between the underlying functors form a contractible
space.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[two cells of the full diagram system]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:iota-2cells}
Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:tate:lem:unital-incl-is-monoidal} together with
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:iota-restriction} upgrades the system
\(\{\iota_{S\subseteq S'}\}_{S\subseteq S'}\) to a diagram
\[
\Phi_{\{\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v}\}}\;:\;\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\;\longrightarrow\;\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})
\]
covariant in the filtered poset ordered by inclusion. Its map for
\(S\subseteq S'\) inserts the specified spherical units in
\(S'\setminus S\).
This is the diagram on which
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:colim-preserves-O-alg} will
take colimits.
\end{remark}

\section{Colimit Preservation}
\label{v4-arith:tate:colim-preservation}

\begin{proposition}[\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\) of \(\mathcal{O}\)-categories is cocomplete under filtered colimits]
\label{v4-arith:tate:prop:colim-preserves-O-alg}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Let \(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}\) be a small
one-coloured coherent \(\infty\)-operad. For every small
filtered \(\infty\)-category \(I\) and every functor
\(\Phi:I\to\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\),
the colimit \(\varinjlim_{i}\Phi(i)\) exists in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\),
and its underlying presentable stable \(\infty\)-category is
\(\varinjlim_{i}(U_{\mathcal{O}}\Phi(i))\) in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\). Every \(\mathcal{O}\)-operation
on the colimit is computed levelwise: given
\(c\in\mathcal{O}(r)\), the operation
\[
\mu_{c}^{(\mathrm{colim})}\;:\;\prod_{j=1}^{r}\Bigl(\varinjlim_{i}\Phi(i)\Bigr)\;\longrightarrow\;\varinjlim_{i}\Phi(i)
\]
is the filtered colimit of the family
\(\{\mu_{c}^{(\Phi(i))}\}_{i}\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put \(\mathcal C_i=U_{\mathcal O}\Phi(i)\) and
\(\mathcal C=\varinjlim_i\mathcal C_i\) in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\).
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:prl-admits-colimits} gives
this colimit and preservation by tensoring. Hence
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:filtered-colim-operadic},
with \(\mathcal E=\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\), gives
an algebra colimit whose underlying object is \(\mathcal C\).

The operations can be seen directly. For \(r>0\), the diagonal
\(I\to I^r\) is cofinal because \(I\) is filtered. Therefore
\[
 \mathcal C^{\otimes r}
 \simeq\varinjlim_{(i_1,\ldots,i_r)\in I^r}
       \mathcal C_{i_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathcal C_{i_r}
 \simeq\varinjlim_{i\in I}\mathcal C_i^{\otimes r}.
\]
For \(c\in\mathcal O(r)\), take the colimit of
\(\mu_c^{(i)}:\mathcal C_i^{\otimes r}\to\mathcal C_i\).
The resulting map is the displayed operation on \(\mathcal C\),
viewed as a functor preserving colimits in each input.
Nullary operations pass through the coherent cocone from
\(\mathrm{Sp}\); the nerve of a filtered category is contractible.
Operadic composition and its homotopies pass through these same
colimit comparisons. Thus the construction retains the full
algebra structure, as asserted by HA~3.2.3.1.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[restricted tensor product as colimit in \(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}\)]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:rtp-as-colimit-in-alg}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere The diagram
\(\Phi_{\{\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v}\}}:\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\to\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\)
of
Remark~\ref{v4-arith:tate:rem:iota-2cells} admits a colimit in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\),
whose underlying presentable stable \(\infty\)-category is
\(\bigotimes'_{v\in V}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\) of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:def:restricted-tensor}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
\(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\) is a filtered \(\infty\)-poset
(union of finite subsets is again finite; the empty set, or any
initial element of \(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\), gives
non-emptiness). Apply
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:colim-preserves-O-alg}.
\end{proof}

\section{Proof of the Main Theorem}
\label{v4-arith:tate:main-proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}]

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Step 1 (construction of the diagram).}
Given the data of Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}, construct
the diagram
\[
\Phi\;:\;\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\;\longrightarrow\;\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}),
\qquad\Phi(S)\;:=\;\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal{C}_{v}
\]
with transition maps \(\iota_{S\subseteq S'}\) of
\eqref{v4-arith:tate:eq:unital-extension}. By
Lemma~\ref{v4-arith:tate:lem:unital-incl-is-monoidal}, each
\(\iota_{S\subseteq S'}\) is an \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal functor.
For every chain of inclusions, each composite tensors with the
same supplied maps \(\eta_v\) at the same positions. Coherence of
the tensor product gives all composition homotopies and their
compatibilities; the triangular identity is
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:iota-restriction}. Thus \(\Phi\) is a functor
from the filtered \(\infty\)-poset
\(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\) into
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\).

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Step 2 (existence of the \(\mathcal{O}\)-structured colimit).}
By
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:rtp-as-colimit-in-alg}, the colimit
\(\varinjlim_{S}\Phi(S)\) exists in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\),
and its underlying object in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\) is
\(\bigotimes'_{v\in V}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\) by
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:def:restricted-tensor}. This supplies the
\(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal structure promised in
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Step 3 (property (M1): each \(\iota_{S}\) is \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal).}
The canonical map
\(\iota_{S}:\Phi(S)\to\varinjlim_{S'}\Phi(S')\) is a morphism in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\)
by the universal property of the colimit applied inside the
\(\infty\)-category of \(\mathcal{O}\)-algebras. Hence \(\iota_{S}\) is
\(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal. This is (M1).

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Step 4 (property (M2): a single-place map).}
If \(V\setminus\{v\}\subseteq V^{\mathrm{sph}}\), the singleton
\(\{v\}\) belongs to \(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\), so Step~3
applies directly. Otherwise put
\(E=V\setminus V^{\mathrm{sph}}\) and choose the additional maps
in (M2). They give an \(\mathcal O\)-algebra map
\[
\mathcal C_v\longrightarrow
\bigotimes_{w\in E\cup\{v\}}\mathcal C_w
\xrightarrow{\,\iota_{E\cup\{v\}}\,}
\bigotimes'_{w\in V}(\mathcal C_w,u_w).
\]
The first arrow tensors with the chosen maps at \(E\setminus\{v\}\);
the second is supplied by (M1). Passing to a larger admissible
finite set inserts only the original spherical-unit maps, so
tensor coherence and the colimit cocone identify the composites.
The resulting functor depends on the extra choices. Full
faithfulness is not part of (M2).

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Step 5 (property (M3): uniqueness up to contractible space of choices).}
By Step~2, the structured colimit is initial among cocones under
\(\Phi\) in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal O}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}})\).
Here compatibility with \((\iota_S)\) includes the whole coherent
cocone. Let another such structure be given on the fixed underlying
colimit. Initiality supplies a contractible space of maps to its
cocone. After forgetting the algebra structure, each is the
equivalence determined by the two colimit cocones. The forgetful
functor detects equivalences, so the structured map is itself an
equivalence. Consequently every compatible structured cocone is
initial, and the space of them is contractible. This proves (M3).

Steps~1--5 together prove Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[categorical content]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:cat-content}
The proof displays the \(\infty\)-categorical Tate restricted tensor
product as a filtered colimit in the \(\infty\)-category
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})\).
This is the \(\infty\)-categorical analogue of the classical Tate
restricted direct product of locally compact abelian groups:
\[
\prod_{v\in V}^{\prime}G_{v}\;:=\;\varinjlim_{S\text{ finite}}\prod_{v\in S}G_{v}\times\prod_{v\notin S}K_{v}
\]
with \(K_{v}\subseteq G_{v}\) compact-open, via filtered union over
finite ramification sets. Here a transition uses the inclusions
\(K_v\hookrightarrow G_v\) at the newly unrestricted places; the
categorical tensor instead inserts the specified objects \(u_v\).
The common filtered indexing does not identify these different
transition maps or their colimits.
\end{remark}

\section{Discrete Factorization and Geometric Comparison}
\label{v4-arith:tate:bd-factorization}

Finite tensor products carry coherent decomposition maps for
disjoint sets of place labels. We construct these maps together
with the allowed unit insertions. A comparison with geometric
factorization must also specify the coefficients and maps at
colliding positions.

\subsection{Factorization on Finite Sets of Places}

\begin{setup}[finite subsets and multisets of \(V\)]
\label{v4-arith:tate:setup:ran-discrete}
For the set \(V\), let \(\mathrm{Fin}_{+}(V)\) be its nonempty
finite subsets. The set of nonempty finite multisets is instead
\(\coprod_{n\geq1}V^n/\mathfrak S_n\). Forgetting multiplicities
maps the latter onto the former. The diagram below is indexed by
finite subsets, including the empty set, with explicitly specified
arrows; no transition between symmetric powers is needed.
\end{setup}

\begin{definition}[Discrete factorization with specified unit insertions]
\label{v4-arith:tate:def:factorization-discrete}
Let \(\mathrm{Fin}^{u}(V)\) have all finite subsets as objects,
with an arrow \(S\to T\) when \(S\subseteq T\) and
\(T\setminus S\subseteq V^{\mathrm{sph}}\). Thus only places
with a specified spherical-unit map may be inserted. A discrete
factorization datum in the category of
\(\mathcal{O}\)-algebras in \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}}\)
is a diagram
\[
\mathcal{A}\;:\;\mathrm{Fin}^{u}(V)\;\longrightarrow\;\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}})
\]
satisfying the factorization axiom: for every pair of disjoint
finite subsets \(S_{1},S_{2}\subset V\),
\[
\mathcal{A}(S_{1}\sqcup S_{2})\;\simeq\;\mathcal{A}(S_{1})\otimes\mathcal{A}(S_{2})
\]
as \(\mathcal{O}\)-algebras, coherently associative and symmetric,
and natural in the allowed inclusions of disjoint subsets.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite tensors give discrete factorization]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:BD-automatic-discrete}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Under Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data},
the finite tensors define a discrete factorization datum in the
sense of Definition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:def:factorization-discrete}.
Its restriction to \(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\) is the
diagram \(\Phi_{\{\mathcal C_v,u_v\}}\) of
Remark~\ref{v4-arith:tate:rem:iota-2cells}. Its value
on \(S=\{v_{1},\ldots,v_{k}\}\) is
\(\bigotimes_{i=1}^{k}\mathcal{C}_{v_{i}}\), and on a disjoint union
\(S_{1}\sqcup S_{2}\) is
\(\mathcal{A}(S_{1})\otimes\mathcal{A}(S_{2})\) by associativity of
the tensor product.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For each allowed inclusion, insert the maps \(\eta_v\) at the
new places. Composing two inclusions inserts the same maps at the
same places, giving the diagram. Associativity of the finite
tensor identifies the value on a disjoint union with the tensor
of its two values. Tensor coherence proves all refinements and
permutations of this identification. On
\(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\), these maps are precisely the
transitions defining \(\Phi\).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Filtered assembly of the finite tensors]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:tate-is-factorization-globals}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Under the hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main},
\[
\bigotimes_{v\in V}^{\prime}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\;=\;\varinjlim_{S\in\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)}\Phi(S).
\]
The colimit assembles the discrete factorization datum of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:BD-automatic-discrete} along its
spherical-unit insertions. Identifying it with sections or homology
of a geometric Ran object requires a separate comparison.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Position geometry is additional]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:factorization-trivial}
The discrete construction proves the disjoint tensor identities
and their unit-insertion coherences. It does not compute the
coefficient on a position diagonal. The formal-disc and derived
closed-point models of Proposition~\ref{v4-prop:word-finite-place-models}
retain different normal data at a finite place. A geometric
arithmetic realization must select and compare its local models,
including the archimedean one in
Remark~\ref{v4-arith:thm:CG-conclusion}.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Compatibility with Dunn Additivity}
\label{v4-arith:tate:subsec:dunn-compat}

The restricted tensor product commutes with Dunn additivity
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:dunn-additivity}) in the following precise sense.

\begin{proposition}[Dunn additivity commutes with restricted tensor product]
\label{v4-arith:tate:prop:dunn-compat}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Fix \(n_{1},n_{2}\geq 0\). Let
\(\{\mathcal{C}_{v}\}_{v\in V}\) be a family of
\(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-monoidal stable presentable \(\infty\)-categories
with the pointing maps of Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}(D2)
for \(E_{n_1+n_2}\). Then the
restricted tensor product
\(\bigotimes'_{v}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\) constructed via
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} with
\(\mathcal{O}=E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\) coincides, as an
\(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-monoidal \(\infty\)-category, with the iterated
construction:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] View each \(\mathcal{C}_{v}\) as an \(E_{n_{1}}\)-algebra in
\(E_{n_{2}}\)-monoidal stable presentable \(\infty\)-categories (via
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:dunn-additivity}).
\item[(b)] Apply Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} with
\(\mathcal{O}=E_{n_{2}}\) to the underlying family to get
\(\bigotimes'_{v}\mathcal{C}_{v}\) as an \(E_{n_{2}}\)-category;
\item[(c)] View the result as an \(E_{n_{1}}\)-algebra in
\(E_{n_{2}}\)-categories, by functoriality of the restricted tensor
product.
\end{enumerate}
Via Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:dunn-additivity}, this is an
\(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-monoidal structure. It agrees with the
\(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-structure of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} applied directly.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both constructions yield filtered colimits in the same
\(\infty\)-category. The key is that the tensor product
of \(\infty\)-operads \(E_{n_{1}}\otimes E_{n_{2}}\)
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:dunn-additivity}) encodes the two supplied
structures together with their interchange coherences. The
colimit over \(S\) preserves these operations and coherences
(Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:colim-preserves-O-alg}). The
induced \(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-structure on the colimit equals the
\(E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}\)-structure of the direct construction.
Formally: the forgetful functor
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{E_{n_{1}+n_{2}}}\simeq\mathrm{Alg}_{E_{n_{1}}\otimes E_{n_{2}}}\to\mathrm{Alg}_{E_{n_{2}}}\)
commutes with filtered colimits
(Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:filtered-colim-operadic}), so both
iterated colimit computations agree.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[special case: \(E_{1}\otimes E_{1}=E_{2}\)]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:dunn-E1-E2}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Suppose each \(\mathcal{C}_{v}\) is
\(E_{1}\)-monoidal in \(E_{1}\)-monoidal presentable stable
\(\infty\)-categories (equivalently,
\(E_{2}\)-monoidal by
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:dunn-additivity}). Suppose the supplied
pointings are maps for that entire structure. Then the restricted
tensor product is \(E_{2}\)-monoidal. It is computed either by direct
\(E_{2}\)-application of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} or by
nested \(E_{1}\)-applications.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[role for F2]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:dunn-F2-role}
In the F2 application, suppose
\(\mathcal{C}_{v}=\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_{2},v}\) has the
required \(E_2\)-structure and pointing maps. Dunn additivity gives a
factorization as an \(E_{1}\)-algebra in \(E_{1}\)-categories. The
compatibility of Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:dunn-compat} confirms
that the global \(E_{2}\)-structure on
\(\bigotimes'_{v}\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_{2},v}\) is
well-defined either via direct \(E_{2}\)-restricted-tensor or via
nested \(E_{1}\)-restricted-tensors.
\end{remark}

\section{Application to the Iwahori Hecke Category}
\label{v4-arith:tate:iwahori-application}

\subsection{The Conditional Local Data}

\begin{setup}[Iwahori Hecke family]
\label{v4-arith:tate:setup:iwahori-Hecke-data}
Fix \(V=\mathbb P\cup\{\infty\}\). Suppose a stable presentable
model \(\mathcal C_v=\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_2,v}\), its
colimit-compatible \(E_2\)-monoidal structure, and the actual
pointing maps of Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}(D2) have
been specified. Write \(|0\rangle_v=\eta_v(\mathbb S)\) where the
pointing is supplied. These are inputs. A class of a double
coset or a fixed vector does not by itself construct such a
categorical pointing.
\end{setup}

\begin{lemma}[the archimedean unit identification required for insertion]
\label{v4-arith:tate:lem:iwahori-sph-arch}
Suppose \(\mathcal C_\infty\) has the specified stable presentable
\(E_2\)-monoidal structure. Its unit functor
\(\eta_\infty:\mathrm{Sp}\to\mathcal C_\infty\) is an
\(E_2\)-algebra morphism. To use a chosen object
\(|0\rangle_\infty\) as its value on \(\mathbb S\), a unit
identification \(|0\rangle_\infty\simeq\mathbb 1_{\mathcal C_\infty}\)
is required. Existence of an \(O(2)\)-fixed vector alone does not
supply this identification.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The unit functor sends a spectrum \(E\) to its canonical spectral
action on \(\mathbb 1_{\mathcal C_\infty}\). Applying an operation
commutes with the spectral action in each input, by colimit
preservation. The unit identities then identify its value with
the smash product of the input spectra acting on
\(\mathbb 1_{\mathcal C_\infty}\).
The operadic coherences give the same identifications under
substitution, including the nullary operation. Thus the functor
is \(E_2\)-monoidal, and its value on \(\mathbb S\) is the unit.
This identifies the extra datum needed for a chosen
object to serve as that value.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Conditional Assembly Corollary}

\begin{corollary}[restricted-tensor \(E_2\) transfer]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:F2-closed}
Under Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:iwahori-Hecke-data}, define
\[
 \mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_2,\mathrm{glob}}
 :=\bigotimes_{v\in V}'(\mathcal C_v,|0\rangle_v).
\]
This object has the \(E_2\)-monoidal structure and admissible
finite-stage maps of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}. A singleton map is
available under exactly its condition (M2), including any
additional pointing maps required there. Uniqueness is relative
to the complete specified colimit cocone. The displayed formula
is a definition, not a comparison with a separately constructed
global arithmetic Hecke category.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The hypotheses give the diagram in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{E_2}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}})\).
Apply Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}, including its
singleton and cocone qualifications. The proof supplies neither
the local \(E_2\)-structures nor pointing maps whose existence has
not been assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[local operations and geometric comparison remain inputs]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:archimedean-vs-finite-E2}
Discrete place labels do not construct the local \(E_2\)-operations,
nor do they identify operations at geometric collision strata.
Dunn additivity compares supplied operadic structures. It does
not supply missing local structures from the discreteness of the
place set. These requirements remain separate from F2.c.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[archimedean pointing]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:archimedean}
The archimedean factor may be included in every admissible finite
stage without a pointing. Inserting that factor, or forming a
singleton map that must insert it, requires the actual
\(E_2\)-algebra map into that factor. A spherical vector in a
representation or in a Fock-space presentation is not a proof
of this categorical datum.
\end{remark}

\section{Uniqueness of the \(E_n\) Structure}
\label{v4-arith:tate:uniqueness}

\begin{proposition}[uniqueness for a fixed cocone]
\label{v4-arith:tate:prop:uniqueness}
Fix the full diagram
\(\Phi:\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\to
\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal O}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}})\)
and an underlying colimit cocone with vertex
\(C=\operatorname*{colim}U\Phi\). The space of lifts of this whole
cocone to a colimit cocone of \(\mathcal O\)-algebras is
contractible. The lift of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main} is one such lift.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
HA~3.2.3.1, in the form of
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:filtered-colim-operadic}, creates
the filtered colimit and detects a colimit cocone on underlying
objects. Thus every lift of the fixed underlying colimit cocone
is a structured colimit cocone. Any two structured colimit
cocones have a contractible space of equivalences respecting
the complete cocone. Forgetting the structure gives the
corresponding equivalences of underlying colimit cocones, whose
space is also contractible. Taking the fiber over the fixed
underlying cocone proves the assertion. Merely specifying
individual arrows or commuting triangles would not specify that
complete cocone.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[compatibility of available singleton maps]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:uniqueness-fibers}
If \(V\setminus\{v\}\subseteq V^{\mathrm{sph}}\), the singleton
map \(\iota_v:\mathcal C_v\to C\) is \(\mathcal O\)-monoidal.
For a general \(v\), the same assertion holds after the additional
pointing maps required by (M2) have been specified. In either
case \(\iota_v\) intertwines the local and global operations,
with the coherences of its specified operadic cocone. No full
faithfulness or identification of \(\mathcal C_v\) with its image
is asserted.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
In the first case \(\{v\}\) is an admissible finite stage, so (M1)
applies. In the second case insert the additionally supplied
pointings to reach an admissible stage and apply (M1).
For example, for two places with categories \(\mathrm{Sp}\) and
\(\mathrm{Mod}_{H\mathbb Q}\) and their unit maps, the singleton
map from \(\mathrm{Sp}\) is rationalization. It is not fully
faithful: for any prime \(p\), the nonzero spectrum
\(H\mathbb F_p\) maps to zero. It is nevertheless symmetric
monoidal. Thus recovery of a local category from its image
cannot follow from uniqueness.
\end{proof}

\section{Base Change and Functoriality}
\label{v4-arith:tate:base-change}

The restricted tensor product is functorial in the family
\(\{\mathcal{C}_{v}\}\) and covariant under base change.

\begin{proposition}[functoriality in the pointed family]
\label{v4-arith:tate:prop:functoriality-family}
Let two families over the same set \(V\) satisfy
Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}, and let
\(f_v:\mathcal C_v\to\mathcal C'_v\) be
\(\mathcal O\)-algebra morphisms. At every place where both
pointings are supplied, require a specified equivalence
\(f_v\eta_v\simeq\eta'_v\) as morphisms of \(\mathcal O\)-algebras.
Then these pointed morphisms induce an \(\mathcal O\)-algebra map
\[
 \bigotimes_v' f_v:
 \bigotimes_v'(\mathcal C_v,u_v)\longrightarrow
 \bigotimes_v'(\mathcal C'_v,u'_v).
\]
This construction is \(\infty\)-functorial in the pointed family.
An equivalence of the objects \(f_v(u_v)\) and \(u'_v\) without
compatibility with the specified operadic pointings is not the
hypothesis for a general \(\mathcal O\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use the common cofinal index consisting of finite subsets
containing
\((V\setminus V^{\mathrm{sph}})\cup
 (V\setminus V'^{\mathrm{sph}})\).
The finite tensor products \(\bigotimes_{v\in S}f_v\), together
with the given pointed equivalences, form a natural
transformation of the complete operadic diagrams. Taking its
colimit gives the asserted map. Functoriality follows from the
functoriality of tensor products and of the colimit functor on
diagram categories.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[base change along a colimit-preserving symmetric monoidal functor]
\label{v4-arith:tate:prop:base-change}
Let
\(F^{\otimes}:(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}},\otimes)\to(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm{L}}_{\mathrm{st}},\otimes)\)
be a symmetric monoidal colimit-preserving functor. For a family
as in Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}, define its transported
pointing and distinguished object by
\[
 \eta_v^F:\mathrm{Sp}\simeq F(\mathrm{Sp})
       \xrightarrow{F(\eta_v)}F(\mathcal C_v),
 \qquad u_v^F=\eta_v^F(\mathbb S).
\]
Then
\[
F\bigl(\bigotimes_{v}^{\prime}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\bigr)\;\simeq\;\bigotimes_{v}^{\prime}(F\mathcal{C}_{v},u_v^F)
\]
as \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal \(\infty\)-categories.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\(F\) preserves both filtered colimits (colimit-preservation) and
tensor products (symmetric monoidality). Both operations are
separately applied to the colimit diagram \(\Phi\); their
commutativity gives the equivalence. Formally:
\[
F\bigl(\varinjlim_{S}\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal{C}_{v}\bigr)\;\simeq\;\varinjlim_{S}F\bigl(\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal{C}_{v}\bigr)\;\simeq\;\varinjlim_{S}\bigotimes_{v\in S}F\mathcal{C}_{v}
\]
by symmetric monoidality of \(F\), and the transition maps
\(F\iota_{S\subseteq S'}=\iota^{(F)}_{S\subseteq S'}\) by functoriality
of \(F\) applied to the pointed spherical maps \(\eta_{v}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[scope of categorical base change]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:vol-I-coherence}
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:tate:prop:base-change} applies to a specified functor
on stable presentable categories and to its transported pointing
maps. It supplies an arithmetic change of base only after those
categorical data have been constructed. The bar--cobar base-change
comparisons in Volume~I concern their own coefficient systems
and hypotheses. Relating the two constructions requires a map
between their carriers that preserves the relevant operations.
Formal commutation with tensor products and colimits alone does
not identify them.
\end{remark}

\section{Examples}
\label{v4-arith:tate:examples}

\subsection{Example 1: Module Categories and the Spectral Tensor Product}

\begin{example}[the tensor colimit of module categories]
\label{v4-arith:tate:ex:discrete-recover}
Let \(R_v\) be discrete commutative rings, put \(A_v=H R_v\),
and give \(\mathcal C_v=\mathrm{Mod}_{A_v}\) its usual symmetric
monoidal structure and unit pointing. Define
\[
 A_{\mathrm{tens}}=
 \operatorname*{colim}_{S\in\mathrm{Fin}(V)}
       \bigotimes_{v\in S,\,\mathbb S} A_v
 \quad\text{in}\quad\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Sp}),
\]
with unit insertions as transition maps and empty tensor
\(\mathbb S\). Then
\[
 \bigotimes_{v\in V}'(\mathrm{Mod}_{A_v},A_v)
       \simeq\mathrm{Mod}_{A_{\mathrm{tens}}}
\]
as symmetric monoidal stable presentable categories.
This is a spectral tensor-colimit formula. It is not an
identification with a profinite completion or with a classical
adelic restricted product.
\end{example}

\begin{proof}
The module-category tensor equivalence identifies each finite
stage with modules over \(\bigotimes_{v\in S,\mathbb S}A_v\),
and identifies the transition functors with extension of scalars.
The module-category functor preserves filtered colimits
(HA~4.8.5.13), with tensor compatibility given by HA~4.8.5.16.
These identifications preserve the symmetric monoidal structures
and unit maps, so passage to the filtered colimit gives the formula.

For example, if \(V=\mathbb P\) and \(R_p=\mathbb Z_p\), every
prime \(\ell\) becomes invertible in \(A_{\mathrm{tens}}\): add
any factor \(H\mathbb Z_p\) with \(p\ne\ell\). Thus the output
is rational, whereas \(H\widehat{\mathbb Z}\) is not. In
particular this construction does not recover
\(\mathrm{Mod}_{H\widehat{\mathbb Z}}\). The classical finite
adeles also use integral subrings at the unramified places.
That restricted-product diagram is not the diagram above with
\(A_p=H\mathbb Q_p\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Example 2: Conditional Function-Field Application}

\begin{example}[function-field assembly]
\label{v4-arith:tate:ex:function-field}
Let \(V=|X|\) for a smooth projective curve over \(\mathbb F_q\).
A family of stable presentable \(E_2\)-monoidal local categories
with actual pointing maps satisfying (D2) has the restricted
\(E_2\)-tensor product of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}. To apply this to chosen
Hecke categories, their local \(E_2\)-structures and those maps
must first be supplied. A comparison with Beilinson--Drinfeld
factorization requires its geometric coefficients, collision
maps and higher coherences separately. Discretizing \(|X|\)
does not provide that comparison or its missing operations.
\end{example}

\begin{remark}[no automatic comparison between arithmetic bases]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:ff-vs-arith}
The filtered-colimit theorem applies over either indexing set
once its categorical inputs are supplied. It gives no
function-field-to-arithmetic comparison functor. Such a
comparison must specify the functor, its pointing compatibility,
and any required geometric factorization data.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Example 3: Conditional Arithmetic Iwahori Assembly}

\begin{example}[arithmetic Iwahori application]
\label{v4-arith:tate:ex:arith-iwahori}
Under Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:iwahori-Hecke-data},
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:F2-closed} gives an
\(E_2\)-structure on the categorical restricted tensor product.
The notation \(\mathrm{Hk}^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{GL_2,\mathrm{glob}}\)
in that corollary denotes this defined object. An independently
defined global arithmetic Hecke category requires a separate
comparison. So does a restricted tensor product of representations.
Neither comparison follows from filtered colimits.
\end{example}

\section{The Finite Labeled-Disk Description}
\label{v4-arith:tate:verification}

\begin{setup}[finite labeled coefficients]
\label{v4-arith:tate:setup:factorization-homology}
Take \(\mathcal O=E_n\), \(n\geq1\), and the data of
Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:data}. For a finite set \(S\),
write \(M_S=\coprod_{v\in S}\mathbb R^n_v\), retaining each
place label. Assign coefficient \(\mathcal C_v\) to its own
labeled component. The finite labeled-disk value is
\[
 F^{\mathrm{lab}}(M_S):=\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal C_v.
\]

This is a family of coefficients, not the single constant
coefficient \(\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal C_v\) on every disk.
For admissible inclusions insert the specified maps \(\eta_v\).
Only this finite labeled diagram and its filtered colimit are
being defined. No presentability assertion about
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\) itself is used.
\end{setup}

\begin{theorem}[comparison with the finite labeled-disk diagram]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:verification-via-factorization-homology}
The diagram \(F^{\mathrm{lab}}\) on
\(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)\), with its specified
insertions, is the finite tensor diagram \(\Phi\) used in
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}. Hence
\[
 \operatorname*{colim}_{S\in\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V)}
       F^{\mathrm{lab}}(M_S)
 \simeq\bigotimes_{v\in V}'(\mathcal C_v,u_v)
\]
as \(E_n\)-algebras in
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\), compatibly with the
complete colimit cocones.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At each finite stage the values are identical. On each inclusion
the morphisms are the same tensor products of identities and
supplied \(\eta_v\). Their higher compatibilities come from the
same symmetric monoidal structure and the same pointing maps.
Thus this is the same operadic diagram, and taking its filtered
colimit gives the assertion.
\end{proof}

For comparison, ordinary factorization homology on framed disks
with a single coefficient algebra \(A\), when defined, satisfies
\[
\int_{\coprod_{v\in S}\mathbb R^n}A\simeq A^{\otimes |S|}.
\]
The disk overcategory has a terminal object. Applying the
symmetric monoidal disk algebra gives the displayed tensor
power. See Ayala--Francis, \emph{A factorization homology primer},
Definitions~3.5 and~3.7.

Putting \(A=\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal C_v\) in this formula
therefore does not give \(\bigotimes_{v\in S}\mathcal C_v\).
Nor is an integral with a single coefficient
\(A_V=\bigotimes'_v\mathcal C_v\) over all the disks a
replacement for the labeled diagram. The usual factorization
homology construction is a left Kan extension of a disk algebra.
Disk normalization and disjoint-union compatibility alone do
not characterize its values on general manifolds.

\begin{remark}[scope of the description]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:ginot-ext-verification}
The labeled-disk description uses exactly the same finite
diagram and colimit argument as the restricted tensor theorem.
It is not a second independent proof, an Ext comparison, or a
construction of geometric collision operations. A comparison
with a factorization algebra on a geometric space requires its
coefficient system and comparison morphisms separately.
\end{remark}

\section{Remarks on Operadic Coherence}
\label{v4-arith:tate:coherence-remarks}

\subsection{Hypotheses Used by the Filtered-Colimit Argument}

The application of HA~3.2.3.1 uses a small operad and filtered
colimits in the ambient symmetric monoidal category.
Tensoring must preserve those colimits in each variable.
It requires neither operadic coherence in the sense of
HA~3.3.1.1 nor presentability of the ambient category. The
coherence assumption retained in some statements above is an
extra hypothesis, not a premise needed for this argument.
The separate presentability result HA~3.2.3.5 is not being
applied to \(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\) itself.
The preservation of higher operation coherences follows from
taking the colimit of the supplied operadic diagram, not from
recognizing operations in its cohomology.

\subsection{Alternative Pointing Maps}

\begin{remark}[non-spherical insertion]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:non-spherical-insertion}
A replacement pointing must be an actual morphism
\(\widetilde\eta_v:\mathrm{Sp}\to\mathcal C_v\) in
\(\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal O}(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}})\),
with \(\widetilde u_v=\widetilde\eta_v(\mathbb S)\).
Specified equivalences between pointing maps, in that mapping
space, induce equivalences of the diagrams and their colimits.
Equivalence of the underlying central objects alone is not the
general operadic compatibility required. In the unital \(E_n\)
cases every such pointing sends \(\mathbb S\) to the actual
monoidal unit. Thus a non-unit central object is not an alternative
pointing of this kind.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Iterated Restricted Tensor Products}

\begin{proposition}[iterated restricted tensor products]
\label{v4-arith:tate:prop:iterated-restricted}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere Let \(V=V_{1}\sqcup V_{2}\) be a partition,
with spherical data restricting to each \(V_{i}\). Then
\[
\bigotimes_{v\in V}^{\prime}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\;\simeq\;\Bigl(\bigotimes_{v\in V_{1}}^{\prime}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\Bigr)\otimes\Bigl(\bigotimes_{v\in V_{2}}^{\prime}(\mathcal{C}_{v},u_{v})\Bigr)
\]
as \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoidal \(\infty\)-categories.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Intersection with \(V_1\) and \(V_2\) identifies the indexing
poset with
\(\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V_1)\times
\mathrm{Fin}^{\mathrm{sph}}(V_2)\).
At each pair of finite stages, tensor associativity identifies
the two values and their pointing maps. Separate preservation
of filtered colimits by tensor products, from
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:prl-admits-colimits}, identifies
the underlying colimits. The same comparison preserves every
operation in the supplied diagrams, so it is an equivalence
of \(\mathcal O\)-algebras.
\end{proof}

\section{Completeness of F2.c and Residual Local Input}
\label{v4-arith:tate:completeness-F2}

\begin{theorem}[F2.c assembly for supplied local Iwahori data]
\label{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2-closure}
F2.c proves the restricted-tensor assembly step for local
Iwahori categories satisfying
Setup~\ref{v4-arith:tate:setup:iwahori-Hecke-data}, including
the specified \(E_2\)-algebra pointings. Its uniqueness is
relative to the complete colimit cocone. It does not construct
any of these local inputs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:tate:cor:F2-closed} with its full
hypotheses, which in turn is the \(E_2\) specialization of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2c-main}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[scope of the F2 conclusion]
\label{v4-arith:tate:rem:F2-form-final}
F2.c is the formal assembly theorem. An arithmetic application
requires the local \(E_2\)-enhancement F2.b and all pointing data
of (D2). These remain hypotheses wherever they have not been constructed.
Identifying the defined tensor colimit with an independently
specified global geometric object remains a separate comparison.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[F3 after formal F2.c assembly]
\label{v4-arith:tate:cor:F3-reduces-with-F2-closed}
Assume the full local \(E_2\) and pointing hypotheses.
Then F2.c supplies the formal restricted-tensor construction
in the reduction of \(F3\). The
local input F2.b and any unverified pointing or global comparison
requirements remain. F2.c also leaves the other hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:thm:F3-reduction} unchanged.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Substitute Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:tate:thm:F2-closure} only for
its formal assembly conclusion in the cited reduction.
That theorem assumes the local pointed operadic diagram and
makes no independent arithmetic comparison.
\end{proof}

\section{Tate Tensor Boundary}
\label{v4-arith:tate:summary}

The categorical restricted tensor product is the filtered
colimit of the finite tensor diagram of supplied
\(\mathcal O\)-algebras and actual operadic pointing maps.
HA~3.2.3.1 applies because the ambient categorical tensor product
preserves filtered colimits separately. Presentability of
\(\mathrm{Pr}^{\mathrm L}_{\mathrm{st}}\) itself is not needed.

The whole colimit cocone has a contractible space of structured
lifts. This does not assert full faithfulness of singleton maps,
and a singleton map requires the precise pointing condition
(M2). The construction is functorial in pointed families.
It commutes with the stated symmetric monoidal base changes and
iterated tensor products, and respects Dunn additivity for
supplied structures.

The finite labeled-disk description is the same diagram, not
an independent derivation. The module-category example computes
a spectral tensor colimit, not a profinite or adelic restricted
product. Neither example supplies geometric collision maps.

F2.c is therefore a proved formal assembly theorem under its
full hypotheses. The local arithmetic \(E_2\)-structures,
pointing maps and any independent global comparison remain
required inputs. The arithmetic comparison in F3 is likewise
not absorbed by this categorical construction.
