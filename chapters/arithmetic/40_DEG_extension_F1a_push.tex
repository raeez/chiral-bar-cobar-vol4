\chapter{The Local Categorical Comparison and the DEG Essential Image}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a}

F1.a asks for the specified local categorical comparison at every
prime, with its coefficient category, spectral support and Hecke
actions. The Dotto--Emerton--Gee theorem constructs a fully faithful
functor on its \(p\)-power-torsion representation carrier at
\(p\ge5\). Its essential image is proper in its stated target.
The arithmetic comparison therefore requires a geometric
identification of the relevant image and comparison carriers, not
an assertion that this existing functor is surjective onto its
whole target.

\section{Representation and Spectral Carriers}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:context}
Fargues--Scholze geometrization and spectral actions concern their
own sheaf and parameter categories. The intended F1.a equivalence
\[
 \mathrm D^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}([GL_2(\mathbb Q_p)])
 \simeq\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{EG},p}_{GL_2})
\]
requires specified comparisons with these carriers. The coefficient
regime, the meaning of the sph-finite realization, and the
nilpotent-support restriction are part of the construction.
They cannot be substituted into a fully faithful theorem on different
categories without a comparison functor.

\section{The Local and Family Construction Problems}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:table}
At \(p\ge5\), the problem is to relate the actual DEG image to the
chosen arithmetic spectral category, construct action-compatible
functors and identify lifted generators. At two and three, the
wild-enhanced carrier, the block functors and their exhaustion are
separate data (H2)--(H4). Finite extension calculations can constrain
these data but do not construct the functors. The three-vertex model
of Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3} retains
its separate arithmetic realization problem.

Varying a central character requires effective category-valued
descent on a specified parameter site, including nonreduced families
and higher overlap maps. Point-character comparisons do not suffice.
Even complete local comparisons leave the cross-prime and global
centre maps required for adelic descent.

\section{Dotto--Emerton--Gee Recalled}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:deg-recalled}

The DEG result, stated precisely, is the theorem on which
the rest of the argument builds.

\subsection{The Proven Coefficient and Spectral Carriers}
Fix \(p\ge5\), the ring of integers \(\mathcal O\) of a finite
extension of \(\mathbb Q_p\), and a central character
\(\zeta:\mathbb Q_p^\times\to\mathcal O^\times\).
Let \(\mathcal A\) be smooth \(\mathcal O\)-representations on
which \(p\) acts locally nilpotently, with central character \(\zeta\).
Let \(X\) be the formal stack of rank-two projective étale
\((\varphi,\Gamma)\)-modules of determinant \(\zeta\varepsilon^{-1}\),
where \(\varepsilon\) is the cyclotomic character.

\begin{theorem}[Dotto--Emerton--Gee, Theorem 1.1.1]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:deg-fixed-chi}
There is an \(\mathcal O\)-linear fully faithful exact functor
\(D^b_{\mathrm{fp}}(\mathcal A)\to D^b_{\mathrm{coh}}(X)\).
It extends to a colimit-preserving fully faithful functor
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:deg-functor}
 F^{\mathrm{DEG}}:D(\mathcal A)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Ind}D^b_{\mathrm{coh}}(X).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Reference]
Dotto--Emerton--Gee, arXiv:2603.26887, Theorem~1.1.1
and the paragraph following it. The extension uses their compact
generation statement for \(D(\mathcal A)\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The essential-image boundary]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:deg-not-assert}
Remark~1.1.2 of that paper states that this functor is not an
equivalence. Thus exhaustion of its entire displayed target is not
an unproved consequence to be appended to this theorem. The
geometric description of its proper essential image, its relation
to a specified nilpotent-support category, and changes of coefficient
or representation carrier are separate comparisons. The theorem
also does not supply the wild-prime or varying-character construction.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[The constructed essential-image carrier]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:deg-image}
Let \(\mathcal I_{p,\zeta}\) be the full essential image of
\(F^{\mathrm{DEG}}\) in its actual target. Then
\(D(\mathcal A)\simeq\mathcal I_{p,\zeta}\), and
\(\mathcal I_{p,\zeta}\) is closed under small colimits.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Full faithfulness identifies the source with this full subcategory.
Every diagram in it lifts to the source; preservation of colimits
places its colimit back in the image. This categorical construction
does not identify the image with a specified support condition or
with a category generated by named cycle sheaves.
\end{proof}

For the F1.a comparison below, use the notation
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{cmp}}_{p,\chi}\) for a chosen functor on the
specified arithmetic representation and nilpotent-support carriers.
Constructing these carriers, the functor, and its relation to the
actual \(F^{\mathrm{DEG}}\) is part of (H1). It is not a coefficient
substitution in the preceding theorem.

\subsection{Why Essential Surjectivity is Subtle}

\begin{remark}[intuition for obstruction (a)]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:deg-fails-surj-intuition}
The sub-\(\infty\)-category
\(\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}^{\mathrm{BM\text{-}image}}\) is only
a test subcategory generated by Breuil--Mezard cycles.  A possible
obstruction to essential surjectivity is the structure sheaf of an
intersection of two such cycles which is not itself a named
Breuil--Mezard cycle.  Whether every such object lies in the
triangulated colimit closure of the test generators is the
block-exhaustion question.
\end{remark}

\section{Obstruction (a): Essential Surjectivity at \(p\geq 5\)}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:ess-surj}

\subsection{The Colmez--Paskunas Block Decomposition}

\begin{theorem}[Componentwise functors on specified stable blocks]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:colmez-paskunas-blocks}
Suppose presentable stable categories and compatible decompositions
\(\mathcal C\simeq\prod_\rho\mathcal C_\rho\),
\(\mathcal D\simeq\prod_\rho\mathcal D_\rho\) are specified.
A family of fully faithful exact colimit-preserving functors
\(\Phi_\rho:\mathcal C_\rho\to\mathcal D_\rho\) gives a functor
with these properties on the products.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Mapping spectra and colimits in a product are computed componentwise.
Apply the given properties in every component.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Required Spectral Block Models}
\begin{proposition}[Image support under a categorical block comparison]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:spectral-blocks}
Suppose the chosen comparison functor is identified with the
componentwise functor under specified equivalences
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:EG-block-decomp}
 \mathcal C\simeq\prod_\rho\mathcal C_\rho,\qquad
 \mathcal D\simeq\prod_\rho\mathcal D_\rho,
 \qquad
 \mathcal D_\rho=
 \mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{EG},p,\chi,\rho}_{GL_2}).
\end{equation}
Then \(\Phi(\mathcal C_\rho)\subseteq\mathcal D_\rho\).
These are categorical decomposition and support hypotheses; a
formal stack is not thereby identified with a deformation ring.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
An object of one component is the tuple whose other components are
zero. The componentwise functor preserves this property.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Reduction to the specified stable blocks]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:cor:ess-surj-block-by-block}
Under these supplied decompositions, \(\Phi\) is essentially
surjective if and only if every \(\Phi_\rho\) is essentially surjective.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Choose preimages componentwise in one direction. In the other,
apply essential surjectivity to a target tuple supported in one
component. Its preimage gives the required component preimage.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Block-Exhaustion Question}

\begin{definition}[Block exhaustion for the chosen comparison]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:def:block-exhaustion}
For the specified stable categories \(\mathcal C_\rho\),
\(\mathcal D_\rho\), a block is exhausted if the chosen
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{cmp}}_{p,\chi,\rho}\) is essentially surjective.
A cycle class alone supplies neither an object of the target nor
its preimage through this functor.
\end{definition}
\begin{conjecture}[The arithmetic block comparison datum]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:block-exhaustion}
For \(p\ge5\), construct the common coefficient carriers, the
specified stable block decompositions and image-support comparisons
for F1.a. Construct exact colimit-preserving fully faithful
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{cmp}}_{p,\chi,\rho}\) with the required Hecke
compatibilities, and actual lifts of a localizing generating family
of \(\mathcal D_\rho\). Identify their relation to the proper
essential image of \(F^{\mathrm{DEG}}\). This does not assert that
the latter functor is an equivalence onto its whole target.
\end{conjecture}
\begin{remark}[Objects and their lifts]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:how-to-prove-block-exhaustion}
A Breuil--Mezard construction must provide actual spectral objects
\(Q_i\), their nilpotent singular support and generation, together
with objects \(P_i\) and equivalences \(\Phi(P_i)\simeq Q_i\).
Generation by target objects alone is insufficient. Matching degree-one
extensions also does not prove full faithfulness on mapping spectra.
\end{remark}
\begin{proposition}[Exhaustion by lifted generators]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:lifted-generators}
Let \(F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D\) be fully faithful, exact and
colimit-preserving between presentable stable categories. If
\(Q_i\) generate \(\mathcal D\) as a localizing subcategory and
specified equivalences \(F(P_i)\simeq Q_i\) exist, then \(F\)
is an equivalence.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Full faithfulness lifts diagrams in the image; preservation of
colimits puts their colimits in the image. Exactness gives closure
under shifts and cones. Thus the image is a localizing subcategory
containing the generators, and is the whole target.
\end{proof}
For example, \(D(k)\to D(k)\times D(k)\), \(M\mapsto(M,0)\),
is fully faithful, exact and colimit-preserving, but misses
\((0,k)\). The two component structure sheaves \((k,0),(0,k)\)
generate the target; only one is in this functor's image.
When full faithfulness also needs proof, the compact-generator
criterion of Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:w5-e:local:generator-criterion}
requires equivalences on the full mapping spectra, not just
\(\operatorname{Ext}^1\).

\begin{theorem}[conditional essential surjectivity at \(p\geq 5\)]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:ess-surj-conditional}
Assume all the carrier, functor and lifted-generator data of
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:block-exhaustion}. Then for every
\(p\geq 5\) and every central character \(\chi\), the functor
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{cmp}}_{p,\chi}\) is an equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:ess-surj-conditional}
\Phi^{\mathrm{cmp}}_{p,\chi}\colon\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\bigl([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})]\bigr)_{\chi}
\;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\bigl(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The lifted-generator proposition makes each chosen block functor an
equivalence. The supplied categorical decompositions assemble them
componentwise. The full faithfulness used here is part of the chosen
comparison datum, not an unproved identification of its carriers
with those of the DEG theorem.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Coefficient and Support Comparison}
The arithmetic block datum includes its common coefficients,
presentable stable realizations, categorical decompositions, Hecke
compatibilities and actual lifted generators. The proven DEG
functor supplies none of these identifications by a change of notation.
Its proper image is a concrete categorical starting point; a
geometric description of that image and the intended nilpotent
support category is the remaining comparison.

\section{Obstruction (b): Extension to \(p=2,3\)}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:wild}

\subsection{Why DEG Does Not Extend Directly}

At \(p\in\{2,3\}\) the obstructions to DEG are multiple:

\begin{proposition}[Data required at the wild primes]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:DEG-wild-obs}
The theorem for \(p\ge5\) supplies no functor at two or three.
A wild comparison requires its own spectral carrier, exact
colimit-preserving fully faithful block functors, and their
support and family compatibilities. A list of types or finite
extension dimensions does not specify these categorical maps.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
These primes are outside the proven theorem's hypotheses.
Constructing a functor requires its action on objects and mapping
spectra, with composition and higher coherences. Neither a type
label nor an extension dimension gives these data. The following
hypotheses retain the actual required constructions.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Wild-EG Enhancement Programme}

The reduction is two-step: (i) construct a wild-block DEG functor
via Bushnell--Henniart types and local Langlands normalisations;
(ii) pair it with an enhanced Emerton--Gee stack incorporating the
wild inertia data absent from the fixed-character setup.

\begin{definition}[wild-enhanced Emerton--Gee comparison datum]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:def:EG-wild-enhanced}
For \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and central character \(\chi\), a
\emph{wild-enhanced Emerton--Gee comparison datum} consists of a
derived formal algebraic stack
\(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_2}\), a map to
\(\mathcal X^{\mathrm{EG},p,\chi}_{GL_2}\), and specified comparisons
of its tangent and trace complexes with the corresponding arithmetic
block data, respecting the chosen coefficients and local Langlands
normalizations. Existence is an additional hypothesis. Adding a
vector space of a prescribed dimension does not construct this datum.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[the proposed degree-one datum at two]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:wild-EG-p2}
The intended local degree-one contribution is a line, as in
Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2}.
Its identification with a derived fibre requires an actual comparison
map. In characteristic two, trivial and sign characters coincide.
The dual-number model has a one-dimensional self-extension but also
has nonzero extensions in every higher degree; it does not prove
that the arithmetic derived fibre is concentrated in degree one.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[the proposed degree-one datum at three]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:wild-EG-p3}
The intended local degree-one contribution is the sum of two lines,
as in Question~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3}.
In the explicit right-module three-vertex model these are
\(\operatorname{Ext}^1(L_0,L_1)\) and
\(\operatorname{Ext}^1(L_1,L_2)\); their Yoneda product gives a
nonzero degree-two class. A comparison with an arithmetic block
and a spectral derived fibre must account for this product and
specify the actual relations, rather than discard the higher degree.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Construction of the Wild DEG Functor}

\begin{conjecture}[wild block functor datum]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:wild-block-functor}
For \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and fixed \(\chi\), construct presentable
stable representation blocks \(\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}}\),
their common coefficient category, and compatible categorical
block decompositions on both sides. Construct exact,
colimit-preserving, fully faithful functors
\[
 \Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi,\tau}:\mathcal C_\tau^{\mathrm{BH}}
 \longrightarrow\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}
 (\mathcal X^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi,\tau}_{GL_2})
\]
with the stated type and Hecke compatibilities, assembling under
those decompositions. Type labels alone do not construct these maps.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{theorem}[existence of wild DEG functor at \(p=2,3\)]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:wild-functor-existence}
Fix \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and the common coefficient ring \(R\) of the
wild comparison datum, with its stated coefficient comparisons. Fix a central
character \(\chi:\mathbb Q_p^\times\to R^\times\).
Assume Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement} below
(existence of the wild-enhanced EG stack with the stated structural
properties) and
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:wild-block-functor}. Then there exists a
fully faithful functor
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:wild-functor}
\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi}\colon
\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\bigl([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})]\bigr)_{\chi}
\;\longrightarrow\;\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\bigl(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\bigr)
\end{equation}
extending DEG to the wild primes, compatible with the
Bushnell--Henniart fiber correspondence on each block.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The wild-enhancement hypothesis supplies the spectral carrier.
The wild block functor datum supplies compatible stable block
decompositions and component functors. Form their componentwise
functor and transport it through the supplied equivalences.
Mapping spectra and colimits in a product are computed componentwise,
so full faithfulness, exactness and colimit preservation follow.
The type and Hecke compatibilities are those of the supplied datum.
\end{proof}

\begin{conjecture}[wild-EG enhancement]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement}
Conjectural statement. For \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and a central character
\(\chi\), there exists a derived formal algebraic stack
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\) with the following
properties:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The truncation
\(\tau_{\leq 0}\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\) equals
the classical Emerton--Gee stack
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\).
\item A specified cotangent complex and deformation comparison,
including infinitesimal automorphisms of the unframed stack.
At a field-valued point \(x\), its positive cohomological degree
must retain
\(H^1(\mathbb L_x)=\operatorname{Lie}(\operatorname{Aut}(x))^\vee\).
A two-term amplitude for a framed or relative chart requires that
chart and its comparison map; it is not an absolute amplitude claim.
Any proposed degree-one block dimensions must be established by the
actual arithmetic class comparison, not prescribed uniformly by
\(p=2\) or \(p=3\).
\item There is a fiber correspondence, compatible with local
Langlands, between the wild components of
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\) and the
Bushnell--Henniart wild types of \(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})\).
\item Ind-coherent sheaves with nilpotent singular support on
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\) are generated under
colimits and shifts by the derived structure sheaves of the
Bushnell--Henniart-indexed closed points.
\end{enumerate}
\end{conjecture}

\begin{lemma}[A split-point automorphism direction]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:lem:stacky-cotangent-direction}
At a split rank-two point \(M_1\oplus M_2\) with fixed determinant,
the unframed deformation stack has a nonzero infinitesimal
automorphism direction. Its absolute cotangent complex cannot have
amplitude contained in \([-1,0]\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The automorphisms \(\operatorname{diag}(t,t^{-1})\) preserve the
summands and determinant. Over \(k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)\),
putting \(t=1+\epsilon a\) gives
\(1+\epsilon\operatorname{diag}(a,-a)\), a nonzero direction
also in characteristic two. In cohomological conventions,
infinitesimal automorphisms are \(H^{-1}(T_x)\); dualizing gives
a nonzero \(H^1(\mathbb L_x)\). For instance
\(\mathbb L_{B\mathbb G_m}|_x=\mathfrak g_m^*[-1]\), which is
concentrated in degree \(+1\). Retaining the classical unframed
stack retains this direction.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[inputs to the wild-EG comparison]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:wild-EG-evidence}
The intended comparison combines smooth representation blocks,
Galois deformation data and an arithmetic trace construction.
Questions~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p2}
and~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion:prop:R4-Ext1-p3} ask for the
coefficient-compatible local maps. The explicit finite-algebra
resolutions establish possible extension patterns, including higher
Ext; they do not establish the arithmetic fibre dimensions.
The existence of a single derived formal stack with these maps,
and compact generation by the proposed closed-point objects,
remain parts of the wild-EG hypothesis.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[domain of the wild-EG conjecture]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:wild-EG-domain}
The wild-EG conjecture requires a derived enhancement and actual
comparison maps with arithmetic block data. The classical stack and
the finite-algebra examples do not by themselves construct that
enhancement. In particular, the local resolutions in
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:bzn-rcompletion} neither determine the
arithmetic derived fibres nor identify their trace classes.
\end{remark}

\subsection{BH-Block Exhaustion and Essential Surjectivity at Wild Primes}

\begin{conjecture}[BH-block exhaustion at wild primes]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:BH-block-exhaustion}
\emph{Conjectural statement.} For \(p\in\{2,3\}\), every central character
\(\chi:\mathbb Q_p^\times\to R^\times\), and every
Bushnell--Kutzko type \(\tau\) (tame or wild) in the support of
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\), the pair
\[
\bigl(\mathrm{Block}^{\mathrm{BH}}_{\tau},\;\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi,\tau}_{GL_{2}}\bigr)

is exhausted in the sense of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:def:block-exhaustion}: the chosen

this exact colimit-preserving fully faithful functor, as in
Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:lifted-generators}.
Generation by named spectral objects without these image
identifications is not an equivalent condition.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{corollary}[wild essential surjectivity, conditional]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:cor:wild-ess-surj}
Assume Conjectures~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement},
\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:wild-block-functor}, and
\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:BH-block-exhaustion}.
Then
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{wild}}_{p,\chi}\) of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:wild-functor-existence} is an equivalence
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\bigl([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})]\bigr)_{\chi}
\;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\bigl(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Fully-faithfulness is Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:wild-functor-existence}.
Essential surjectivity follows block-by-block from the BH-block
analogue of Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:cor:ess-surj-block-by-block}
plus Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:BH-block-exhaustion}.
\end{proof}

\section{Obstruction (c): Central Character Variation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:central-char}

\subsection{The Central-Character Parameter and Its Families}
A fixed central character is a continuous homomorphism
\(\chi:\mathbb Q_p^\times\to k^\times\). To vary it, fix a
coefficient regime and a parameter site \(\mathcal T_p^{\mathrm{cent}}\)
for the intended character families, including any nonreduced
coefficient algebras. Its topology and representability are part
of the family construction; a list of point-valued characters
is not a descent model.

Throughout this family construction, write \(\mathcal X_p^{\mathrm{fam}}\)
for the chosen spectral carrier: the classical Emerton--Gee carrier
for \(p\geq5\), and the chosen wild-enhanced carrier of (H2) for
\(p\in\{2,3\}\). In the latter case its fixed-character carriers
and functors are the data of (H2) and (H3), with block exhaustion
as in (H4). The family carrier and its base-change identifications
are part of the descent model below; they are not inferred from
point-valued characters. The chosen fixed-character functors are
the chosen arithmetic comparison functors of (H1), related to the
actual DEG image by their supplied maps, and the
wild block functors at two and three. Denote their sought family
extension by \(\Phi_p^{\mathrm{fam}}\).

\begin{definition}[Central-character descent model]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:def:central-char-family}
A central-character descent model consists of a specified cover
\(U\to\mathcal T_p^{\mathrm{cent}}\), its \v{C}ech diagram
\(U_\bullet\), and compatible diagrams of presentable stable
categories \(\mathcal C(U_\bullet)\), \(\mathcal D(U_\bullet)\),
with equivalences
\[
 \mathrm D^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}([GL_2(\mathbb Q_p)])
 \simeq\operatorname{Tot}\mathcal C(U_\bullet),\qquad
 \mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal X_p^{\mathrm{fam}})
 \simeq\operatorname{Tot}\mathcal D(U_\bullet).
\]
The models must realize the stated fixed-character categories
under the chosen base-change operations. Equivalence must be
detected on this conservative descent cover, not merely on its
point characters.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Generalized central characters]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:fibre-decomp-standard}
Smooth representations need not split into a direct sum of strict
central-character subrepresentations. For an explicit example,
let \(k\) have characteristic different from two and let \(N\ne0\)
on \(k^2\) satisfy \(N^2=0\). The formula
\[
 g\longmapsto (1+N)^{v_p(\det g)}=1+v_p(\det g)N
\]
defines a smooth representation of \(GL_2(\mathbb Q_p)\): the
valuation is additive and locally constant. The central element
\(pI\) acts by \(1+2N\), which is not scalar and is not
semisimple. Its two composition factors have the trivial central
character, but the extension is not a direct sum of strict-character
objects. A family model must retain this extension data.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[Central-character family comparison datum]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family}
Construct the descent models of
Definition~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:def:central-char-family} and a
morphism \(\Phi_\bullet:\mathcal C(U_\bullet)\to\mathcal D(U_\bullet)\)
extending these chosen fixed-character functors. Prove that each
\(\Phi_n\) is an equivalence on the specified conservative cover
and all its higher overlaps, compatibly with the descent maps,
Hecke actions, coefficient changes and central/determinant twists.
Naturality at point characters alone is not this datum.
\end{conjecture}

\subsection{Assembly of the Family Functor}
\begin{theorem}[Central-character variation by effective descent]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:central-character-variation}
Assume the actual models and levelwise equivalences of
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family},
with the fixed-character block comparisons on their stated domains.
Then their totalization gives an equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:varying-chi}
 \Phi_p^{\mathrm{fam}}:
 \mathrm D^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}([GL_2(\mathbb Q_p)])
 \xrightarrow{\sim}
 \mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal X_p^{\mathrm{fam}}),
\end{equation}
compatible with the supplied family and Hecke data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A natural transformation of diagrams that is an equivalence at
every level is an equivalence in the functor category. Applying
the limit functor preserves equivalences. Compose
\(\operatorname{Tot}\Phi_\bullet\) with the two specified descent
model equivalences to obtain the displayed functor. Its
compatibilities follow from those of the diagram. The proof uses
actual overlap data; pointwise equivalences do not replace them.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Fixed characters and families]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:fixed-vs-varying}
Fixed-character block exhaustion is only one input to the family
problem. The coefficient extensions, conservative cover, overlap
comparisons and descent models remain the construction required
by Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family}.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Categorical Hypotheses for Obstruction (c)}

\paragraph{Local representation-theoretic inputs.} The central-character-variation
argument uses Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum Vol.~I Ch.~9--10 (fibrations
of categories) plus DEG arXiv:2603.26887. The passage from fixed-character functors to a family requires
the effective descent and conservative-cover datum stated above.

\paragraph{Conventions.} The determinant-fibration
convention on the classical Emerton--Gee carrier follows
Emerton--Gee arXiv:1908.07185 \S 2.2; its wild-enhanced analogue
and compatibility are part of the chosen family datum. The sheaf-of-categories
convention follows Gaitsgory--Rozenblyum Vol.~I Ch.~10 Thm~10.3.2.1.

\paragraph{Ambient categories.} Sheaves of presentable
stable \(\infty\)-categories over \(\mathcal{T}^{\mathrm{cent}}_{p}\);
the totalisation equivalence is an equivalence in this ambient.

\paragraph{Hypotheses.} The effective descent models and compatible
equivalences over the conservative cover are not imported from the
fixed-character theorem. They form the separate hypothesis
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family}.  The
fibre-to-total passage is formal only after that hypothesis is in
place. 

\paragraph{Functoriality.} The twisting morphism
\(\chi'\to\chi\) is compatible with both sides only under
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family}.

\paragraph{Unproved comparisons.} The actual cover-level equivalences, including coefficient
extensions and higher overlaps, remain to be constructed as part
of the family datum. The totalization theorem is conditional on them.


The central-character family datum is a genuine residual.

\section{F1.a Reduction}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:reduction}

\subsection{Assembly of (a), (b), (c)}

\begin{theorem}[F1.a at all primes, conditional]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1a-conditional}
Assume:
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{(H1)}] Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:block-exhaustion}
at every \(p\geq 5\) and every central character \(\chi\).
\item[\textbf{(H2)}] Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement}
at \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and every \(\chi\).
\item[\textbf{(H3)}] Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:wild-block-functor}
at \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and every \(\chi\).
\item[\textbf{(H4)}] Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:BH-block-exhaustion}
at \(p\in\{2,3\}\) and every \(\chi\).
\item[\textbf{(H5)}] Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family}
at every \(p\).
\end{itemize}
Then for every prime \(p\) and every continuous central character
\(\chi\) there exists an equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:F1a-conditional}
\Phi^{\mathrm{LocCat}}_{p,\chi}\colon\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\bigl([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})]\bigr)_{\chi}
\;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\bigl(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild\text{-}enhanced},p,\chi}_{GL_{2}}\bigr),
\end{equation}
where the spectral side is the classical Emerton--Gee stack for
\(p\geq 5\) and its wild enhancement for \(p\in\{2,3\}\), and the
family over \(\chi\) assembles to the displayed F1.a equivalence
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:F1a-displayed}
\Phi^{\mathrm{LocCat}}_{p}\colon\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\bigl([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})]\bigr)
\;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\bigl(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild\text{-}enhanced},p}_{GL_{2}}\bigr).
\end{equation}
Local Hecke-functoriality at the place \(p\) is part of the local
categorical input; it is not inferred from full faithfulness alone.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Assemble Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:ess-surj-conditional} (valid
for \(p\geq 5\) under (H1)),
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:cor:wild-ess-surj} (valid for \(p\in\{2,3\}\)
under (H2), (H3), and (H4)), and
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:central-character-variation}
(assembles the family under (H5)).
Per-\(\chi\) equivalences (from (H1)+(H2)+(H3)+(H4)) combine with the
family decomposition to produce the total equivalence
(\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:F1a-displayed}).
\end{proof}

\section{Consequence for F1 and \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\)}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:global-check}

\subsection{Local F1.a Closed at Every Prime Suffices for Adelic F1}

\begin{theorem}[Adelic F1 from local comparison and descent]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1-adelic-check}
Conditional statement. Assume the conditional closure of F1.a at
every prime \(p\) of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1a-conditional} (i.e.\ assume (H1)--(H5)), together with the specified adelic
descent data. Then the F1 obstruction is reduced to the current
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) table of
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:cl:cor:F1-single-lemma}:
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:F1-under-F1a}
\mathrm{D}^{\mathrm{sph\text{-}fin}}\bigl([GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q})\backslash GL_{2}(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}})]\bigr)\;\simeq\;\mathrm{IndCoh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}\bigl(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{Sel}}_{\bar\rho,\det,\mathcal{L}}\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:cl:cor:F1-single-lemma}: F1 holds given
(F1.a) and \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\). Our
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1a-conditional} reduces (F1.a) under
(H1)--(H5), up to the wild-enhancement of the local conditions
at \(p\in\{2,3\}\). These wild conditions must be inserted into the
Selmer-derived stack as modified factors
\(\mathcal{X}^{\mathrm{EG,wild\text{-}enhanced},p}_{GL_{2}}\) in the
local condition \(\mathcal{L}_{p}\). The global equivalence
(\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:eq:F1-under-F1a}) therefore uses the
Selmer-derived target on the right-hand side; it is not an
ind-Noetherian product of local stacks.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[three local pieces]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:global-three-pieces}
Under the assumptions (H1)--(H5) of
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1a-conditional} plus
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\), F1 holds. The assumptions
(H1)--(H5) are the local-categorical-comparison residuals
of the DEG reduction; \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\) is the adelic-categorical-descent
residual of Remark~\ref{v4-arith:cl:rem:F1-substantial}. The F1 obstruction
set therefore has six items: (H1)--(H5) and
\(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\).
\end{remark}

\subsection{Refined Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:closure} Reduction}

\begin{proposition}[Local comparison inputs and adelic descent]
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:F1-reduction-updated}
The sufficient input set for the stated F1 comparison consists of
(H1)--(H5) from Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1a-conditional}
and the adelic descent datum \(\mathrm L_{\mathrm{ACD}}\).
Here (H3) constructs the wild block functor, (H4) requires its
block exhaustion, and (H5) supplies the central-character family
comparison. These are distinct requirements.
\[
 \mathrm{Inputs}(F1)=
 \{\mathrm{H1},\mathrm{H2},\mathrm{H3},\mathrm{H4},\mathrm{H5},
       \mathrm L_{\mathrm{ACD}}\}.
\]
This is a sufficient construction route, not a proof that each input
is logically necessary for every possible F1 equivalence.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Under (H1)--(H4), the specified block functors give the per-character
local comparisons in the stated coefficient categories. Hypothesis
(H5) supplies their compatible family as the character varies.
The adelic descent datum then assembles these local comparisons on
the specified Selmer target, as in
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1-adelic-check}.
Neither a family of unrelated equivalences nor essential
surjectivity without a constructed functor performs these steps.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Global Assembly and Its Hypotheses}

The global comparison uses the local categorical comparisons together
with the F1 reduction of Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:cl:cor:F1-single-lemma}.
Its inputs are (H1)--(H5) and \(\mathrm L_{\mathrm{ACD}}\).
The spectral geometry is formulated in ind-Noetherian formal algebraic
stacks over \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\), while the automorphic
geometry uses the adelic double coset. The comparison concerns their
associated presentable \(\infty\)-categories.

The global Emerton--Gee construction uses the ind-Noetherian limit
of Emerton--Gee, \S2.3. Incorporating the proposed wild enhancements
at the two small primes requires compatibility with that limit.
Assembly over primes is a restricted tensor product, and its
compatibility with cross-prime Hecke functoriality is part of
\(\mathrm L_{\mathrm{ACD}}\). Thus the global consequence retains the
local, wild-functor, family, and adelic descent hypotheses.

\section{Residual Conditions}

The DEG extension has five residual inputs:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:block-exhaustion}
(\textbf{R1a/F1.a.1}): the specified arithmetic comparison functor,
common coefficients and support, and actual lifts of spectral generators

\item Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement}
(\textbf{R1b/F1.a.2}): existence of a derived formal algebraic
wild-enhanced EG stack at \(p\in\{2,3\}\) with four structural
properties.
\item Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:wild-block-functor}
(\textbf{R1b'}): existence of the Bushnell--Henniart-compatible
per-block fully faithful functor at \(p\in\{2,3\}\).
\item Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:BH-block-exhaustion}

Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:block-exhaustion}; essential

\item Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family}
(\textbf{R1d}): effective descent of the specified comparison functors over the
central-character family.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What is Unconditional}

The established fixed-character theorem and the conditional assembly

\begin{enumerate}
\item The fixed-\(\chi\), \(p\geq 5\), fully faithful DEG embedding
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:DEG-family-ff}): no residual needed.

\subsection{Out of Domain}

Three items are outside the stated domain:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Langlands functoriality at the global level.} Deferred to
the adelic categorical descent \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\).
\item \emph{Higher \(GL_{n}\) for \(n\geq 3\).} The chapter focuses on
\(GL_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p})\); DEG's technology generalises under
significant additional input (Emerton--Helm--Paskunas for
\(GL_{n}\)), not in domain here.
\item \emph{Number fields beyond \(\mathbb{Q}\).} The chapter focuses
on \(\mathbb{Q}_{p}\); extension to \(K_{v}\) for \(K/\mathbb{Q}\) a
finite extension requires additional considerations at primes
ramified in \(K\).
\end{enumerate}

\section{DEG Extension Boundary}
\label{v4-arith:deg-f1a:summary}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Dotto--Emerton--Gee arXiv:2603.26887 supplies the stated
embedding on its integral coefficient and formal spectral carriers:
\emph{fully-faithful, fixed central character, \(p\geq 5\) only}
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:deg-fixed-chi}).
\item Three obstructions separate DEG from F1.a:
(a) essential surjectivity; (b) extension to \(p\in\{2,3\}\);
(c) central-character variation
(Remark~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:rem:deg-not-assert}).
\item Obstruction (a) reduces to
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:block-exhaustion} (F1.a.1): block
exhaustion per residual Galois representation.
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:ess-surj-conditional} closes (a) under
this conjecture.
\item Obstruction (b) reduces to
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:EG-wild-enhancement} (F1.a.2: existence
of wild-enhanced EG stack),
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:wild-block-functor}, plus
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:BH-block-exhaustion} (F1.a.3).
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:wild-functor-existence} and
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:cor:wild-ess-surj} close (b) under these
conjectures.
\item Obstruction (c) is the family datum
Conjecture~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:conj:central-character-family}.  It is not a
consequence of the fixed-character DEG theorem.
\item Full conditional closure of F1.a at all primes:
Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1a-conditional}, conditional on
(H1)--(H5).
\item Unconditional partial closure: the fixed-\(\chi\), \(p\geq 5\),
fully faithful DEG embedding
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:DEG-family-ff},
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:cor:partial-F1a-large-p}). No family
statement is asserted.
\item Global assembly check: F1 follows from F1.a (at every prime,
including wild) plus \(\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{ACD}}\)
(Theorem~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:thm:F1-adelic-check}); total F1 residual
table is six items
(Proposition~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:prop:F1-reduction-updated}).
\end{enumerate}

The fixed-character DEG functor has the proper image specified in
Corollary~\ref{v4-arith:deg-f1a:cor:partial-F1a-large-p}. The proposed
arithmetic equivalence requires different comparison data: the actual
coefficient and support maps, lifted generators, wild functors, family
descent and centre compatibility. The conditional assembly proves what
these maps would imply; their construction remains the arithmetic target.
