\chapter{Final Residual Obligations of the Arithmetic Branch}
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:chapter}

After the Koszul, chiral-homology, and arithmetic Chern--Simons
constructions have been pushed to their natural boundary, the
arithmetic branch's open proof obligations decompose into four classes:
one statement equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis, four conjectures
outside the internal construction, one sub-RH Ramanujan hypothesis,
and two small residuals of combined scope \(3\)--\(6\)~pp. The
original Vol~IV HZ-IV decorator campaign is orthogonal to these
obligations and is recorded in Chapter~\ref{v4-ch:realization-programme}.

\section{Condensed-Hilbert Descent and Archimedean Tate Positivity}
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:w10-a}

The quantum arithmetic Chern--Simons bulk construction
(\Cref{v4-arith:w10-a:conj:condensed-descent}) and the archimedean
Tate positivity statement (\Cref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity})
are the two ingredients whose conjunction implies the Riemann
Hypothesis. The theorem below separates the two obligations:
the first is a construction statement; the second is a positivity
statement, and only the second carries RH-equivalent content.

\begin{theorem}[condensed-Hilbert descent plus archimedean Tate positivity implies RH]
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:thm:cs-descent-atp-rh}
\ClaimStatusProvedHereConditional \emph{(on the Clausen--Scholze
condensed-Hilbert descent \Cref{v4-arith:w10-a:conj:condensed-descent}
and the archimedean Tate positivity statement
\Cref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity})}. The
conjunction of Clausen--Scholze condensed-Hilbert descent and
archimedean Tate positivity (A-TP) implies the Riemann Hypothesis.
The descent alone does not decide RH: after the descent is assumed,
A-TP is the only remaining RH-equivalent obstruction in the quantum
ACS construction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The implication is
\Cref{v4-arith:w10-a:thm:descent-plus-ATP-equals-RH}. The descent
constructs the condensed Hilbert space and identifies the
Wilson-surface action with the Arakelov scaling operator; it does
not assert positivity of the scaling quadratic form. That positivity
is A-TP, equivalent to RH by
\Cref{v4-arith:w9-r2:thm:purity-ATP-RH}. Hence the conjunction implies
RH, while the descent alone supplies the construction and not the
zero-line assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[construction versus positivity]
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:rem:w10-a-shape}
The condensed-Hilbert descent is the construction half of the quantum
ACS package; A-TP is the positivity half. RH is equivalent to A-TP
alone, given the descent.
\end{remark}

\section{The Unique RH-Equivalent Obstruction}
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:tier1}

The archimedean Tate positivity statement
(\Cref{v4-arith:w5-a:conj:archimedean-tate-positivity}) asserts:
\begin{equation}
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:eq:atp}
W(f \ast \widetilde{f}) \geq 0 \quad \text{for all }
f \in \mathrm{PW}(\mathbb{R}),
\end{equation}
where \(\mathrm{PW}(\mathbb{R})\) denotes the Paley--Wiener space of
entire functions of exponential type and \(W\) is the Weil explicit
functional on the completed zeta function. This statement is
equivalent to RH by \Cref{v4-arith:w9-r2:thm:purity-ATP-RH}. It is
denoted A-TP throughout.

\begin{proposition}[A-TP is the unique RH-equivalent obstruction]
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:prop:tier1}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. The arithmetic branch has exactly one
remaining RH-equivalent obstruction, namely A-TP
\eqref{v4-arith:w10-final:eq:atp}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Chapters~\ref{v4-arith:w6-a:ATP-direct-attack}--\ref{v4-arith:w7-j:ATP-adelic-scattering}
and Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w8-atp:chapter} identify the same residual
through the direct, Nyman--Beurling, de Branges, Gaussian,
Mellin--Barnes, Hadamard, Gram, Guinand--Weil, adelic-scattering,
Arthur, geometric-Langlands, Iwasawa, quantum-chaos, and Arakelov
approaches. Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w9-r2:archimedean-wilson-surface}
identifies this residual with RH. No subsequent construction removes
it.
\end{proof}

\section{The Generalised Ramanujan Conjecture for
\texorpdfstring{\(\mathrm{GL}_{4}\)}{GL\_4}}
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:sub-rh}

The Rankin--Selberg square of a Maass form on \(\mathrm{GL}_{2}/\mathbb{Q}\)
is an automorphic \(L\)-function on \(\mathrm{GL}_{4}/\mathbb{Q}\).
Full temperedness of that \(\mathrm{GL}_{4}\) representation at every
finite place (the Generalised Ramanujan Conjecture in this family)
closes the Maass sector of the \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\)
Hochschild-trace identity. This conjecture is independent of A-TP:
it concerns Satake eigenvalues, not zero placement.

\begin{hypothesis}[GRC for \(\mathrm{GL}_{4}/\mathbb{Q}\) in the Selberg class]
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:hyp:GRC-GL4}
\ClaimStatusConditional. Every cuspidal automorphic representation of
\(\mathrm{GL}_{4}/\mathbb{Q}\) in the Selberg-class envelope relevant to
the Rankin--Selberg square of a Maass form is tempered at every finite
place.
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{proposition}[GRC-\(\mathrm{GL}_{4}\) closes the Maass sector]
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:prop:GRC-GL4}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. Conditional on
\Cref{v4-arith:w10-final:hyp:GRC-GL4}, full Ramanujan holds for the
Maass forms entering the \(\mathcal{V}^{\mathrm{prim}}\)
Hochschild-trace \(P_{3}\) identity of \Cref{v4-arith:w10-c:thm:main}.
This closes the Maass sector. The hypothesis is not equivalent to RH;
it occupies a different Selberg-class axis from A-TP.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Chapter~\ref{v4-arith:w10-c:chapter} proves that the Arthur--Ramakrishnan
sub-RH hypothesis implies full Ramanujan for Maass forms
(\Cref{v4-arith:w10-c:thm:AR-implies-Ramanujan}) and reduces that
hypothesis to \Cref{v4-arith:w10-final:hyp:GRC-GL4}
(\Cref{v4-arith:w10-c:prop:sub-RH-strict}). The \(P_{3}\) identity
is the exact conditional statement of \Cref{v4-arith:w10-c:thm:main}.
A-TP concerns the zeros of \(\zeta\) through Weil positivity;
GRC-\(\mathrm{GL}_{4}\) concerns Satake parameters; the two residuals
are independent.
\end{proof}

\label{v4-arith:w10-final:tier3}

Two residuals remain in the \(F_{1}\text{.a.1}\) sector. Both have

\(3\)--\(6\)~pp.

\label{v4-arith:w10-final:prop:small-residuals}

remaining small residuals:

\item \textbf{Supersingular small-weight tail}
(\Cref{v4-arith:w10-f:conj:ss-small-weight}): Fontaine--Laffaille
representations with Hodge--Tate weights

\item \textbf{\(A_{5}\) non-solvable descent defect}
(\Cref{v4-arith:w10-f:conj:ns-defect}): local-global descent defect
in the icosahedral sector, scope \(1\)--\(2\)~pp.

\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The complete list of \(F_{1}\text{.a.1}\) residuals is
\Cref{v4-arith:w10-f:prop:F1a1-ledger-w10f}. The Fontaine--Laffaille
supersingular core and the odd-determinant icosahedral core close
unconditionally by \Cref{v4-arith:w10-f:thm:f1a1-ss-FL} and
\Cref{v4-arith:w10-f:thm:A5-odd-closure}. The two tails listed
above are what remains.

\section{Near-Closure of the Arithmetic Branch}
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:assessment}

\begin{theorem}[arithmetic branch near-closure]
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:thm:near-closure}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere. The arithmetic branch's open proof obligations
reduce to exactly:
\begin{enumerate}
\item one statement equivalent to RH: archimedean Tate positivity (A-TP);
\item four conjectures outside the branch's internal constructions:
Wild-EG enhancement, \(\Pi\)-flat pro-\'etale flatness, arithmetic
Arinkin--Gaitsgory singular support, and Clausen--Scholze
condensed-Hilbert descent;
\item one sub-RH hypothesis independent of A-TP: GRC for
\(\mathrm{GL}_{4}/\mathbb{Q}\);
\item two small residuals of combined scope \(3\)--\(6\)~pp and density
zero.
\end{enumerate}
The Vol~IV HZ-IV decorator campaign for Vols I--III is a separate
obligation, orthogonal to the arithmetic branch.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Item (1) is \Cref{v4-arith:w10-final:prop:tier1}. Item (2) is
\Cref{v4-arith:w10-final:thm:four-upstream}. Item (3) is
\Cref{v4-arith:w10-final:prop:GRC-GL4}. Item (4) is
\Cref{v4-arith:w10-final:prop:small-residuals}. The separation from
the realization programme is \Cref{v4-arith:w10-final:prop:hziv-separate}.
No residual outside these four classes is left by
Chapters~\ref{v4-arith:w5-synth:chapter}--\ref{v4-arith:w10-d:chapter}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[scope of near-closure]
\label{v4-arith:w10-final:rem:not-rh}
The theorem isolates RH-equivalent content into A-TP and places the
remaining large obstructions in four named conjectures. It does not
prove RH. Any proof of RH through the arithmetic branch still requires
A-TP or an equivalent positivity statement.
\end{remark}
